# Thompson V: checked literature correction

Primary source: Collin Bleak, Luna Elliott and James Hyde,
*Sufficient conditions for a group of homeomorphisms of the Cantor
set to be two-generated*, JIMJ 23 (2024), 2825–2858.
[Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748024000045).
Bibliography key: `CpathsBleakElliottHyde2024`.

I opened the complete publisher PDF and read Lemma 2.1
(printed p. 2833), Lemma 3.1 (p. 2834), and Theorem 3.5
(p. 2835), including their proofs.

Lemma 2.1 constructs the required homeomorphism of clopen
sets using three batches of prefix transpositions. Lemma 3.1
supplies an element whose nonzero powers move one clopen
set into another. In Theorem 3.5, choose disjoint domain
and image clopens for the finitely many nonidentity
constants. Homeomorphisms with disjoint supports then
route a starting clopen successively through the mixed
word, ending disjointly from its start. This is an actual
nonidentity evaluation. Pure constant and pure power
words are immediate separately.

Thus Thompson's `V=V_2` is mixed-identity-free. The earlier
informal suggestion of `V` as a simple group satisfying
a mixed identity was incorrect. No numbered result used
that suggestion. Quotients isomorphic to `V` are included
in A.28's first exclusion class.

primary PDF was read through the browser tool.
