# Uncountable locally free groups: exact scope of the current search

## The remaining construction requirement

The sought source has to be nontrivial, perfect and locally free, have an actual two-dimensional classifying CW complex, and have no nontrivial countable homomorphic image. No example with all these properties has been obtained in this investigation. No residual-countability theorem for arbitrary locally free groups was found in the checked sources.

## Almost-free filtrations do not supply a perfect source

Every nontrivial perfect group P contains a nontrivial countable perfect subgroup. Fix g != 1. Start with the countable subgroup generated by g. Given a countable subgroup H_n, choose for every h in H_n one finite expression of h as a product of commutators of elements of P, and let H_(n+1) be generated by H_n and all entries of those expressions. It is countable. The union H contains g, is countable, and every h in H belongs to [H,H], because its chosen witnesses occur in the next stage. Hence H is perfect and nontrivial.

Therefore a group in which every countable subgroup is free cannot be perfect and nontrivial. The exact obstruction is the constructed inclusion H -> P together with the nonzero abelianization of every nontrivial free group. It applies to aleph_1-free groups in the nonabelian sense, even when those groups are uncountable and not free themselves. It does not apply to groups required only to have free finitely generated subgroups.

## The dimensional proof obligation

Warren Dicks, Peter H. Kropholler, Ian J. Leary and Simon Thomas, *Classifying spaces for proper actions of locally finite groups*, author addendum of 30 May 2002, <https://mat.uab.cat/~dicks/DKLT.html>, was opened in full. It states that arbitrary locally free groups satisfy a cardinal-dependent geometric-dimension bound, and explicitly records as open there the assertion that every locally free group has geometric dimension at most two. This source does not establish that the question is still open in November 2026; it proves that an unqualified deduction of dimension two from local freeness cannot be read out of that argument.

Berrick, *The acyclic group dichotomy*, Lemma 2.1, uses the unqualified wording that perfect locally free groups are acyclic of geometric dimension two, immediately after its countable grope construction. The actual constructions used in the present manuscript have separately verified two-dimensional CW models (countable graph telescopes and wedges). No dimension bound for an arbitrary uncountable locally free group is being imported from that sentence.

## The same-cardinality free subgroup theorem

Tsunekazu Nishinaka, *Uncountable locally free groups and their group rings*, official primary PDF <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1601.00295>, five pages, was read in full. Theorem 1.1 proves that, for every finitely generated subgroup A of an uncountable locally free group G, there is H of cardinality |G| such that the homomorphism A*H -> G induced by the subgroup inclusions is injective. In particular G contains a free subgroup of the same cardinality.

The proof supplies these subgroup embeddings. It supplies no retraction G -> H, and no homomorphism from G onto a countable group. Therefore it has not settled the detecting-quotient requirement. Its finite-rank lemma also proves that a locally free group of bounded local free rank is countable; this excludes an uncountable example built as an increasing union of free groups of one fixed finite rank.

Published metadata: J. Group Theory 21 (2018), 101–105, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1515/jgth-2017-0031>.

## A 2026 normal-generation example and its exact map

Sam P. Fisher and Yash Lodha, *A note on normal generation and the first l2-Betti number*, arXiv:2608.25988v1, official PDF <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.25988>, was downloaded and all eight pages were read. Local copies are D_FisherLodha2026.pdf and D_FisherLodha2026.txt. The paper constructs a countable locally free group of normal rank one and prescribed positive first l2-Betti number as the telescope

F(a_m,s_(m,1),...,s_(m,n)) -> F(a_(m+1),s_(m+1,1),...,s_(m+1,n)),

a_m -> a_(m+1), s_(m,i) -> s_(m+1,i)^(-1) a_(m+1) s_(m+1,i).

For every m the homomorphism from that stage to Z sending every displayed generator to 1 commutes with the transition. The induced map from the colimit onto Z is consequently an explicit nontrivial countable quotient. On abelianizations each transition sends every basis vector to the a-vector, so the colimit abelianization is exactly Z. These examples are therefore consistent with A.22 and do not supply a perfect source.

## Two proved restrictions on nearby constructions

The authored note D_Higman_locally_free.md proves, with the exact length and recurrence, that every homomorphism from a Higman-complete group to a perfect locally free group is trivial. This excludes a perfect locally free target with Higman completeness, but does not exclude arbitrary countable target groups.

The independently audited note Cpaths_Atelescope_retracts.md gives exact countable detecting maps for specified graph-telescope constructions, including Brunnian basis images and common-word/private-support patterns. Those statements have explicit hypotheses on the transition words. They are not being promoted to all graph telescopes or all locally free groups.
