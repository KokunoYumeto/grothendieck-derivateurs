# Results proved while writing the companion that we did not find in the literature

Some statements had to be proved along the way, while checking Grothendieck's text and the returns of the research
prompts, because we could not find them in the sources we searched. They are collected here so that a reader can check
them, find the literature we missed, and see where they bear on the manuscript. Our search was limited, so some of
them may well be known; whoever knows a reference is asked to tell us, and the entry will cite it. No priority is
claimed. Each entry gives the statement, where the proof is, who proved it (model and effort setting), whether anyone
has checked it yet, and the pages of the manuscript it bears on. Statements that turned out to be known are listed at
the end with the reference.
