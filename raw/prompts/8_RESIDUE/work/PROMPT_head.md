# RESIDUE: what seven packages on *Les Dérivateurs* left undecided

Package 8 on Alexander Grothendieck, *Les Dérivateurs* (1990–91). File prefix for your deliverables: `RESIDUE`.

## What is to be settled

Seven packages of this series have been answered (CATHOT, TOTASPH, POLES, GALOIS, STRATA, PATHS, BISITE; AI-produced by GPT-6 Astra, ChatGPT Pro mode; 658 numbered statements, 29 of them left Open). Their papers, registers and reviews are attached, and their statements are cited below by package and number ("BISITE B.9"). The reviews (by Claude, Anthropic; attached as `reviews/`) found no statement labelled Proved, Known or Refuted false, and each lists what it did not verify. This package takes the questions of Grothendieck's text that the seven left undecided, the ones that became sharper when the seven were read together, and the statements of the last package that matter most and that no reviewer has checked. It is a list of problems to be cracked, not a survey: each Part names an exact statement, and "done" means a theorem with a complete proof or a counterexample given completely and checked by a program.

The questions in his words and where they stand:

- VI ms. 186 (audit row VIc-C022; the row is called VIc-C008 in the older files): can every locally constant relative homotopy type over a small category be represented by a functor that is both a fibration and a cofibration in the sense of categories ("bifibration")? Yes over posets, over bases with only monomorphisms or only epimorphisms, and for 1-truncated values (STRATA E.10). Undecided over the five-element monoid `M = {1, e_00, e_01, e_10, e_11}`, `e_ij e_kl = e_il`, whose classifying space is `S²` (STRATA E.11 = PATHS E.11 = BISITE B.21).
- XVI ms. 1–3: his Définition 1.1 of a "localiseur fondamental" is Loc (1) (isomorphisms; two-out-of-three; `gf, fg ∈ W ⇒ f, g ∈ W`), Loc (2) (terminal object), Loc (3) (absolute Theorem A); the relative form is Loc (3 bis), of which he writes that it "m'apparaît comme le plus important des deux". BISITE A.32 gives a class satisfying Loc (1), (2), (3) and not (3 bis); it is not closed under retracts. With Loc (1 bis) or Loc (1 ter) the question is open, and so is his Rappel 1 (`W = W°`) for the literal axioms.
- XII ms. 217–218 (row XIIc B062): "J'ignore si les axiomes a), b) ci-dessus (axiome de la fibre homotopique) impliquent déjà la variante de (a)", his (c) for every full subcategory between `Fib_W S` and `M/S`. BISITE E.12: no, by a six-element lattice in which one map has no factorization through an intrinsic fibration. He continues: "je présume que pratiquement, quand on pourra vérifier (a), on pourra vérifier (c) également", with a footnote to ms. 240 ff., where every map factors as a `W`-map followed by a `W`-fibration. Under that hypothesis the implication is undecided.
- XII ms. 225–226 (row XIIc-a C033): `Liss ∩ Prop ⊆ Fib_W` "(peut-être)". BISITE B.9: exactly for `W∞` and for the class of all functors. What `Fib_W` is for the other localizers he uses (the truncations `W_n`; the coefficient localizers `W_D^{lc}` of I ms. 99–100) is not determined.
- VII ms. 65–67: his categories of paths `Ch_Σ(M; a, b)`, with arbitrary vertical arrows, "calculent" the maps from `a` to `b` in the localization. PATHS proves that the comparison `J` from the hammock localization is split injective in `Hot` and bijective on components (B.6), an equivalence for `Σ ⊂ Iso` and for `Σ` everything (B.4), and leaves the general case open (B.8).
- XII ms. 88 (row II-C-003): total asphericity. False as "equals filtered" (TOTASPH C.7–C.10). Whether total `W∞`-asphericity forces exact colimits of abelian groups (D.7), whether total `W_Z`-asphericity of a presheaf forces total `W∞`-asphericity (F.27), and which shapes compute colimits in `Cat` correctly (PATHS A.8) are open.
- XII ms. 225: for topological spaces, "deux versions complémentaires, ou 'duales' l'une de l'autre, d'une notion de type d'homotopie relatif". BISITE D.2 proves: over a finite Alexandrov base, spaces over the base modulo the maps that are weak equivalences over every open set are the cosheaves of spaces. For the real line it is open (D.12).
- XVI Théorème 5.4 and Loc (6 b) (POLES): which localizers are reversible, and are they classified by subcategories of spectra (POLES F.14). I ms. 99–100 (GALOIS): is `W_D^{lc}` the localizer `W_{D′}` of a derivator `D′` made functorially from `D` (GALOIS E.11, G.6).
- VI ms. 83 with the claimed model structures on strict `n`-categories: the two fine theories for strict 2-categories (BISITE C.10, F.18).

Not verified by any reviewer, and to be proved again here by a different route: BISITE D.11 (the direct image along the realization of a proper map of finite posets is the categorical right Kan extension), F.10 (a correspondence formalism on the smooth maps of `Cat`), F.15–F.17 (forgetting a marked point of `M̄_{0,n}`), and the table of B.15.

## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

The three leads below are inferences made by the reviewer (Claude, Anthropic) when the seven returns were read together. Nobody has checked them. They are written out in `reviews/7_BISITE/COMBINED_REVIEW.md`, section 3. If a lead is wrong, say "false" and show the step.

- **Lead 1 (boundary maps decide `Fib_{W_n}`).** Let `p: E → B` be a functor of small categories whose strict pullbacks are homotopy pullbacks (for instance `W∞`-smooth and proper; BISITE B.7), with strict fibres `F_b`. For `n ≥ 0`, `p ∈ Fib_{W_n}` (pullback along `p` and along each of its base changes preserves `n`-equivalences) if and only if for every object `b` and every object `x` of `F_b` the boundary map `π_{n+1}(BB, b) → π_n(BF_b, x)` of the fibration sequence is zero; for `n = 0` read: `π_1(BB, b)` acts trivially on `π_0(BF_b)`. Argument: by BISITE B.17 (Cisinski 2006, Proposition 6.2.3) membership means that `τ_{≤n}(E ×_B X) → τ_{≤n}E ×_{τ_{≤n}B} τ_{≤n}X` is an equivalence for every `X → B`; over `τ_{≤n}X` the two fibres agree below degree `n` and are `π_n(F)/∂π_{n+1}(X)` and `π_n(F)/∂π_{n+1}(B)` in degree `n`; `X = e` gives necessity. The lead accounts for BISITE B.10, B.12, B.13 and GALOIS D.4.
- **Lead 2 (no bifibration with invertible transitions over `M`).** Let `p: E → BM` be a Grothendieck fibration and opfibration over the one-object category of the five-element monoid `M`, with fibre `C` and cocartesian transition functors `T_m: C → C`. If every `T_m` is an equivalence of categories, then `BE` is not contractible unless `BC` is a 1-type; in particular `p` does not represent the local system of path spaces, whose fibre is `ΩS²`. Argument: `T_e ≅ T_e T_e` and invertibility give `T_e ≅ 1` for every idempotent `e`; the pseudo-action is then equivalent to the identity functors with coherence given by a normalized 2-cocycle `c: M × M → A`, `A = Aut(1_C)` (an abelian group); `E` is isomorphic to the category with the objects of `C` and arrows `(m, f)`, composed by `(n, g)(m, f) = (nm, g f c(m, n))`; the functor `C × M_c → E`, `(f, (m, α)) ↦ (m, fα)`, where `M_c` is the central extension of `M` by `A` with cocycle `c`, is the quotient by a free action of the one-object groupoid of `A`, so `BE` is the Borel quotient of `BC × BM_c` by `BA`; if `BE` is contractible then `BC × BM_c ≃ K(A, 1)`, so `π_k(BC) = 0` for `k ≥ 2`. What the lead leaves: transition functors that are idempotent up to isomorphism, have right adjoints, are weak equivalences and are not equivalences.
- **Lead 3 (B062 holds with enough intrinsic fibrations).** Let `M` have pullbacks, let `W` contain the isomorphisms and satisfy two-out-of-three, and suppose every map factors as a map in `W` followed by a map in `Fib_W`, with no functoriality. Then for every `S` and every full subcategory `Fib_W S ⊆ 𝓗 ⊆ M/S` both inclusions induce equivalences of the localizations at `W_S`. Argument proposed: (i) every map `B → A` in `𝓗` with `A` in `Fib_W S` factors as `B → A′ → A` with `B → A′` in `W_S` and `A′` in `Fib_W S`, hence in `𝓗`; (ii) two replacements `B → A′`, `B → A″` are dominated by a third (factor `B → A′ ×_S A″`); (iii) an approximation theorem for localizations (in the style of Cisinski's catégories dérivables or of Kahn–Maltsiniotis) then gives the equivalence. Step (iii) is not checked: the theorems known to the reviewer ask for structure on the target that `𝓗` does not have.
- **Lead 4 (from BISITE, unverified by the reviewers).** For a `W∞`-proper map `f: P → Q` of finite posets, the direct image along `|Nf|` preserves the sheaves constructible for the least-vertex stratification and is the categorical right Kan extension (BISITE D.11; its proof is an induction over chains with a homotopy-initiality claim for a minimum functor on cell posets).
- **Lead 5 (from BISITE, unverified).** Forgetting a marked point is a `W∞`-proper functor between the entrance-path categories of `M̄_{0,n+1}` and `M̄_{0,n}` for every `n ≥ 3`, is not smooth for `n = 4`, where one lifting category is a `K(Z, 1)`, and its right Kan extension is the geometric direct image on constructible coefficients (BISITE F.15–F.17).
