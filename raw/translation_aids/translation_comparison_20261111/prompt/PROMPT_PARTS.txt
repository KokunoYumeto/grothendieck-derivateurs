@@ LM WORK
our diplomatic transcription of his manuscript La Longue Marche à travers la théorie de Galois (1981), his research notes: what he wrote, as he wrote it, with his deletions, insertions, marginal notes, underlining and abbreviations, and the transcriber's marks of doubt.

@@ LM STYLE
- These are notes written for himself: keep their sketchy, exploratory tone, the dashes, exclamation marks and unfinished sentences.
- His abbreviations are written out in English (the terms below give those on these pages; write out any other the same way). "ssi" is "iff".

@@ LM TEXT_MACROS
\deleted, \inserted, \margin, \unsure, \uline, \text

@@ LM ANCHORS
\scanpage{n}, the scan; \mspage{n}, his page number

@@ LM APPARATUS
The marks of the transcription
- \deleted{...} is what he struck out, \inserted{...} what he added between the lines, \margin{...} a note in his margin. Translate their content, the deletions too, and keep each mark where it stands, also where it cuts a sentence; marks inside marks stay inside.
- \unsure{...} is a reading the transcriber is not sure of: translate it and keep the mark around the English. \unsure{A|B} gives two possible readings: translate each reading that is a word, in the same order, keeping the bar; readings that are symbols or formulas are copied.
- \illegible{n} (n illegible words) is copied. \uline{...} is his underlining: keep it, with the words inside translated.

@@ DER WORK
our transcription of Les Dérivateurs, chapter XVIII, in the edition of M. Künzer, J. Malgoire and G. Maltsiniotis (Universités de Montpellier II et Paris VII).

@@ DER STYLE
- The edition's own wording (headings, the editors' additions) is translated in the same way.

@@ DER TEXT_MACROS
\section*, \subsection*, \textit, \textbf, \footnote, \texttt, \text

@@ DER ANCHORS
\printedpage{n}, the edition's page; \msp{page n}, the manuscript page; \newpage

@@ DER APPARATUS
The marks of the edition
- \texttt{[...]} is an addition or a doubt of the editors, in typewriter type: translate the words inside and keep the brackets.
- The edition's macros (\cM, \uHom, \Ens, \defeq, ...) are kept as they are; the English edition defines how they print.
- Quotation marks “...” are written ``...''.
