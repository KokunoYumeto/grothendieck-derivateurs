## Conclusion

No version of either work has a major error: every sentence is translated, the formulas are intact (except Centre respelled in LM_D), and the hedges ("sans doute", "je présume", "probablement") are kept everywhere. The differences between the top versions (LM: F, H, then A, C, E, G; DER: A, D, E, then B, H) are single word choices and would not matter to a reader of an edition; the one point worth settling in the glossary is "action effective" -> "actual action", which only F and H have. Only LM_D (marks dropped or moved, untranslated \text and abbreviations) and DER_G (French left in six labels, a dropped "d'ailleurs") would need a repair pass, and DER_F is clumsier than the rest.
