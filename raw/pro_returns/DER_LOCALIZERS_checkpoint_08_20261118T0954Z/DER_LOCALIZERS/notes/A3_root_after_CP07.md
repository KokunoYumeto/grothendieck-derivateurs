## 3. Other unsuccessful reductions

The first nonzero homology group of A gives primary Eilenberg–Mac Lane spaces killed by A, as in A34.6. Consequently an A-null witness is also null for those spaces. This does not place its suspension spectrum in the smaller ideal generated by one such Eilenberg–Mac Lane space: that ideal is contained in L_A, and the needed membership implication goes in the other direction. No reduction of the entire existence question to the single previously studied ideal L_p follows from this observation.

We also considered taking a stable cellular approximation of Sigma-infinity Z in the smaller ideal. Such an approximation need not be nonzero, need not be a suspension spectrum, and need not supply a map of spaces preserving the required nullity. None of those three missing steps was established.

## 5. Why stable cellular constructions do not yet produce the space witness

The stable ideal is closed under spectral cofibers and arbitrary homotopy colimits. Realizing an arbitrary map between infinite suspension spectra by an actual map of spaces after one fixed finite suspension is a separate requirement. The finite-cell stabilization argument gives a bound for a finite source; it does not give a uniform bound for the infinite generator. Thus a spectral cellular expression for an object in L_A is not automatically an unstable construction in the reversible class. Conversely, taking an infinite-loop space of the constructed spectrum does not provide a null H-space witness outside the existing reversible class. The finite-loop normalization A34.6 does not allow passage to a coherent infinite-loop witness.

The related finite-detection proposal would require maps K→Z→K for some finite primary K with a nonnilpotent composite, or another actual construction placing such a K in the reversible class. A stable map out of Z to a finite target alone does not supply the reverse map or such a composite. The elementary finite-factorization criterion is recorded separately by the A.4 agent; its missing hypothesis is not inferred from nonzero cohomotopy.
