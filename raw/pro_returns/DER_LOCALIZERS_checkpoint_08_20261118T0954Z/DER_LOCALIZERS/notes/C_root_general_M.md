# Root investigation of the unrestricted linear theorem

Status: ongoing; no full counterexample or proof is claimed. Started after checkpoint 03, 18 November 2026.

## Exact products and coproducts do not replace the missing colimit argument

Neeman's 2002 paper, *A counterexample to a 1961 theorem in homological algebra*, was opened at the author's IAS PDF. Sections 1–3, pp.397–413, were read, including Proposition 2.9, Corollary 3.8 and Lemmas 3.10–3.11. Its category of additive functors preserving countable coproducts as products is abelian, has enough projectives, and in the specified example satisfies AB4 and AB4*. Nevertheless a nonzero sequence of monomorphisms has zero colimit. This invalidates a proposed replacement of C.13's filtered-colimit argument by exact products and coproducts alone. It is not a counterexample with the manuscript's monoidal and interval data. The published construction uses normed groups and contractions; it is not the algebraic construction below.

An attempted different construction takes the additive category generated by k=Z_(p), Z_p, their algebraic tensor powers and countable coproducts, then imposes preservation of those coproducts on its contravariant module functors. A Day-type tensor would need to descend through that condition. The represented k would be a projective unit, so its arbitrary copowers would also be projective; this model cannot realize the free-coefficient normalization obstruction described below. More seriously, the map represented by k→Z_p is not yet proved monic after tensoring with every functor, and its tensor images have not been proved derived-phi-null. Algebraic faithful flatness does not settle this: the category includes large test objects, and maps from them to Z_p⊗a need not factor through finite free stages. Using completed tensor instead would introduce an idempotent interval, whose universal monicity and right exactness force its cokernel to vanish. Thus neither version is a complete example. No result-register row changes status from this investigation.

## A normalization obstruction to test

The exact cocontinuous resolving functor of C.15 makes the canonical counit extend from the unit to every free coefficient. Outside AB4 and without such a functor, an explicit possible obstruction is an exact copower embedding `kappa:Mod(k)->M`, with left adjoint `phi`, for which `Lphi(kappa(k))=k` but `Lphi(kappa(k^(I)))` has positive homology for some infinite set I. Merely pointing to a failure of coproduct exactness is not enough: C.18 has such a failure in a summand invisible to phi, and still satisfies the theorem. A candidate must also supply the actual monoidal unit, symmetry/associativity, copower adjunction, and the interval for every object.

Replacing M by nonnegative complexes in a candidate abelian monoidal category would supply the usual contractible disk interval, provided its monoidal and normalization hypotheses hold. At the time of this initial attempt no such base category had been obtained. The subsequent C.24 construction supplies the scalar adjoints and the failed counit, but still lacks a compatible symmetric monoidal structure. Thus this remains a construction plan for the full manuscript question.

### Subsequent resolution of the Day-tensor monicity question

The preceding universal-monicity gap is now an actual failure for this candidate, conditional on the proposed cocontinuous Day tensor descending at all. The linear-case agent supplied the argument and root independently re-proved it: put C=Z_p/Z_(p), a nonzero rational vector space. ## Contramodule normalization test (after checkpoint 04)

This is an exclusion of one proposed source of counterexamples, not a proof of the unrestricted manuscript theorem. Let C be a coaugmented coalgebra over a field k, with coaugmentation g:k→C. Write A=C-Contra, U:A→Vect(k) for the exact forgetful functor, and T(V)=Hom_k(C,V) for its free left adjoint. The free contramodule T(V) is projective: Hom_A(T(V),−)=Hom_k(V,U−) is exact. These definitions and exactness are the ones checked in the opened Positselski source above.

The proposed unit is the usual trivial contramodule k. Its actual scalar copower κ(V)=V⊗_k k in A must not be identified at the outset with the usual trivial contramodule on the underlying vector space V. Infinite contramodule summation data can obstruct that identification.

Assume precisely manuscript hypothesis (a) for this actual copower: it has a k-linear left adjoint φ. Then φT is left adjoint to Uκ. Every cocontinuous k-linear endofunctor of Vect(k) has the natural form V↦V⊗_k W, with W its value at k: the canonical comparison is an isomorphism first on free vector spaces and hence on every vector space. Its right adjoint is Hom_k(W,−). Since Uκ(k)=k, we have Hom_k(W,k)≅k. Faithfulness of algebraic duality and elementary linear algebra force dim W=1. It follows that Uκ is naturally isomorphic to the identity functor.

There is a canonical natural morphism θ:κ→κ_triv, induced by all linear maps k→V into the usual trivial contramodule. Its component at k is the identity of the proposed unit. Both underlying vector-space functors are naturally isomorphic to the identity. A k-linear natural endomorphism of the identity on vector spaces is multiplication by its value at k; thus Uθ is invertible everywhere. The forgetful functor reflects isomorphisms, so θ is an isomorphism. In particular, under (a), the actual scalar copower is the usual trivial contramodule functor. Its left adjoint is therefore the contratensor functor k⊙_C−, by the defining coequalizer of the contraaction and evaluation at g.

For P∈A, the augmented monadic bar resolution has terms

B_n(P)=T(UT)^n(UP),  n≥0.

Each term is free and projective. After applying U, its augmented simplicial vector space has the standard extra degeneracy given by the monad unit, so its associated chain complex is a resolution. For P=κ(V), applying φ gives the complex with degree-n term Hom_k(C^{⊗ n},V), with C^{⊗0}=k. Its differential is the dual of the coaugmented cobar differential on D^n=C^{⊗ n}: the two endpoint maps insert g and the inner maps apply the coalgebra comultiplication, with alternating signs. The endpoint signs make D^0→D^1 zero. Equivalently one may normalize to the reduced cobar complex. This calculation follows directly by applying the free-forgetful counit at each bar face and the trivial contraaction at the last face.

Because Hom_k(−,V) is exact, the homology in degree n is canonically Hom_k(H^nD,V). If the canonical derived counit Lφ(κ k)→k is a quasi-isomorphism, the faithfulness of Hom_k(−,k) implies H^nD=0 for n>0 and H^0D=k, with its specified augmentation. The same calculation then gives the canonical quasi-isomorphism Lφ(κ V)→V for every vector space V, including arbitrary infinite-dimensional ones. Thus this coalgebra contramodule construction cannot produce the proposed failure of normalization only on an infinite free coefficient while satisfying the actual copower adjunction (a).

No internal symmetric tensor product or interval is supplied by this argument. Nor is the above bar functor asserted to be cocontinuous: Hom_k(C,−) generally does not preserve coproducts. Consequently this is not an application of C.15 and is not a proof of C.general_M. It rules out one particular normalization obstruction in one class, without changing the current main gate.

The elementary finite-arrow alternatives also require care. For the pullback-product tensor on two-term arrows over a field, the finite projective objects satisfy P0⊗P0=P0 and P0⊗P1=P1⊗P1=0. This operation is not right exact: applying −⊗P1 to the exact sequence P1→P0→1→0 would otherwise make 1⊗P1 vanish. Consequently it cannot be extended by a cocontinuous formula on the projective generators. This observation does not rule out a merely bilinear tensor, since cocontinuity is not one of the raw assumptions. It only prevents quietly using the usual projective-presentation construction for that particular finite tensor.
