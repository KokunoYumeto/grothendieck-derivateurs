# A34.6 universal reduction: proof and scope

The full theorem is in `sections/A34_reduction.tex`. It is a reduction of the existential A.3 problem, which remains open. Given any hypothetical witness, A34.5 first reduces to one prime. The union over small families of actual aspherical CW spaces then produces a set of generators supporting its one stable witness. Their triple-suspended wedge U and its infinite-loop replacement A=QU remain aspherical. Snaith gives exactly the same generated stable ideal. The one-generator reversible closure is accessible by the rechecked bounded-subcomplex construction.

The first nonzero integral homology group G of A in degree n controls the precise ordinary-nullification suspension bound: d=n−1 if G/pG is nonzero, and d=n if it vanishes. The additional degree in the divisible case is required by Bousfield1997 Lemma8.13. On d-connected primary H-spaces, repeated use of Bousfield1996 KeyLemma1.6 removes all d suspensions. Applying this to the loop-suspension replacement of the assumed witness and then A-nullifying yields the exact criterion: a noncontractible d-connected primary A-null H-space Z whose suspension spectrum belongs to loc(Σ∞A).

Every smallness statement is relative to fixed Grothendieck universes. The source set is chosen as actual aspherical CW models from the start. The proof claims neither a countable witness nor a uniform accessibility rank. Single generation refers to reversible closure, not necessarily to fundamental generation by one collapse map. The ordinary nullification and its cone construction are used at the space level, and the simplex category gives a concrete conditional categorical witness.

The small-subfamily union argument is already in supplied REVLOC D.3.8, and accessibility of small reversible closure is already B.4; both were reread with their surrounding proofs. They are not presented as new standalone results. The universal combination with A34.5 and the H-space comparison was not located in the supplied background search. No priority claim is made. The original Bousfield statements and Snaith theorem were opened. James's original is explicitly countable; unrestricted CW size uses the actual Snaith hypothesis instead.

## Finite generation exclusion in the same normal form

For each finite m at least two, a nonzero normal-form witness Z_m cannot have mod-p cohomology finitely generated as an unstable algebra. This scoped corollary is integrated in A34.6. Bousfield Theorem5.2 preserves a loop-on-H delooping under BC_p nullification; CCS Theorem7.2 gives finite total cohomology; Hemmi Theorems1.1 and2.2 force mod-p acyclicity, which is integral acyclicity for a simply connected primary space. The actual CCS Theorem5.5 fibre then makes Z_m a finite primary Postnikov piece killed by A, contrary to A-nullity and noncontractibility. Root and schematization independently reopened the exact primary inputs and accepted the argument. No sphere-nullity or completion is assumed. This is part of the same normal-form refinement, not an additional existence result or stopping-rule movement.
