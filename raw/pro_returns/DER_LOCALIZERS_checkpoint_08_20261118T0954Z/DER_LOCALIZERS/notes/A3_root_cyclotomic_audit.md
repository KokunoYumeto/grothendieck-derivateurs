# Independent audit of the cyclotomic comparison

18 November 2026. A34.4 was accepted after an independent proof check and a separate primary-source check. It is a consequence of the cited deep theorems; no priority is claimed. It does not change the principal A.3 gate.

Write D=K(C_(p-infinity),2), B=K(Fp,2), and C=Sp_T(2). The BHLS coassembly map specified in A34.4 is a noninvertible localization map for the infinite height-two cyclotomic algebra. Its actual fibre F is therefore nonzero and annihilated by that algebra in C. The CSY splitting applies to the actual multiplication-by-p map on D, not merely to an abstract isomorphic object. Its complementary summand vanishes after tensoring with F. Hence multiplication by p acts invertibly on the localized suspension spectrum with coefficient F.

The essential comparison back to ordinary spectra is to put E=T(2) smash F. The defining acyclicity criterion for L_T turns the preceding equivalence into an ordinary E-homology equivalence. The p-telescope of D is contractible, because every element of its sole nonzero homotopy group is eventually killed. Its compatible augmentations show that the initial E-homology augmentation to E is invertible. Finite cyclotomic faithfulness gives nonzero E-homology of B. Thus E kills the whole localizing ideal generated by D and does not kill B. No ring structure on E is required or asserted.

Bousfield's Lemma 8.13 gives ordinary B-acyclicity of D and supplies the inclusion of the stable ideals. It also applies to D and every 2-connected primary GEM, since its extra divisible-group requirement is then satisfied in degree two. A34.1 therefore gives the exact P_D Sigma^2 test. The stable detector also proves that no suspension of B is ordinarily D-acyclic. These two facts do not make B an A.3 witness: its suspension spectrum fails the required membership in J_p.

## A34.6: universal accessible one-generator form (root audit)

The small-subfamily step is valid in the declared pair of Grothendieck universes. We form the union of the full localizing subcategories generated by small families of actual aspherical CW models, not a class of spectra followed by an unlicensed choice of realizations. A cofiber of two objects has the union of their two families as a witness; a small coproduct has the small disjoint union of its witnessing families. Hence the union is already localizing. This gives an actual wedge V in the original aspherical class. Its third suspension U is 3-connected. Bousfield1996 Proposition3.1 puts QU in the same ordinary nullification kernel, and the reversible class contains that kernel.

The stable ideal of QU is exactly that of U. The little-cubes filtration has rth quotient C_m(r)_+ smash_{Sigma_r} U^{smash r}; May2.7/6.1 provides the approximation for all connected well-pointed compactly generated models. His4.6/4.8 identifies C_infinity(r) as free contractible. Snaith1.1 and the compatibility in finalRemark3.6 therefore give the extended-power stable splitting. Tensor-ideal and homotopy-orbit closure give one inclusion of ideals; the linear summand gives the reverse. No cardinal bound on V is needed.

We reread all of supplied REVLOC B.1-B.4. The simultaneous enlargement proof handles all three cofiber implications: two vertices are enlarged inside the original class, compatibility is restored, and the countable union has the third vertex in the class. The generating arrows are all bounded arrows with the prescribed cofibers; restricting the generator set to collapse maps would not prove accessibility of the same fundamental localizer. Taking a regular bound larger than the chosen generator then proves accessibility by minimality. This is rechecked background.

For the ordinary test, the first nonzero primary homology G of A occurs in finite degree n. Corollary3.2(ii) makes K(G,n) A-acyclic through the symmetric-product retract. Lemma8.13 gives the required GEM range with d=n-1 in the nondivisible case and d=n in the divisible case.
