# A.3 candidate work after checkpoint 07

This is exploratory work, not a principal-row status change or an additional normal-form theorem.

## 1. Comparing the finite free-loop witnesses

For the accepted A34.6 data, consider H_m=Omega^m Sigma^{m+d}X and Z_m=P_AH_m. The construction proves each Z_m is nonzero under an assumed original witness. It does not prove that a chosen nonzero homotopy class survives the transition to the next m. The first surviving homotopy degree can increase. The known spectral nullification comparison excludes the putative sufficiently connected infinite-loop witness in the relevant stable ideal; therefore simply taking the stabilization limit cannot give a separator. A new argument would need an explicit persistence or uniform-connectivity bound, not merely a nonzero object for every finite m.

## 2. K(1)-acyclization of a finite loop target does not solve the stable-membership gate

Let T be a finite simply connected primary complex and Z_0=Omega^r T. A proposed target is the homotopy fibre of Z_0 to its K(1)-homology localization. Both ends are already local for the source class whose suspension spectra lie in L_p=loc(Sigma-infinity K(F_p,2)): Z_0 by the established finite-target exclusion, and its K(1)-localization because K(1) kills that stable ideal. Local target classes are closed under homotopy fibres. Consequently this fibre cannot both be noncontractible and itself have suspension spectrum in L_p: otherwise its identity map would be null. This is the same target-locality obstruction recorded in the A4 size routes; it is not a new principal reduction.

Merely showing that the fibre is K(1)-acyclic would be weaker than the required membership in L_p. Finite spectra of chromatic type two already distinguish those conditions.

## 3. Altering an infinite cell system

A remaining possible construction is a space with the stable homotopy type of a known L_p generator but different reversible asphericity, assembled through unstable changes whose suspension bounds are not uniform. No such space has been constructed here.

There are concrete traps. A telescope of actual stable equivalences is already a homology equivalence at the space level and supplies no example, as M6 proves. Changing the maps between the literal finite skeleta of K(F_p,2), while preserving the bottom homology isomorphism, cannot change their stabilized low Postnikov groups: in every fixed degree above two the skeletal system eventually has zero homotopy group. Thus that simplest skeletal-telescope variant still has homotopy type K(F_p,2). An argument using different finite-stage spaces would need actual attaching maps and compatible stable equivalences, together with an independent surviving nullification invariant. No claim that arbitrary stable maps lift after one common finite suspension is made.

The same-n-type route also needs care. The already read localization-genus sources do not provide a pair with equal suspension spectra and distinct reversible asphericity. No new original theorem establishing such a pair was located.

## 5. Rechecked periodic-homotopy exclusion, with the telescope distinction

A further proposed separator was a primary H-space Z whose suspension spectrum is T(n)-acyclic but whose v_n-periodic homotopy is nonzero. This does not work. The primary arXiv text of Barkan–Heuts–Shi, *On periodic homotopy and homology equivalences of spaces*, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.10867, Theorem1.13, was reopened, along with its Section4.3 proof. For a map of H-spaces with p-infinity-torsion homotopy groups in positive degrees, a T(n)-equivalence is a v_n-periodic equivalence (and an equivalence on truncations through n). Its proof invokes Theorem2.15, Theorem4.4 and Theorem2.10. Taking the map Z to the point gives the claimed exclusion. This is a re-use of the theorem already listed in the source ledger, not a new principal reduction.

T(n) cannot be replaced by K(n) here. The distinction is material at heights where the telescope conjecture fails. Nor does the theorem say that periodic localization of an ordinary A-null space is still A-null. The latter preservation statement remains a missing premise in the periodic-target attempt.

Theorem4.1 of the same text was also reopened: simultaneous T(i)-homology equivalence for i at most n is equivalent to the sufficiently suspended finite-nullification / v_i-periodic equivalence criterion. This supplies no unsuspended ordinary A-nullity comparison for the infinite generator A of A34.6.
