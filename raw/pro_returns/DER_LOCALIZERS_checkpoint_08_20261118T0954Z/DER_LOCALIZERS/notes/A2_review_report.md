## Earlier attempts at the coefficient-strength comparison (historical)

1. Parameterwise evaluation proves all shifts, but does not turn an arbitrary corner coefficient into a constant one. The proposed diagonal-in-a-shift step changes the corner parameter and does not commute with the corner Kan extension.
2. The homotopy density/coend formula expresses an arbitrary coefficient diagram through free constant pieces. Passing the right corner Kan extension through that coend would require the unproved commutation of pullbacks with the relevant colimits; it is circular as a proof of half-stability.
3. A pointed suspension square is obtained by taking pointwise cofibers of the constant-coefficient free-loop or circle square. Inferring that those pointwise cofibers preserve cartesianness again assumes the stability implication at issue. No such inference is used.
4. The standard connective and coconnective examples separate the unrestricted half-axioms, but do not separate a restricted-coefficient version from its own unrestricted half. Both are therefore insufficient for that converse question.
5. Adding a new initial or terminal object to a stable infinity category does not supply a separator: the constant coefficient equal to that added object, with an empty-fiber/nonempty-fiber span, already violates the corresponding restricted axiom.
6. An ordinary coherent-arrow fiber is not a replacement for the missing general derived-slice theorem: the explicit q:S→S⊕ΣS mapping-space calculation in the section shows the loss.

These unsuccessful routes explain the gaps present at the first checkpoint. A2.10–A2.13 subsequently settle the coefficient-strength comparison by different arguments. They do not supply a general bare-derivator slice parametrization; that distinct boundary remains explicit.
