The full original body was retrieved; its scan and OCR remain in background/research, outside the deliverable. The targeted statements and their surrounding proofs were read. Exact matrix formulas in the long construction have not been independently recomputed.

B.1, printed372–373, characterizes full embeddability of a small monocategory into a quasifiltered monocategory by boundedness of every morphism-chain relation R_Delta(A,X). B.2–B.9 prove necessity and construct the completion by adjoining controlled pair quasifilters and common successors. The conclusion is quasifilteredness, not totality.

A.4, printed370, explicitly corrects the earlier conflation of pair affine equalizers with equalizers of arbitrary finite families. C.2, printed385–389, constructs a quasifiltered category whose integral affinization is not filtered. Its proof maintains |union_i h_i(W)| >3r/2 for r distinct three-point copies.

Our independent implication from that displayed bound: every nonempty finite collection has a private selected vertex, so its marginal-difference matrix is injective over every nonzero ring. Thus this particular construction cannot provide a category exact in all positive characteristics but not over Q. No totality assertion or coefficient-selective completion theorem is imported from it.
