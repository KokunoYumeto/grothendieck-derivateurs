## Approaches explored and their exact limitations

1. **Finite modular incidence patterns.** Exact integer computation shows the exceptional-prime effect occurs for actual ordered triple incidence matrices, not just arbitrary integer matrices. The finite category cannot itself be used as an exactness counterexample. A recursive extension that preserves the rational obstruction while solving *every* modular family remains unbuilt.
2. **A_2(Q).** Its action components are contractible or K(Q,2), hence mod-p acyclic. This does not establish exactness for arbitrary Fp-module diagrams: total C_delta already disproves that general inference. A direct affine equalizer proof or an all-module theorem is still required.
3. **Primitive cochain expansions.** The ordered class of (r-1)-cochains is Ramsey. For finite coefficient groups Lambda, primitive fibers are finite and uniformly restrict, suggesting exactness after inverting |Lambda|; this is delegated to dynamics_totality. For Lambda=Q the fibers are infinite. The forgetful expansion functor is not homologically final: X/u splits by the primitive restricted to X. Its components do not merge merely because K(Q,1) has trivial mod-p homology.
4. **Allowing gauge morphisms.** The category of primitive cochains with gauge maps has fiber BQ and a mod-p homological-final projection, but it is not itself exact: a nonzero central constant gauge gives an infinite translation on a representable and an injective difference operator. Thus this construction alone does not transfer exactness.
5. **Coefficient-independent algebra.** General augmented rings such as Z plus a rational square-zero ideal exhibit the desired fiberwise-flat versus generic-nonflat behavior, but their additive relations do not directly arise from an ordinary category's free abelian linearization. No valid realization as the required category has been supplied.
6. **Finite-hom arithmetic compactness.** Each individual target has only finitely many exceptional primes, but the targets can grow without bound. Finite hom-sets alone do not justify choosing a common finite target for all primes or passing from an ultraproduct target back into the original category.
7. **Literature.** Opened the author-upload full text of Husainov, *Homological dimension theory of small categories*, J. Math. Sci. 110 (2002), 2273–2321, DOI 10.1023/A:1014945907335. Section 2.3, Theorems 2.5–2.7, recapitulates affinization and exactness; it does not settle the characteristic question. The survey's Problem 2.5 is the older fixed-point-property equivalence, distinct from the present all-prime implication. Opened Isbell's Annals 1974 landing page but full text not yet retrieved. No unverified literature theorem is being used.

## Breakthrough: main implication refuted

The earlier central-open status above is superseded by Theorem `C4:counterexample`, now written in full and integrated into `sections/C.tex` at the former gate paragraph. The counterexample is A_2(Q). The essential proof is not actionwise homology: in characteristic p, group algebras of uniquely p-divisible abelian groups are perfect. Their marginal kernel ideals generate a distributive lattice, so a generalized Chinese remainder argument gives finite-support lifts of every finite compatible system of primitive marginals. Induction on finite ordered structures supplies coherent normalized modular weights. A fully explicit Ramsey-expansion argument then gives affine equalizers for every finite arrow family, proving exactness for all module diagrams. A fixed two-block face family excludes rational exactness. Dynamics_totality independently audited the algebra and cochain extension steps.

The same proof yields the complete field profile for every countable abelian Lambda and r>=2: Fp exactness iff multiplication by p on Lambda is bijective; Q exactness iff Lambda is torsion; Z exactness iff Lambda=0. Necessity in characteristic p uses both Lambda/pLambda and the UCT Tor quotient Lambda[p].

Concrete finite primitive distributions are in `code/C4_perfect_marginal_certificate.py(.out)`. For a rational triangle of cocycle value1, 2p-1 signed primitive atoms of augmentation1 have all three edge marginals delta0 modulo p; one integral marginal differs by p(delta_(1/p)-delta0). This is a transparent model of the denominator phenomenon.

**Only remaining main C.4 gate:** the additional restriction that A is total. Contractible Kan simplex categories are excluded by C4.5. ## Continuing the total-only gate

The following further routes have been examined; none is a proof or counterexample.

- A categorical initial cone over A_2(Q) preserves its exactness profile and makes the base contractible, but is not total: the original A_2(Q) remains a full cosieve, and extending its singleton action by the empty set gives a noncontractible action component. Contractible base alone cannot be substituted for totality.
- Adding a named fixed point to the Fraisse presentation has the same problem. If every object contains it, nonempty-base functorial amalgamation at that point would require the missing empty-base amalgamation of the original class. If objects may omit it, the action detecting a nonempty old part still has a K(Q,2) component.
- Allowing finite ordered lists of A-objects and component-merging maps fails condition I, already for A=Delta_inj: two source singleton components can map to distinct points in one target component or to the same point in another, and injectivity inside a component prevents an amalgamating square. The proposed componentwise proof of exactness was invalid because it controlled marginals rather than joint tuples.
- Requiring distinct source components to remain distinct repairs that particular defect, but yields a decorated convex equivalence class construction. The action selecting one equivalence class recovers the old A_2(Q) homotopy type; hence it is still not total.
- Increasing the cocycle degree r gives arbitrarily highly connected A_r(Q), but does not directly yield a total counterexample. A direct limit that eventually erases every fixed finite cocycle pattern also erases every fixed finite affine obstruction; an ultraproduct would need uniform support bounds for modular affine equalizers, which have not been proved.
- The conjectural implication "total plus rational exact implies integral exact" would help A.3(ii) but has not been proved. The known examples (C_delta, ordered posets, finite-group cocycles) do not decide it. No inference from vanishing set-action homology to arbitrary module homology has been made.
- A possible geometric candidate for A.3(ii), rather than the all-prime direction, is independently ordered finite rational probability Boolean algebras with strong subalgebras. Conditional-product amalgamation and a finite Markov-chain bridge might supply the structural axioms, and normalized finite additive measures give a plausible Fp obstruction. Amenability, the required ordered functorial amalgamation, and the bridge have not been established. The familiar natural-order measure-algebra Ramsey route encounters a separate published difficult Ramsey question and is not being assumed.

I found and proved a five-map F2 obstruction. For the three-dimensional space Q with binary labels and reference order 1<2<3<4<6<5<7, take the five plane maps from the ordered punctured two-dimensional space with image triples 123,167,257,347,365. In every auxiliary natural order, the parity sum of comparisons 2<1, 1<6, 2<5, 5<6, and the assertion that4 lies between3 and7 is zero. This is an elementary identity of leading-coordinate linear functionals. If the five marginals were equal, the same sum would be the augmentation, namely1. Thus there is no F2 affine equalizer at any target. `notes/ordered_vector_obstruction.tex` gives the coordinate proof; `code/C4_ordered_vector_probe.py(.out)` verifies all168 natural orders and finds the parity certificate. Dynamics_totality then proved amenability directly by random-color counting, avoiding the unproved double-order Ramsey expansion. Start with a Reiter measure on order injections of q points into m colors; randomly color the nonzero vectors of a large F2-space and order by color, breaking ties arbitrarily. Weight each linear embedding by its induced color injection. Conditional on a fixed P-embedding, expected marginal weights are the order Reiter marginals. Extension pairs with intersection exactly P are conditionally independent; the proportion of exceptional pairs is at most (2^d-2^c)^2/(2^n-2^c). The resulting L1 expectation bound, compared with expected total weight, provides a coloring with positive weight and arbitrarily close normalized marginals. I checked the extension count, covariance bound, summation over all linear P-maps, and the strict-negative-expectation step. This establishes Re, hence amenability and CC. Together with totality and the F2 obstruction it refutes both requested implications.

### Rational follow-ups checked without a conclusion

- Uniform probability on natural orders is restriction coherent, but using it to prove rational exactness requires exactness of the expansion by both a natural order and the arbitrary reference order. That expansion has not been proved Ramsey or exact here.
- Bodirsky's free-superposition theorem cannot be applied without checking its relational hereditary and strong-amalgamation hypotheses: vector spaces have nontrivial finite algebraic closure, and arbitrary subsets need not be closed subspaces.

It proves Ramsey for canonically ordered colored-subspace systems with avoidance of irreducible systems. Encoding the reference order by plane colors leaves a completion gap: its partial output can contain long directed comparison cycles, which need not be irreducible. - Opened Evans, *Permutation modules for Ramsey structures*, arXiv2603.29606 (2026), Theorem2.8 and Lemma3.7. ### New conditional rational criterion (C4.10)

All-prime exactness implies rational exactness if each finite-family saturated-image quotient has bounded exponent. A prime outside that exponent lifts a modular kernel vector to an integral kernel vector with nonzero augmentation. Finite generation of the saturation gives the exponent bound. This yields sufficient Noetherian conditions either on integral representable functors or, in setting(S), on integral smooth permutation modules. The theorem and full proof are in `sections/C4_addendum.tex`, label `C4:noetherian`; it does not claim the required Noetherian property for general oligomorphic groups.

### Ordinary ultraproduct approach excluded (08:01 UTC)

C4.17/C.29 excludes the literal category ultraproduct route, over every nonzero coefficient ring. Already the two inclusions [n] into [n+1] obtained by dropping the first or last point require affine equalizers with at least n support terms.
