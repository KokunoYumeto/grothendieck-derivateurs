\section*{Conventions and certification}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Conventions and certification}
All categories, sets, and groups are small in a fixed universe. We put
$BA=|NA|$. A functor is final when each under-comma category is nonempty
and connected. We use the classes $W_n$, $W_\infty$, and $W_{\Z}$ with
the meanings in the prompt; in particular $W_n$ includes the test on
components. Actions are covariant set-valued functors. Presheaves are
contravariant. For $H:U^{\op}\to\Set$, an arrow
$(u,h)\to(v,k)$ in $U/H$ is an arrow $a:u\to v$ with $H(a)k=h$.
The terminal presheaf defines totality of a category. ``Total'' without
a subscript means total for $W_\infty$.

The supplied earlier returns are treated as mathematical arguments to audit,
not as authorities. We state the particular steps retained from them.
``Proved'' certifies the quantified positive result. ``Refuted'' identifies
the proposed assertion or implication being rejected; the displayed
counterexample and its failure are proved in the accompanying text.
In a working or interrupted return an unresolved claim is
explicitly marked ``Open'' and is not counted as a completed item.
