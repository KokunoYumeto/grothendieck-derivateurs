\begin{claim}{F.1}{Proved}{The exact hierarchy at II ms. 36 ter}
\label{F:hierarchy}
Among nonempty connected small categories, the corrected hierarchy is
\[
 \{\text{filtered}\}\subsetneq\{\text{exact}\}
 \subsetneq\{\text{total}\}\subsetneq\mathcal Q_2.
\]
The successive witnesses are $\Delta_{\rm inj}$, $C_\delta$, and the
specified repaired $K^*$. Moreover $\mathcal Q_\infty$ is exactly
the total class, every $\mathcal Q_{n+1}\subsetneq\mathcal Q_n$
is strict, and the interleaved truncated-totality hierarchy is
that of A.9. Free-monoid comparisons do not recognize the first
of these classes: every exact index passes them by D.6.
\end{claim}
\begin{proof}
We distinguish the previously established inputs from the conclusions
settled here. Filtered colimits of abelian groups are exact.
The affine-cone proof of exactness of $\Delta_{\rm inj}$ was
checked in D.7; it is not filtered, because two distinct injections
cannot become equal after a further injection. The exact-to-total
implication is the audited R1/R6 argument used in D.2: exactness
kills the derived colimits of permutation modules, hence the
positive homology of every action component; applying this also
to the cyclic covers of those components kills their fundamental
groups, and Hurewicz gives weak contractibility.

For $C_\delta$, D.4 audits the full R17 action reduction and the
module with $T_1=\Z$, so totality and nonexactness are both
retained with their proofs checked. For $K^*$, A.15 checks the
repaired construction and its surviving binary-cycle class;
its action component with nonzero $H_2$ excludes totality while
the quasi-filtered axioms hold. No specific admissible choice
is left implicit in our named witness. The remaining hierarchy
and each of its strictness witnesses are proved in A.8--A.9.
Thus the three counterexamples already furnished by the returns
settle the older exactness questions, and the cocycle categories
settle every higher truncated question.
\end{proof}

\begin{claim}{F.2}{Refuted}{The converse question at XII ms. 94--97}
\label{F:presheaf-category}
Total asphericity of a presheaf does not imply total asphericity
of its category of elements, even for $W_\infty$, $W_{\Z}$, or
any $W_n$ with $n\geq2$, and even for a five-element act over
the three-element monoid $M_1$.
The forward implication of XII Proposition 8 remains valid for
every fundamental localizer.
\end{claim}
\begin{proof}
Let $K$ be the simplicial graph with three vertices $v_0,v_1,v_2$
and edges $e_{01}:v_0\to v_1$, $e_{12}:v_1\to v_2$.
There are no other nondegenerate simplices. Put $H=K_1$; its
five elements are the three degenerate edges and the two named
edges. In $M_1=\{c_0,\id,c_1\}$ the two constant maps send each
edge to its indicated endpoint, and fix each degenerate edge.
These formulas specify the whole right action.
Since $|K|$ is a contractible interval, E.4 proves total
$W_\infty$-asphericity of $H$, and therefore totality for all
the other displayed localizers.

Write $E=M_1/H$. Both nondegenerate edges have only their
identity arrow going out. They are distinct, whereas $v_1$
has an arrow to each. Thus this span has no common successor;
$E$ fails condition I and is not quasi-filtered. For a direct
homological witness, let $V=\{e_{01},e_{12}\}\subset E$ be the
full cosieve, and take the covariant action on $E$ which has
two points outside $V$ and one point on $V$, collapsing the
two points whenever an arrow enters $V$. Its element category
is two copies of $E$ glued along $V$. Every nerve simplex lies
in one copy, so this is a pushout of nerve subcomplexes.
By E.2, $BE$ is contractible; $BV$ is the discrete two-point
space. Mayer--Vietoris gives $H_1=\Z$ for this action component.
Hence $E$ is not total for any $W_n$, $n\geq2$, by R2's
audited restricted-action criterion, nor for $W_{\Z}$ or
$W_\infty$. The program \texttt{code/F\_five\_act.py} checks
the complete monoid, act, category, doubled category and integral
homology certificate. Notice that this action is on $E$;
it is not being claimed to be pulled back from $M_1$.

For the forward implication, if $E=U/H$ is total and
$p:U\to U'$ is any functor, write $\pi:E\to U$ for projection.
The canonical isomorphism $\pi_!1_E\cong H$ follows from the
discrete-fibration description of the element category.
Consequently $p_!H\cong(p\pi)_!1_E$, whose element category
is $W$-aspherical by the definition of totality of $E$.
This checks precisely the proof of XII Proposition 8 on
ms. 93--94. Grothendieck's hesitation at ms. 97,
\emph{je me vois mal construire un exemple}, is answered by
the five-element act above. The distinct 52-element example
of PRETOT A.5 remains relevant to the stronger distinction
between homological and weak-homotopy totality of the presheaf
itself, as E.4--E.5 explain.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
Each row cites the proof of its mathematical assertion. For the
retained composition statement in XII ms. 113--114 we checked
PRETOT B.4 and TOTASPH F.9. Here is the specific strict-composition
step. For strict $f:X\to Y$, a total presheaf $H$ on $Y$, and
arbitrary $p:X\to X'$, put $Q_y=p_!Y(f(-),y)$ and
$K=\int^{y\in Y}Q_y\times H(y)\cong p_!f^*H$. Each $Q_y$
is total. In the collage with full parts $X',Y$ and crossing
sets $Q_y(x')$, extend $H$ along $Y\hookrightarrow Z$ to $F$.
Its restrictions are $H$ and $K$. The slices of
$X'/K\hookrightarrow Z/F$ are either terminal slices or
$X'/Q_y$, hence are $W$-aspherical. Relative Theorem A makes
this inclusion a $W$-equivalence. Totality of $H$ makes $Z/F$
aspherical, proving totality of $f^*H$ for every $p$ and thus
strict composition. For total $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to Z$, the
slice of $X/z\to Y/z$ over $(y,\beta)$ is exactly $X/y$;
strict restriction applied to $1_{Y/z}$ proves total composition.
For total sieves the extra closure
needed is exactly nonempty-cosieve closure, isolated in A.2;
this is why the answer changes at finite truncation levels.
We also checked the negative strict-sieve assertion of PRETOT
B.4, rather than using its positive composition proof for a different
claim. Let $H=K_2$ over $U=M_2$, where $K$ is the free degeneracy
completion of the coned polygon presentation
$\langle a,b\mid a^5b^{-3},\ b^3(ab)^{-2}\rangle$.
The cellular exponent matrix is
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}5&-2\\-3&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$,
of determinant $-1$, so $K$ is acyclic. The permutations
$a=(1\ 4\ 3\ 2\ 5)$ and $b=(1\ 2\ 3)$ satisfy the relators
and generate a nontrivial quotient. E.2 and E.4 therefore give
total $W_{\Z}$-asphericity of $H$ and nontrivial
$\pi_1 B(U/H)$. On $B=U^{\triangleleft}$ extend $H$ by a
singleton at the new initial object, obtaining $F$.
Then $B/F=(U/H)^{\triangleleft}$ is contractible. For any
covariant $L:B\to\Set$, its restricted action is obtained
from that of $H$ by adjoining one cone on $U/H$ for each
element of $L(0)$. Each attaching space and each old component
is acyclic; no cone joins distinct components. The restricted
action criterion thus makes $F$ totally $W_\infty$-aspherical:
after any change of base the canonical final map gives a
simply connected target, and the acyclic comma resolution
gives its acyclicity.

Adjoin a lower copy $U_0$ to $B$ with
$X(u_0,b)=B(u,b)$ and no arrows backwards. The sieve
$U_0\to X$ is classical Dwyer, since its generated cosieve
is $U\times[1]$ with the evident right adjoint onto $U_0$.
For $j:B\to X$, put $G=j_!F$; its restrictions to $B,U_0$
are $F,H$, respectively. The one-point collage $Y=X\cup_G\{t\}$
makes $X\to Y$ dwy\'erienne because $G$ is total, but the
composite has crossing weight $H$ and fails its identity
$W_\infty$ test. This is the complete finite example with
$32$ arrows in $X$ and $138$ in $Y$.
Our independent reconstruction
\texttt{code/F\_strict\_composition\_check.py} checks the
$52$-element act, the exponent matrix, the nontrivial quotient,
both sieves, the right adjoint, all $2{,}131$ composable pairs,
and all $28{,}204$ associativity triples.
The manuscript's \emph{n'ont pas du tout l'air de l'\^etre par
composition} is therefore resolved separately for these classes,
not by conflating them. At XV ms. 133 quinquies the displayed
$h=(f_0,f_1)$ has target $Y$, so its type is $X\amalg X\to Y$;
G.6 records the same transcription correction with the proof
of the direction of implication. The literature and source
access records are supplied in the report and bibliography.
\end{proof}
