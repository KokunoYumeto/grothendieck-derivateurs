## Discarded approaches and remaining requested issue

Before D.6, we tried to realize a five-element rectangular-band monoid (classifying space S²) by interval/circular subdivision diagrams over Delta_inj. Ordinary interval subdivision gives the absorption presentation above; circular subdivision gives further idempotent absorption, not a sphere. The new theorem proves that no nonaspherical target can arise from an exact-index diagram of free monoids, so continuing that counterexample search would be futile.

The C_delta group presentation does not automatically supply free monoids: forgetting a group leaves nontrivial units, and free-factor inclusions do not select compatible positive bases. A single relator has positive realizations (for example u2=a u1 and v1=a v3, or v1=u2 t and v3=u1 t). Across overlapping hyperedges these impose common-prefix/suffix constraints; no functorial simultaneous positive basis has been established. A closed one-edge copy meets another closed subobject in at most one vertex, which may help a future positive-refinement argument, but this observation alone is not a proof. We make no assertion that C_delta passes or fails all FreeMon tests. This classification is expressly requested in D.1 and remains unresolved; it is not discharged by the counterexample refuting D.1's general implication or the free-group alternative settling D.4.

The exact-index proof cannot be extended to merely total A by dropping exactness: the comma spectral sequence may then contain T_p H_1(D(a)), p>0. Totality kills the derived colimits of permutation modules, not arbitrary H1 modules. Similarly, transporting the vanishing from the source to BC before proving the comparison would be circular. These gaps are explicitly avoided in D.6.
