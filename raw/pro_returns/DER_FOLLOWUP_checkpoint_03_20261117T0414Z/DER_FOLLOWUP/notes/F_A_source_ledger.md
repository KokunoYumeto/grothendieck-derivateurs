## Attribution safeguards

The 1976 Isbell–Mitchell paper separates pairwise affine equalization (condition IV) from finite-family affine equalization (condition III). Its Theorem 3.1 and Proposition 3.3 concern exact abelian colimits. Corollary 4.4 needs conditions I and IV, connectedness, and a weak terminal object. These distinctions matter for the strict chain in Part F and are preserved. A.2's translation between quasi-filteredness and all-action simple connectivity is explicitly proved from the attached arguments; it is not attributed verbatim to a theorem about nerves in the 1976 article.

The source ledger makes no claim of bibliographical priority for A.3–A.14. The constructive proofs and the all-action quantifiers are what is certified here. “No matching theorem found” would be stronger than the performed searches warrant and is not asserted.
