Every original payload byte is preserved. After extraction run:

  python FINMON_restore_all_encodings.py

All original SHA256s are verified. Source tables are restored before source-linked certificates, and original page PNGs before contact PNGs. Conflicting existing files are never replaced. GNU gzip and compatible Python/zlib are required for the indicated records. Exact contact PNG reconstruction additionally requires compatible Pillow and FreeType; its exact font bytes are embedded and every full original PNG hash is checked. Self-contained C++ stdout recipes also require g++ with C++17 support and can take several minutes.
