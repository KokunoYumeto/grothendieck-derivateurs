## C.L6 — Lead 6 is true

Let A be small, nonempty, and connected. For any small D and functor u:D→A, define the covariant A-set H_u by H_u(a)=π₀(u↓a). Here an object is (d,f:u(d)→a), and a morphism (d,f)→(d',f') is δ:d→d' satisfying f=f'u(δ). The A-action is postcomposition. The functor j_u:D→El_A H_u takes d to (u(d),[d,id]) and δ to u(δ).

For every A-set H and functor v:D→El_AH, write v(d)=(u(d),h_d). There is a natural transformation of A-sets λ:H_u→H, defined by λ_a[d,f]=H(f)h_d. It is well defined because v being a functor means H(uδ)h_d=h_d', so f=f'uδ implies H(f)h_d=H(f')h_d'. Postcomposition establishes naturality. The induced functor El_Aλ:El_AH_u→El_AH has the *literal* equality v=(El_Aλ)j_u on objects and arrows.

If A is total, every component of El_AH_u is a weakly contractible CW realization, hence contractible by Whitehead; therefore Bj_u is nullhomotopic on every component of D.

Conversely suppose Bj_u is nullhomotopic for every u from a face poset of a finite triangulated sphere. We use the following standard finite nerve approximation in its precise form: for every small category E and continuous f:Sⁿ→|NE|, n≥1, there is a finite triangulated n-sphere K, an iterated barycentric subdivision sd^rK, its face poset D, and a functor v:D→E such that f is homotopic to |Nv| after identifying |ND| with Sⁿ. One proof first uses the weak equivalence NE→Ex^∞NE. A homotopy class from a finite simplicial sphere is represented at a finite Ex-stage, since the source and the simplicial homotopy witnessing equality have finitely many simplices. Sd–Ex adjunction gives a simplicial map from a finite iterated subdivision to NE. One further subdivision is the nerve of the face poset; compose its last-vertex map with the preceding map. Every simplicial map between category nerves is induced by a unique functor. The realization of the last-vertex map is homotopic to the subdivision homeomorphism. Thus the specified homotopy class is represented by Bv.

Apply this to E=El_AH and project v to u:D→A. The literal factorization just proved makes Bv nullhomotopic by hypothesis. Every homotopy group πₙ of every component of BE therefore vanishes. The component is nonempty and path connected by definition, so it is weakly contractible. This is totality. All finite posets give a formally stronger test, and the forward implication already proves it; the sphere-poset subclass suffices. The empty poset contributes no condition. S⁰ can be included harmlessly, but n≥1 suffices.

## C.L7 — Lead 7 is true, with its stated hypothesis essential

Let A' be the collage adjoining one final-height object Z to A along a contravariant functor R:A^op→Set, and let H':A'→Set. Write H=H'|A. Define E=El(R,H): objects (x,r,k) with r∈R(x), k∈H(x); a morphism to (y,s,l) is an A-arrow f:x→y satisfying R(f)s=r and H(f)k=l. There is a function λ:Ob(E)→H'(Z), λ(x,r,k)=H'(r)k. Functoriality makes λ constant along every E-arrow. The old comma category over (Z,h), denoted L_h, is exactly the full union of E-components on which λ=h. This statement does not require any H-arrow to be injective.

Suppose ũ:D̃→A and an isomorphism of presheaves R≅colim_{d∈D̃} A(-,ũd) are specified. Thus R(x)=π₀(x↓ũ), canonically. The comparison functor F:El_D̃(ũ*H)→E sends (d,k) to (ũd,[d,id],k), and an arrow δ to ũδ. For e=(x,r,k), the comma category e↓F is isomorphic to the component of x↓ũ indexed by r: its object is (d,f:x→ũd) in that component, and its H-coordinate is forced to be H(f)k. Its arrow condition is exactly the comma-arrow condition. If each such component is weakly contractible, Quillen's Theorem A applies to F and gives BF a weak equivalence. This is a specified comparison, not merely an abstract isomorphism on homology.

For fixed h, take D_h to be the union of components in the source selected by λF=h. The restriction F:D_h→L_h has the same contractible comma categories and is a weak equivalence. If n=dim ND̃ is finite, then dim NEl_D̃(ũ*H)≤n: an arrow over an identity is an identity, so a nondegenerate chain projects to a nondegenerate chain. Consequently H_j(L_h;Z)=0 for j>n. The category El_A'H' is obtained from El_AH by coning each L_h, as is seen by classifying a nondegenerate nerve simplex according to whether its last vertex is old or (Z,h). Its relative chain complex is the direct sum of shifted augmented link complexes. Hence the long exact sequence gives an injection H_j(El_AH;Z)→H_j(El_A'H';Z) for every j>n. The claim remains valid if there are infinitely many h, because chains are finite sums and homology commutes with direct sums. When n=0 or 1, no old H₂ class can die.

The dimension conclusion is *conditional* on the comma-component hypothesis. A necklace diagram of a quasi-filter is one-dimensional. If f,g:T→S agree after c:x→T, the copies of c and of fc=gc form a nonzero circular 1-cycle in x↓ũ. Because the latter is a graph, no 2-boundary can fill that cycle; its component is not weakly contractible. In particular the actual-path repair has α₄ₖv₁=α₀v₁, so the next quasi-filter for (α₀,α₄ₖ) fails the hypothesis at x=V. The lemma alone therefore cannot establish the universal AKR persistence assertion.

## C.L8 — Lead 8 is true

The precise Ty definition is PRETOT Definition E.1 (label E:path-definition), read in full: finite sign words τ define free oriented-line categories I_τ, and τ→ρ is an endpoint-preserving functor whose vertex function φ:{0,…,|τ|}→{0,…,|ρ|} is nondecreasing. This is the final restriction at Grothendieck VII ms.31–32, verified in the supplied transcription.

Every target letter j∈{1,…,|ρ|} has a unique source index i with φ(i−1)<j≤φ(i), since the nondecreasing intervals partition the target letters. Define f(j)=i. This f is weakly increasing and preserves signs: every nonempty interval traversed by one source edge must have that edge's sign. Conversely a weakly increasing sign-preserving f determines φ(i)=|{j:f(j)≤i}|. The two constructions are inverse, including empty words. They respect composition because the unique source edge whose image traverses a target edge is obtained by successive pullback of edge indices. Thus Ty^op is *isomorphic*, on these fixed word objects, to the category of weakly increasing sign-preserving letter maps.

In A=Ty^op put p=(++), q=(+−+), a=e=(1,3), b=c=(1,1). For every word σ and arrow g:q→σ, monotonicity and opposite adjacent signs force g(1)<g(2)<g(3). Hence ge:p→σ is injective, whereas every h c is constant. Therefore no pair g,h:q→σ satisfies ge=hc: condition I fails.

For every nonzero commutative unital ring R and σ define an R-linear functional ℓ_σ:R[A(p,σ)]→R by ℓ_σ(d)=1 if d is injective and 0 if d is constant. These are the only possibilities for p's two letters. For every finite R-linear combination t=Σ_g r_g g of q→σ, ℓ_σ(te)=Σ_g r_g and ℓ_σ(tc)=0. Equality te=tc therefore forces aug(t)=0. No affine equalizer exists over R, and condition IV fails integrally. The integral implication total⇒IV (checked in TOTEX A.4 against its explicit two-sequence computation) excludes totality; exactness over R also fails by the affine criterion of Isbell–Mitchell 1976, Theorem 3.1 and the ring remark p.296. The category has empty word as an initial object, so it is nonempty and connected; those hypotheses are not the obstruction.

## C.L9 — Lead 9 is true

Let A be a separator, and let a₀,…,a_k:p→q be a family without integral affine equalizer, with k minimal over all such families in A. Totality gives I and IV, so k≥2.

(a) If b:q→p existed, Isbell–Mitchell 1976, Corollary 4.3, p.297, would supply an *ordinary* arrow equalizing the finite family. We opened the statement and its preceding Theorem 4.1/Corollary 4.2 argument. Its second assertion applies exactly to A=p,B=q with C(B,A) nonempty; no ambient weakly terminal hypothesis is needed. An ordinary equalizer is an affine equalizer, contradiction.

(b) If |A(p,x)| were bounded above by a finite integer, the representable A-set A(p,-) is indecomposable (its category of elements p/A has initial object id_p). Proposition 4.5, pp.297–298, applies under I and V; IV and V are equivalent by the necklace criterion. It yields condition II for that representable. Finite ordinary equalization follows inductively from pair equalization, contradicting the chosen family. The source proof was opened: it quotients by eventual equality, uses I to make this a congruence, chooses a value of maximal finite cardinality, and uses the necklace identity to force every pair of quotient elements equal. Thus the claimed bound really need only concern this one representable; a bound on every representable is unnecessary.

(c) Let C⊂A be full on objects x with A(p,x)≠∅. It is a cosieve, is nonempty, contains p and q, and p is weakly initial in C. For every C-set H, extend H by ∅ on A\C. This is a covariant A-set because no arrow leaves the cosieve; arrows from outside into C have their unique empty-source map. Its category of elements is literally El_CH. Totality of A therefore makes C total. Since every affine arrow of C is one in A, the specified family still has no affine equalizer. C is connected because p maps to every object, so it is again a separator.

(d) If ga_i=ga_j for some g:q→x and i≠j, the distinct arrows in {ga₀,…,ga_k} form a family of cardinality at most k. Minimality of k gives an affine equalizer t:x→y for that smaller family. Bilinear composition makes tg:q→y affine, because aug(tg)=aug(t)aug(g)=1, and it equalizes all a_i. This contradicts the choice of family. Thus every postcomposed family is pairwise distinct. This argument uses global minimality among families in A, exactly as stated; minimality merely among subfamilies of the original family would not suffice.

## C.1 — ongoing

The general AKR non-totality/persistence assertion is not yet proved. The exact missing point is the map from H₂ of an arbitrary future link into the old element category. The Lead 7 hypothesis fails for specific allowed higher quasi-filters, and a blanket one-dimensional argument is invalid.

A strengthened algebraic consequence of the AKR expansion invariant is available: if each nonempty finite support S⊂A(W,X) of size r uses more than 3r/2 points among all gv_i, then the simultaneous-annihilator module ker(P_W→P_V²) is zero at X, not merely of zero augmentation. If t is a nonzero annihilator with support S, every used point must appear in at least two incidences (otherwise one of the two difference equations has a unique nonzero coefficient there). There are 3r incidences, so at most 3r/2 points, contradiction. This holds over every nonzero ring for a finite-support nonzero t: a unique coefficient cannot cancel. It does not by itself imply non-totality.
