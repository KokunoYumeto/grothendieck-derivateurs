## Approaches and exact obstacles

E investigation: the uncountable simple locally free group route fails. A nontrivial locally free perfect group cannot be finitely normally generated (Magnus-degree argument); a simple group would be normally generated by any nonidentity element. The known general dimension bound for uncountable locally free groups also does not supply an actual two-dimensional model.

The remaining E2 problem is exact: construct an actual two-dimensional K(G,1) with H₁(G;Z)=0, H₂(G;Z)=Z and no nontrivial countable image, or prove such a group has a nontrivial countable image. The finite-bound construction works, and the audited residual-cardinality theorem rules out every bound at least the continuum. The interval ℵ₀≤μ<2^ℵ₀ remains undecided here; CH reduces it to the single bound ℵ₀ without deciding it. For infinite rank κ, existence is established for μ<κ and nonexistence for μ≥κ^ℵ₀; the interval κ≤μ<κ^ℵ₀ remains undecided. Rank zero admits all bounds. If κ^ℵ₀=κ the answer is exactly μ<κ.

Two additional obstruction lemmas have been proved during this investigation. A cyclic cover of an actual two-dimensional classifying space has H₂ free over Q[t,t⁻¹], which prevents an uncountable H₂ rank from being compressed to finite rank by a semidirect-product shift. Also, the pointwise finite normal-generation norm in INDLOCAL A.31 prevents ordinary countable equation compactness: an essential countable subgroup of an ultrapower supplies a countable, finitely solvable but unsolvable equation system. Its exponent rows need not be independent, so this does not supply the nonsingular acyclic extension needed to decide E2. Both proofs and the precise limitation are in Statements E.11–E.12.

The current research paths are: (i) convert the essential pure extension into an actual two-dimensional relative homology equivalence, without hiding singular exponent rows; (ii) strengthen the nested commutator diagonal construction so that it handles simple groups with pointwise finite but globally unbounded normal-generation norms; (iii) find a dimension-preserving small-homology construction beyond the cyclic-kernel and locally free approaches. Mere finite solvability, a nondiscrete group topology, or a list of homology groups is insufficient for these steps.
