# Root investigation of the remaining E2 step

## Countable compactness versus nonsingularity

The essential-ultrapower construction in E.12 gives a countable finitely solvable unsolvable system over every residual simple group with pointwise finite normal-generation norm. Its arbitrary multiplication-table relations can have dependent exponent rows. Introducing variables y_i=r_i makes rows independent only by removing the original constraints; adding y_i=1 restores the dependencies. A relative homology equivalence of actual two-dimensional models needs an integral boundary isomorphism, not simply a larger presentation.

A possible refinement is an essential countable subgroup P' of an ultrapower with relative H_1 and H_2 zero. Its existence has not been proved. The normal closure of the diagonal perfect group is perfect, but conjugators witnessing essentiality in the whole ultrapower need not lie in that normal closure. This is a precise obstruction to combining the two closures inside one countable subgroup.

## Nested commutator ranges

For a nonabelian simple E and a≠1, put S_a={v[a,u]v^-1:u,v in E}. This is a conjugation-invariant generating subset containing 1. For h,a≠1 the word [h,[a,t]b] is nonconstant: otherwise all [a,t] would centralize a conjugate of h, contrary to their normal generation. Thus the first nested substitution can preserve nonconstancy.

The needed induction is stronger: after arbitrarily many translated substitutions, the remaining root word must still be nonconstant and its image must omit the next enumerated element. The property that AA^-1 generates E is insufficient, because a generating set may lie in a transporter of a small subgroup into a proper subgroup. A bound on N_E(c) controls expressions for c, not lengths of conjugators modulo C_E(c). The conjugation orbit map can have unbounded inverse distortion. No general nested-range lemma is claimed.

## Metrizable topology

A29 gives a nondiscrete Hausdorff metrizable group topology. A countable such group is not complete, so a completion supplies points outside the group. Translating this into a countable acyclic equation system is unresolved. Linear recurrences x_i=a_i x_(i+1) retain a nonzero relative homology class. Replacing child exponents by zero using commutators gives an identity exponent matrix, but arbitrary conjugators destroy the uniform small-neighborhood control needed for the metric diagonal. A nondiscrete invariant metric is already excluded by A30; it must not be inferred from ordinary metrizability.

## Literature distinctions

Ershov's 1979 algebraic compactness paper uses a purity condition defined through maps into finite groups. Its profinite-retract theorem is not a theorem about arbitrary finitely solvable group equation systems and is not used. Berrick's displayed sequential construction does not by itself prove the asserted two-dimensional geometric dimension for arbitrary uncountable perfect locally free groups; the countable grope construction and its free products are safe. Recent simple-envelope constructions that preserve geometric dimension add uncontrolled H_2 and therefore do not give the required rank-one example.

These are research records, not claims of nonexistence or independence. The smallest remaining case is exactly E2 with H_2=Z and bound ℵ₀.
