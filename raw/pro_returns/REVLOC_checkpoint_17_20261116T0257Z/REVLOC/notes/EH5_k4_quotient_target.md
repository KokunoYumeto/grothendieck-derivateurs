## 2. A top James--Hopf variation cannot disappear just because of tau

thirteen-connected, pi14G=Z2, pi15G=Z8{c}+Z2{u}, and the eta

    Sigma5(R/R8) ~= S14_W wedge M(Z2,14)_V.

Sigma5R8 has dimension thirteen and G is thirteen-connected. The
factor need not be unique, which does not affect the following test.
The projected Sigma5tau is in pi15 of the displayed quotient. That
group is Z2 plus Z2, generated by eta on the two bottom14 classes:
the Moore pinch has no free lift in this degree because its integral
degree-two map is injective. There are no Whitehead products in range.
Therefore every composite

    S15 --Sigma5tau--> Sigma5R -> G

is zero, by the bottom-eta vanishing in G. The stable map exact

## 4. An integral target edge test

The simple reader's actual coefficient comparison gives an integral
class D16 on L with 2D16=-b8 squared, and

    beta_Z Theta15 = q*D16,

where q:L''=L<10>->L. Fourfold cohomology suspension gives the
corresponding identity for mu11 on V. If q*D16=0, mu11 has an
integral lift. Every positive integral homology group of V is torsion,
since V is a finite-type primary connected cover. Such an integral
cohomology class evaluates zero on every image of H11(Q;Z).
Therefore q*D16=0 would exclude the required mu-one correction.
This is a sufficient target test, not an established vanishing.

There is an exact base-image reformulation. In the actual fibration

    L'' -> L -> P9L,

the base is six-connected and the fiber nine-connected. Integral
cohomology has no positive mixed Serre terms through total degree
sixteen: a positive base degree is at least seven and a positive
fiber degree at least ten. Thus in degree sixteen the kernel of
restriction to L'' is exactly the image of the scalar base edge.
Consequently

    q*D16=0  iff  D16 belongs to im[H16(P9L;Z)->H16(L;Z)].

Possible differentials into the scalar base line only quotient that
edge image and do not alter this equivalence. This gives a finite
integral Postnikov-cohomology question. No value of that image has
been inferred from the mod-two calculation of D.2.42.

If q*D16 is nonzero, the coefficient sequence and the torsion
universal-coefficient theorem do supply an integral H11(V) class
detected by mu. They do not supply a map Q->V realizing that class;
the exact tau-null lower-map and filling problem remains.
