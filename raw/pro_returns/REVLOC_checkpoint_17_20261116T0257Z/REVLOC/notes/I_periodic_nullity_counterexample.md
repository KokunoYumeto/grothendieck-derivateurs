# Ordinary A_r-nullity is not preserved by the composite K(2)-periodic reflection

**Status:** proved from D.2.10, D.2.11 and the opened Heuts generator/reflection theorems. This is a failed-route note, not a new numbered claim.

**Attribution:** GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI / ChatGPT Pro).

## Precise scope

Fix a prime p and any r>=0. Put

    B = K(Fp,2),  A_r = Q Sigma^r B,
    I_p = loc(Sigma-infinity B).

Use Heuts's finite-space localization L_i^f, with connectivity parameters d_2<=d_3, and his full ordinary-space realizations of S_(v_2) and M_(K(2)). On the full subcategory

    D = { d_3-connected pointed p-local X : L_1^f X = * },

there is an actual reflection into M_(K(2)), obtained by first applying L_2^f and then the K(2)-local reflection of periodic Lie algebras. Denote this composite by R_2. It comes with an ordinary map X -> R_2 X and the ordinary mapping adjunction against every realized object of M_(K(2)).

There exists a finite simply connected p-primary suspension V in D such that

    Map_*(A_r,V) = *,       R_2 V != *,
    Map_*(A_r,R_2 V) != *.

Thus this periodic reflection does not preserve ordinary A_r-nullity, even on finite simply connected targets of arbitrarily large connectivity, for the specific finite-type p-torsion infinite-loop sources A_r under study.

This does **not** separately prove that L_2^f alone fails to preserve nullity, or that the K(2)-Lie reflection alone fails on already L_2^f-local, A_r-null inputs. It proves failure of their explicit composite, so a blanket commutation argument through both steps is invalid. No telescope conjecture is used.

## 1. A finite target with exactly the required hypotheses

Choose the finite Moore spectrum M=S/p, of chromatic type one. Hopkins--Smith Theorem 9 supplies a positive-degree v_1 self-map. Take a power so that it is a K(1)-isomorphism and its K(2)-homology map is zero; this is also explicit in their Lemma 3.2 and Corollary 3.3. Its finite cofiber F has

    K(0)_*F = K(1)_*F = 0,       K(2)_*F != 0.

The last assertion follows from the cofiber exact sequence and the zero K(2)-map between two nonzero shifts of K(2)_*M. Hence F has type exactly two. Its integral reduced homology is p-primary: both the source and target of the self-map are shifts of the Moore spectrum.

After sufficiently many suspensions, represent this stable self-map by an actual map between simply connected finite Moore spaces. This uses the defining stabilization colimit for maps between finite CW complexes. Suspend once more, if necessary, so that the representing map itself is a suspension. Its ordinary mapping cone V is then a suspension, its suspension spectrum is a shift of F, and V is a finite simply connected p-primary CW complex. Further suspensions make V d_3-connected, or as highly connected as desired. All these operations preserve type two.

The integral V is already p-local: it is simply connected and its reduced integral homology is p-primary, so its p-localization map is an integral homology equivalence and hence a weak equivalence. Alternatively one can work throughout with its p-localization, exactly as in Heuts's convention; the original finite model suffices for the nullity argument below.

## 2. V is A_r-null in ordinary spaces

The Snaith splitting expresses Sigma-infinity A_r as extended powers of Sigma^r Sigma-infinity B, including that spectrum as its weight-one summand. The stable localizing class I_p is a tensor ideal and is closed under homotopy orbits, so Sigma-infinity A_r belongs to I_p.

D.2.6 proves I_p is contained in the stable sphere annihilator: F(E,S)=0 for every E in I_p. Therefore D.2.10, applied to source A_r and finite simply connected target V, gives

    Map_*(A_r,V) = *.

This is the full derived pointed mapping space, not merely its set of components. The same V works simultaneously for every r>=0.

## 3. The periodic reflection of V is nonzero

Heuts Proposition 3.14 states that for a finite d_(n+1)-connected type-n suspension V, L_n^f V belongs to S_(v_n) and is a compact generator. Its proof uses Lemma 3.16: the classification of finite suspension nullity classes gives L_(n-1)^f V=*. Thus at n=2 our V belongs to D and

    G = L_2^f V

is a compact generator of S_(v_2).

Here generator can be used in its colimit sense. In a presentable category the colimit-closed full subcategory generated by a small set has a right adjoint to its inclusion; conservativity of mapping out of the stated generator makes the counit an equivalence. This is also the precise argument used in the proof of Heuts Proposition 3.14, pp.14--15 of the opened author version. Consequently G generates S_(v_2) under small colimits.

Transport G across Heuts Theorem 2.6 to an object L_G of Lie(Sp_(T(2))). Proposition 4.26 gives a reflection

    q : Lie(Sp_(T(2))) -> Lie(Sp_(K(2)))

whose underlying-spectrum operation is K(2)-localization. Being a left adjoint, q preserves colimits. If q(L_G) were zero, then q would be zero on every Lie algebra, because L_G colimit-generates. Equivalently, for the nonzero trivial K(2)-local Lie algebra T on the K(2)-local sphere, adjunction would give Map(L_G,T)=Map(q(L_G),T)=*. Since L_G is a generator this would force T=0, a contradiction. Thus

    q(L_G) != 0.

Let Z be its fully faithful ordinary-space realization in M_(K(2)), as in Heuts Corollary 2.8 and Section 4.5. Then Z is noncontractible.

The ordinary maps

    V -> G -> Z

are the coaugmentations of the two stated reflections. For an arbitrary X in D, Proposition 3.6 preserves the required d_3-connectivity, and L_1^f L_2^f X=L_1^f X=* makes L_2^f X an object of S_(v_2). Nullification adjunction followed by the Lie reflection adjunction therefore proves the claimed ordinary reflection R_2:D->M_(K(2)). In particular Z=R_2 V. The monochromatic functor on arbitrary spaces has not been silently treated as a reflection.

## 4. Nullity is destroyed

D.2.11(b) says that an ordinary A_r-null target realized in M_(K(2)) must be contractible. The target Z above is noncontractible. Consequently

    Map_*(A_r,Z) != *.

Together with Section 2, this gives the asserted counterexample. Noncontractibility of this mapping space need not occur in pi_0; the claim is the failure of full derived mapping-space nullity.

## 5. Why the restricted I_p question remains open

The finite nonzero spectrum Sigma-infinity V is not in I_p, because D.2.6 gives zero finite part for I_p. Thus this counterexample does not settle the restricted assertion

    Sigma-infinity Y in I_p and Y A_r-null
       implies R_2 Y A_r-null,

even when Y lies in D.

That restriction is relevant to the intended target Y_0=Sigma^(r+1)B and its ordinary nullification P_(A_r)Y_0. The cofiber of Y_0 -> P_(A_r)Y_0 is A_r-acyclic; its suspension spectrum lies in loc(Sigma-infinity A_r)=I_p. Since Sigma-infinity Y_0 also lies in I_p, the cofiber sequence shows Sigma-infinity P_(A_r)Y_0 lies in I_p. Therefore both the proposed source target and its nullification remain in the restricted stable ideal. No preservation theorem for that restricted class has been proved here.
