## Exact statement

Let G=BZ/p, let C be connected and ordinarily G-acyclic, and let
M=M(Z/p,1)=cofib(p:S1->S1). Set X=G smash C. Then

    P_X Sigma²(M smash C) = *,  P_X Sigma4 C = *,
    minRev(G smash C) = minRev(M smash C) = minRev(C).

More generally minRev(G smash C)=minRev(C) for every C in the
reversible null class generated by G: apply the ordinary case to
Sigma C, which is ordinarily G-acyclic by D.2.7 with r=1.

This excludes the proposed tensoring of the BZ/p/Moore distinction
with EVERY connected ordinary BZ/p-acyclic factor, not only the GEM
case D.2.37. It does not settle the Q Sigma K(Fp,2) nullification test.

## 1. The mapping-space construction and exact variance

For every X-null Z, Map(C,Z) is G-null. Since C is G-acyclic,
Map(C smash C,Z)=Map(C,Map(C,Z))=*. Every higher smash power is
therefore X-acyclic. The quotient C^r/(vee_r C) has a finite filtration
whose successive quotients are wedges of the powers C^smash j, j>=2;
mapping-space fibrations prove its X-acyclicity.

Choose a simplicial group H equivalent to Omega Z. Restriction

    Map(C^r,H) -> Map(C,H)^r

is a Kan fibration of simplicial groups with contractible zero fiber.
Every tuple extends by ordered multiplication along its coordinates.
Translation identifies all other fibers with the zero fiber. This is
therefore an equivalence of entire mapping spaces, not just of zero
components. The repair from D.2.37 remains essential.

For a pointed finite set S put F(S)=Map(C^(S-minus-basepoint),H).
A pointed map f:S->T induces coordinate pullback C^T->C^S, inserting
the basepoint when f sends a coordinate to the basepoint. Precomposition
therefore gives a COVARIANT Fin_*-functor F. Equivalently it is
contravariant on Segal's original Gamma category. The Segal projections
S->1_* induce exactly the coordinate restrictions above. The fold
2_*->1_* induces the diagonal C->C². The composite of its induced
mapping map with the ordered-product inverse to the Segal equivalence
is pointwise multiplication in H. Thus pi0 F(1_*) is a group, and F
is very special.

Segal's iterated classifying-space construction provides a connective
spectrum E with

    K=Map(C,Omega Z) ~= Omega-infinity E.

There is no Fp-linear theory for an arbitrary C and no GEM conclusion.
The group-like Gamma-space recognition is exactly the needed weaker
replacement. It applies regardless of components or cardinality of K.

## 2. Spectral arithmetic lemma with all limit bounds

If K=Omega-infinity E is BZ/p-null, with E connective, then pi_jK is
p-inverted for j>=2.

Proof. W=Sigma-infinity BZ/p is 1-connective and satisfies Bousfield's
1-supported p-torsion condition. By adjunction, E is W-null in the
mapping-SPACE sense. Write W_i=Sigma^-i W and E_i=P_(W_i)^Sp E for
i>=0. Thus E_0=E. The maps go E_i -> E_(i+1). ULP Theorem2.6 gives

    fib(E_i -> E_(i+1)) = Sigma^-i H G_i

for p-torsion groups G_i. Extension closure gives, for j>i,

    fib(E_i -> E_j) in Sp_{<=-i}.

Filtered colimits of spectra are exact and preserve these homotopy-group
bounds. For E_inf=colim_i E_i,

    F_i=fib(E_i -> E_inf) in Sp_{<=-i}.

To prove that E_inf is fully W-null, FIX i and use E_(i+1), rather
than E_i. The relevant fiber F_(i+1) lies in degrees <=-i-1; its
suspension lies in degrees <=-i. In contrast W_i lies in degrees
>=1-i. Thus BOTH

    Map(W_i,F_(i+1))=*,
    Map(W_i,Sigma F_(i+1))=*

by t-structure orthogonality. The map

    Map(W_i,E_(i+1)) -> Map(W_i,E_inf)

is therefore an equivalence including components. Its source is
contractible because W_i=Sigma W_(i+1). Hence E_inf is null for every
integer shift of W. This argument never invokes compactness of W or
commutation of its mapping functor with a filtered colimit.

The established Kahn--Priddy calculation gives loc(W)=loc(S/p).
Right orthogonality then gives F(S/p,E_inf)=0. This function spectrum
is the fiber of p:E_inf->E_inf, so E_inf is p-inverted. Since F_0 is
<=0, E=E_0 -> E_inf is an isomorphism on pi_j for j>=2. This proves
the lemma. The boundary j=1 is deliberately not included: K(Z,1)
is BZ/p-null but has non-p-inverted pi1.

## 3. Moore mapping test and suspension count

K=Map(C,Omega Z) is G-null because Map(G,K)=Map(X,Omega Z)=*.
The preceding lemma makes p an isomorphism on pi_jK for j>=2.
Thus p on Omega²K is an equivalence on EVERY component and every
homotopy group. The Moore cofibration gives

    Map(M,Omega K) = fib(p:Omega²K -> Omega²K) = *.

Finally Omega K=Map(Sigma²C,Z), so this is precisely

    Map(Sigma²(M smash C),Z)=*.

Desuspension gives M smash C in minRev(X). There is no discarded
pi0 or pi1 assertion hidden in this step.

## 4. The elementary telescope strengthens the conclusion

For ANY connected D with p-primary reduced integral homology, put
T=Sigma D and let f=p smash id_D:T->T, using the degree-p circle
map. The telescope has zero reduced integral homology and is simply
connected, hence contractible. Let D_r=Cof(f^r). The genuine
cofiber-of-composition pushout supplies homotopy cofiber sequences

    D_r -> D_(r+1) -> D_1,  D_1=M smash D.

Thus each D_r is ordinarily D_1-acyclic. The map of arrows uses
the identity on the source T and f on the target T. Consequently

    hocolim_r Cof(f^r) = Cof(T -> tel f) = Sigma²D.

This proves ordinary (M smash D)-acyclicity of Sigma²D, and therefore
minRev(M smash D)=minRev(D). No unstable octahedral axiom is assumed:
the indicated cofiber sequence is the direct pasting law for homotopy
pushouts, which is valid in pointed spaces.

Our ordinarily G-acyclic C has p-primary reduced homology by the
HZ[1/p]-local-target test already proved in D.2.7. Suspending the entire
preceding cofiber construction twice builds Sigma4C from
Sigma²(M smash C), already X-acyclic by Section3. Hence P_X Sigma4C=*,
which proves the stronger minRev(X)=minRev(C). The opposite inclusion
is immediate from smash closure. Finally apply this result to Sigma C
whenever C is in minRev(G), using D.2.7, to obtain the stated generality.

Root independently suggested this same telescope finish. It avoids
an unnecessary James-construction plus p-power H-space telescope and
Milnor-limit argument, which would also give a bound but introduces
more interfaces than are needed.

## 5. Optional independent reverse ordinary inclusion

G is ordinarily M-acyclic. Here is a direct proof independent of the
Gamma construction. If V is M-null, the Moore mapping fibration says
that the pth-power map Omega V -> Omega V has contractible fiber at
the identity. Hence pi1V has no element of order p, and p is an
isomorphism on pi_jV for every j>=2. All maps BG->Bpi1V are therefore
constant; the pointed mapping space into that aspherical target is
the discrete Hom(Cp,pi1V), which is a point.

The universal cover Vtilde has p-inverted homotopy groups. Finite
group cohomology H^j(Cp;A)=0 for j>0 when p is invertible on A.
Induction up the Postnikov tower therefore gives
Map(G,P_q Vtilde)=* for every q. Mapping spaces preserve the inverse
limit, and spaces are Postnikov complete, so Map(G,Vtilde)=*.
The covering fibration now gives Map(G,V)=*.

For an (M smash C)-null target Z, Map(C,Z) is M-null. Apply the
preceding result and exponential adjunction to obtain
Map(G smash C,Z)=*. Thus G smash C is ordinarily (M smash C)-acyclic,
proving the reverse reversible inclusion. This proof uses no compactness
of BG and does not assume away phantom maps; the target Postnikov
mapping spaces themselves are all contractible.

## 7. Reversible meet consequence and its exact limit

For arbitrary pointed C and G=BZ/p, the following identity follows
from D.2.39 without a new numbered claim:

    Rev(G smash C) = Rev(G) intersection Rev(C).

The forward inclusion is smash closure. For the reverse inclusion,
take an aspherical D in both classes. D.2.39 gives
Rev(G smash D)=Rev(D). Smashing every pointed homotopy colimit,
cofiber sequence, retract, suspension and desuspension used to
generate D from C with G preserves the corresponding construction.
Equivalently, the inverse image of a reversible aspherical class
under G smash - is such a class. Hence G smash D belongs to
Rev(G smash C), and then so does D. The cofiber criterion identifies
the localizers from their aspherical classes.

Every such tensor candidate is therefore below Rev(G), and cannot
by itself give a new interval element above Rev(G). For the main
zero-finite-part test B=K(Fp,2), A=Q Sigma B, this supplies no
inclusion G smash B in Rev(A). D.2.39 identifies the reversible
closures of G smash A and A, but does not identify them with the
closure of B. That missing unstable inclusion remains explicit.

## 8. More general collapse with an Eilenberg--Mac Lane factor

Let A be connected, suppose H_d(A;Fp) is nonzero for some d>=1,
and let B=K(Fp,n) be ordinarily A-acyclic. Then

    P_(A smash B) Sigma^(d+1) B = *,
    Rev(A smash B) = Rev(B).

Here is the complete deduction. For an (A smash B)-null target Z,
Map(B,Z) is A-null. The assumed acyclicity of B consequently makes
B smash B (A smash B)-acyclic, and the product-quotient filtration
used in D.2.37 gives every finite product-to-wedge comparison.
Using a simplicial-group model of Omega Z makes restriction an
equivalence on all components, by ordered-product extensions and
translation of fibers. The opposite theory of finite free Fp-vector
spaces acts on these product powers of B. Exactly the pointed
Badzioch/Dold--Kan argument of D.2.37 therefore makes

    K=Map(B,Omega Z)

a pointed GEM with Fp-vector-space homotopy groups. It is A-null
by exponential adjunction. If its j-th homotopy group V were
nonzero for j>=d, the retract K(V,j) would receive the nonzero
H^d(A;V)=Hom_Fp(H_d(A;Fp),V) in mapping degree j-d. This contradicts
A-nullity. Thus pi_j K=0 for all j>=d, and Omega^d K is contractible,
including components. The latter is exactly Map(Sigma^(d+1)B,Z).
Desuspension gives B in Rev(A smash B); smash closure gives the
opposite inclusion. No assertion is made when B is not ordinarily
A-acyclic.

In particular, replacing BZ/p by a highly connected infinite-loop
generator Q Sigma^m F from a finite p-primary type-h spectrum does
not rescue the tensor construction with a sufficiently high
K(Fp,n) factor. D.2.9 supplies the required ordinary acyclicity,
and the displayed argument collapses the tensor class to Rev(B).
The generic non-GEM factor case has not been reduced to this proof.
