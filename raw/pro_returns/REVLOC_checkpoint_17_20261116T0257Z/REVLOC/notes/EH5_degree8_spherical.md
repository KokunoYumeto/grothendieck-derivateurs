# The degree-eight class is unstably spherical

Established 2026-11-15, after the 16:50 assignment. This is a positive
realization statement, not an Atiyah--Hirzebruch spectral sequence
collapse assertion.

Let B=K(F_2,2), let u in H^2(B;F_2) be its fundamental class, and let
Y=Sigma^2 B and T=Omega^2 Y. In degree six, write f for the homology
class dual to u^3 and x for the class dual to (Sq^1 u)^2. There is an
actual based map

    alpha:S^8 -> Sigma^2 B

whose mod-two Hurewicz image is Sigma^2 f.

## Construction

Put M=RP^3 x RP^3. The primary embedding result of Akhmetiev,
Repovs, and Skopenkov (2001), Theorem1.1 and the proof on printed
page9, gives a smooth embedding

    M x I^2 -> R^8.

The proof explicitly states this stronger product-with-I^2 assertion,
not merely embeddability of M. Restricting to the middle slice M x {0}
therefore gives a smooth codimension-two embedding with a specified
trivialization of its normal two-plane bundle. Indeed the derivative
of the I^2 directions projects to a frame for the normal quotient.
Equivalently one can restrict the construction to a small open disk
in the interior of I^2, so corners have no role.

Let r,s in H^1(M;F_2) be the degree-one generators of the two factors.
Choose g:M->B representing the cohomology class rs. The
Pontryagin--Thom collapse for the framed embedding, followed by the
map on the Thom space induced by g, is

    S^8 -> Th(epsilon^2_M)=S^2 smash M_+
        -> S^2 smash B = Sigma^2 B.

Here M_+->B is g on M and sends the disjoint basepoint to the chosen
basepoint of B. This is an actual unstable map with precisely two
suspensions.

Under the Thom isomorphism, its Hurewicz image is the double
suspension of g_*[M]. The two basis evaluations are

    <g^*(u^3),[M]> = <r^3 s^3,[M]> = 1,
    g^*((Sq^1 u)^2) = (r^2s+rs^2)^2
                    = r^4s^2+r^2s^4 = 0.

Thus g_*[M]=f and alpha has the claimed Hurewicz image. No framing of
M in a larger Euclidean space is being substituted for a rank-two
normal framing; the embedding theorem supplies the latter directly.

## Consequence for the double-loop primitive

The manuscript's degree-six calculation gives

    Prim H_6(T;F_2) = < b^2, {a,b}, p_3 >,
    p_3=f+ac+a^3,

and the simultaneous primary-square and second-Bockstein conditions
leave precisely the one-dimensional subspace generated by

    z=b^2+{a,b}+p_3.

Let alpha-hat:S^6->T be the double adjoint of the constructed alpha.
Its Hurewicz image is primitive and satisfies those necessary
conditions because it comes from a sphere. Naturality of double
homology suspension identifies its image in H_8(Y) with the
Hurewicz image of alpha, namely Sigma^2 f. In particular it is
nonzero and has f coefficient one. Therefore

    (alpha-hat)_*[S^6] = b^2+{a,b}+f+ac+a^3.

The only input from the earlier loop-homology calculation is its
already computed uniqueness statement; the present construction
independently settles existence.

The source's exact construction uses Brown's product embedding lemma,
the existence of an immersion R x I -> R^4 for a closed orientable
3-manifold R, and a framed embedding R x I^2 -> R^5. The final
paragraph of the embedding proof explicitly gives
R_1 x ... x R_r x I^2 -> R^(3r+2). Setting r=2 gives the required
rank-two framed embedding directly.
