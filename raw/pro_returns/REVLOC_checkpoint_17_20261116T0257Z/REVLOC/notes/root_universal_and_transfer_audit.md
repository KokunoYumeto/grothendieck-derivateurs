## A gap found and its necessary repair

The first draft identified that specified cofiber-map composite with
the actual Whitehead composite merely from w-jc=h p8 and p8 l4=0.
Root rejected that inference: precomposition by l4 need not preserve
addition of maps out of the Moore space. The mixed Hilton term occurs
in exactly the dimension being studied.

The correction can be evaluated rather than assumed away. Write
P8=Sigma P7. Through dimension14 the Hilton--Milnor decomposition for
P8 wedge P8 has only its two singleton factors and the mixed factor
Sigma(P7 smash P7); all factors of weight three start in dimension19.
After the second map factors through p8, the mixed factor factors
through Sigma(P7 smash S7)=P15. Its pi14 is Z/2 on the bottom sphere.
Thus the only possible correction is a multiple of [alpha,h], where
alpha=[u,u] and u is the order-two bottom class in pi4(P5).
Graded Jacobi gives

    [[u,u],h] = 2[u,[u,h]] = [2u,[u,h]] = 0.

This justifies additivity for this particular composite; it does not
assert a general Moore-space additivity rule. Both other readers
independently checked the factor dimensions and the sign. The corrected
final research note must retain this argument and the history of the
gap. The remaining bottom-sphere element and its image in Y have still
not been evaluated.
