**Status: complete component argument independently passed by root,
axioms_derivators, and simple_higher, 15 November 2026.** The input homology and operation tables are the
independently audited `FGH_D2_four_connected_cover.md`, including BOTH
third Bocksteins beta3(B5)=beta3(B6)=N. The two even-nu attachment
coefficients below are retained as parameters. No value of the actual
Browder square is inferred from an abstract group calculation.

Let E=F<5>. Write r=D+E6=e6+f6 for its degree-six cycle. All spectra
and groups below are localized at two. A finite stable cell model of
Sigma-infinity E is chosen through dimension eleven before cancelling
odd-unit pairs, so retaining its ten-skeleton computes pi9 correctly.
The cells and bases are the ones in the cover note.

## 1. The actual cover and the stability comparison

There is no need to assume a cohomology class lifts to a loop map.
Write F=Omega²Z, where Z=fib(Y -> K(F2,4)) and Y=Sigma²K(F2,2).
The space Z is five-connected and pi6Z=Z2. The first Postnikov map
Z -> K(F2,6) double-loops to F -> K(F2,4). Its fiber E is therefore
a four-connected double-loop space. The unit E -> Omega Sigma E
has a homotopy retraction by its loop-space counit. Freudenthal makes
it nine-connected, hence it is an isomorphism on pi9. Since Sigma E
is five-connected, pi10 Sigma E is already stable. Consequently

    pi9T = pi9F = pi9E = pi9(Sigma-infinity E).

This does not identify the original Browder square with a stable
kernel of F; it simply makes the actual degree-nine group computable
in a different, sufficiently connected, suspension spectrum.

## 2. The stable seven- and eight-skeleta

Let M=M(Z4,5), with bottom i5 and top d6. Put t=i5 eta5.
The seven-skeleton is D0 vee S7_R, where

    D0=(M vee S6_r) union_(t+2r) e7_S.

Equivalently D0 is the cofiber of the stable matrix

    (4 eta; 0 2): S5 vee S6 -> S5 vee S6.

The degree-six Moore top is D; the other mod-two six-class is
E6=D+r. The two seven-class operations beta(S)=r and Sq2(S)=u5
give exactly the displayed attachment. There are no Whitehead
components in this stable model.

Let b in pi7M be the order-two lift of eta on the Moore top,
and let c=r eta6. Then

    pi7(D0 vee S7_R)=Z R + Z2 b + Z2 c.

The complete eight-attachment list, in the cover basis, is

    W:0, D8:b+c, J1:b, J2:0, J3:2R+b.

The two torsion components are independently detected by Sq2 with
vectors D and r. The integral component is fixed by beta(J3)=R.
Changing the D8-cell by subtracting the J1-cell, and the J3-cell by
subtracting the J1-cell, gives the exact stable splitting

    L8 = K8 vee M(Z2,7)_R vee S8_W vee S8_J2,
    K8 = D0 union_b e8_B union_c e8_C.

Here B=J1 and C=D8-J1. These are actual stable cofiber row changes,
not a claim about a minimal unstable CW structure.

## 3. Exact pi9 of the core, with no unknown coextension order

First, pi9M=Z4{m}, where the top quotient sends m to 2nu6.
The sphere component r nu6 will be denoted z. The full matrix
cofiber sequence injects the quotient of pi9D0 by Z2{z} into

    Z8{nu5} + Z2{eta6²},

with image the kernel of (x,y) |-> 4x+4y in Z8. This kernel is
cyclic of order eight generated by (nu5,eta6²). Choose a lift s0.
The relation 2s0=m follows from this quotient and the primary
fourth-square detector: a possible discrepancy is z; both 2s0 and
m have zero fourth-square detector, while z has detector r.
Likewise 8s0=0: its only possible nonzero value would be z, again
excluded by the fourth-square detector of an even multiple. Thus

    pi9D0=Z8{s0} + Z2{z},  2s0=m.

For clarity, fourth-square detection here is a stable cofiber
operation in a range where the lower skeleton has no degree-ten
cell; it is additive and vanishes on twice any attaching map.
Its value on z is the ordinary Hopf-invariant-one detector of nu.
The lift s0 may be changed by z to normalize its fourth-square
vector to D; this does not change 2s0=m.

The b and c eight-cells have eta-squared composites

    b eta7²=2m=4s0,   c eta7²=4z=0.

The cofiber exact sequence has no extra eta-lift term: on the
relevant pi8(S7) summands, b eta and c eta are independent before
those cells are attached. Hence

    pi9K8=Z4{s}+Z2{z},

where s is the image of s0 and still has Sq4 vector D, whereas z
has vector r. Adding the other three summands gives exactly

    pi9L8=Z4{s}+Z2{z}+Z4{tau}+Z2{w eta}+Z2{j eta}.       (1)

The class tau is the Moore eta coextension for M(Z2,7), so
2tau=R eta7². Its Sq2 vector is J1+J3 and its Sq4 vector is zero.
The last two classes are the eta maps on the W and J2 eight-spheres.

Here and below the basic sphere and Moore identities are the opened

pi1S=Z2 eta, pi2S=Z2 eta², pi3S_(2)=Z8 nu,

## 4. The eta composites of the free degree-eight lifts

This is needed to pass from (1) to the ninth skeleton without an
unmeasured even-nu term. Set J=M union_b e8_B. The exact sequence
gives pi9J=Z2{m-bar}. Its quotient by S5 is Ceta with bottom S6_d.
There is a free e in pi8J of top degree two on B. In that quotient,
e is the ordinary top-degree-two Ceta class, so

    e eta8=m-bar.

Indeed eta_(Ceta)=i nu q sends that top-degree-two class to 2i nu,
which is precisely the nonzero image of m-bar. Thus in L8,

    e eta8=2s.                                         (2)

The other free lift, with Hurewicz 2C=2(D8-J1), can be chosen in
the actual Ceta subcomplex on r and C. Its eta composite is
2r nu=2z=0 in L8. The W and J2 free lifts have composites w eta
and j eta, respectively.

The degree-eight torsion of L8 is Z4{n=i5 nu5} plus Z2{ell=R eta7}.
The free Hurewicz lattice is generated by W,J2,2J1,2(D8-J1).
The nine-cell list supplies all four integral boundaries. Its
zero-boundary combinations N and Ksum=K2+K3+K4 kill n and ell.
On composing the nine-attachments with eta, n eta=0, ell eta=2tau,
and (2) gives 2s. Possible torsion additions to the integral
attachments contribute only 2tau, already killed. Therefore the
image of (1) in pi9L9 is exactly the elementary group

    V=(Z2){s,z,tau,w eta,j eta}.                        (3)

The quotient pi9L9/V is free of rank four, the kernel of the full
nine-dimensional attaching matrix. Thus V is the whole torsion
subgroup, not merely a subgroup detected by primary operations.

Its complete detector table is

| Class | Sq2 vector in H8 | Sq4 vector in H6 |
|---|---|---|
| s | 0 | D |
| z | 0 | r |
| tau | J1+J3 | 0 |

All five vectors are independent, so no invisible torsion survives

## 5. Retaining the two even-nu parameters

Normalize the zero-boundary nine-cells as follows. Replace N by
N'=N-Ksum and replace the torsion basis n,ell by an odd multiple
of n and ell plus an even multiple of n. Then N' attaches as n
and Q=Ksum attaches as ell. These changes preserve the order-eight
integral class modulo order-two summands and preserve the detector
table above. The other two zero-boundary cycles have attachments

    K1:2a n,   K5:2b n,  a,b in {0,1}.                 (4)

The primary tables alone do not determine a,b. In particular a
mod-two spherical representative of K1 or K5 could have integral
Hurewicz K_i-2N, and therefore does not justify setting the bit to
zero. The generalized Browder construction of a spherical K5 is
compatible with either bit.

Choose integral order-two cycles K1,K5,Q using the half-boundaries
of B3,B7,B4, respectively. Choose N' as the order-eight cycle,
as in the audited configuration-chain calculation. The free
Hurewicz lattice of pi9L9 then has basis

    h(p)=4N',  h(q)=2Q,
    h(x)=K1-2aN',  h(y)=K5-2bN'.                       (5)

Choose p and q so that both their second- and fourth-square
detectors vanish. This is possible and unique modulo (3): for
either p or q the Adem relation Sq2 Sq2=Sq3 Sq1 forces the
Sq2 vector into <W,J2,J1+J3>, since beta(Sq3(top))=0.
For q, Sq3(top)=R; for p it is zero. Its Sq4 vector is any element
of <D,r>. The five entries of (3) therefore remove all five
coefficients. An even multiple of any attaching map has zero
second- and fourth-square detectors, a consequence of its factorization
through the degree-two sphere map and naturality of cofibers.

## 6. All tenth-cell relations, including the second beta3=N

The exact integral normalization uses the known group
H9E=Z8{N'} plus Z2{K1,K5,Q}. A first-Bockstein cycle whose third
Bockstein is zero can be made an exact zero-boundary tenth-cell:
its remaining N' coefficient is divisible by sixteen and can be
removed by twice an eight-N' cell; its order-two coefficients are
removed by even multiples of the corresponding degree-two cells.
These changes preserve all primary detectors. Similarly B5 and
B6 may each be normalized to boundary 8N'; odd units do not change
the resulting two-primary relation.

The zero-boundary cells Z2, B2, and T10 have the exact relations

    w eta=0,  j eta=0,  w eta+s+z=0.                  (6)

The two eight-N' cells give, by (5) and the primary-normalized p,

    B5: 2p+tau+s=0,
    B6: 2p+j eta=0.                                  (7)

Consequently 2p=0 and tau=z=s. The other zero-boundary cycle
B1+B3 repeats j eta; U2,Pr,B8 have all-zero detectors and give no
new relation, by the completeness of (3). The beta3-zero combination
B5+B6 repeats tau+s+j eta. Thus exactly one nonzero element of V
survives, represented by s; no primary-undetected extra component
has been suppressed.

The remaining integral cells have boundaries 2Q,2K1,2K5. In the
lattice (5), these are q, 2x+a p, 2y+b p. Their exact relations
are

    B4: q+tau+s+j eta=0,
    B3: 2x+a p=0,
    B7: 2y+b p=0.                                    (8)

Here q was primary-normalized, and 2x,2y have zero primary
detectors. B1 differs from B3 by the already killed j eta.
Equations (6)--(8) give the complete presentation

    pi9E = <p,x,y,s | 2p=2s=0, 2x=a p, 2y=b p>.     (9)

The sign of the two last relations is immaterial since 2p=0.
The calculation used all twelve tenth-cells and all independent
integral boundary directions. No group-order assumption is used
to substitute for an attaching map.

It follows independently of the unknown bits that

    4 pi9E=0,
    ker(h_Z:pi9E -> H9E)=Z2{s},  s != 0.             (10)

The integral Hurewicz map is exactly (5), with h(s)=0; its image
has order eight in either case. The group is (Z2)^4 if a=b=0,
and Z4 plus (Z2)^2 otherwise. These alternatives are retained;
the abstract isomorphism type has not been selected.

## 7. The eta variation and the remaining actual square

The already computed pi8E is Z2 squared, represented by the W and
J2 eight-sphere classes. Their eta composites are precisely the
two classes killed in (6). Therefore the actual unstable relation is

    pi8T composed with eta8 = 0.                      (11)

This rules out that particular proposed variation of a canonical
eight-cell filling. It does not prove that all possible changes of
the earlier map have been exhausted.

The surviving class has the actual representative s=r nu6=rho nu6:
the r sphere maps to an odd multiple of the generator rho of pi6T=Z4,
and odd multiples agree after composition since s has order two.
The integral map H9E -> H9F is injective: its mod-two reduction is
injective, so an integral kernel lies in 2H9E=2Z8{N}; the N generator
maps with odd coefficient on the unique order-eight generator of
H9F and hence no nonzero multiple of it vanishes. The splitting
F -> T therefore identifies the same integral Hurewicz kernel for T.

The actual Browder square lambda(chi,chi) has mod-two Hurewicz zero.
If its integral Hurewicz is also proved zero with the integral
normalization of A checked, (10) reduces it to 0 or s. The present
note does not choose between these values. In particular neither
the tentative Moore antecedent nor the split double-unit map of F
is used to identify it. Since (9) has exponent at most four, any
four-divisibility test in the actual group is a vanishing test.

A possible direct detector of s on a stable mapping cone is the
functional combination Sq4(D*+r*)+Sq2(J1*). It evaluates as one on

annihilates all relations (6)--(8). Its value on the actual Browder
