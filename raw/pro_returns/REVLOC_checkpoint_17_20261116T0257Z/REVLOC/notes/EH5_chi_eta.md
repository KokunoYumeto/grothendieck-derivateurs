# The actual chi--eta composition and bottom Moore extensions

**Status, 15 November 2026: the chi--eta composition is proved zero.**
This is a direct low-degree consequence of D.2.18. It does not move
an unstable calculation in Y outside the stable range: the calculation
is made on the bottom sphere of the three-connected double-loop fiber F.
The further Moore-extension classification below is a concrete interface
for the independently owned once-suspended source analysis.

## 1. Actual vanishing, with the shifts explicit

Let Z=fib(Y->K(F2,4)), W=Omega Z, F=Omega W=Omega²Z. The bottom
generator chi_F in pi4F is the order-two class with mod-two Hurewicz
A4. Write chi_W in pi5W and gamma_Y in pi6Y for its adjoints.

D.2.18i gives the finite stable six-skeleton

    C vee M(Z/4,5) vee S6,
    C=cofib((2,eta4):S4 vee S5 -> S4),

and identifies the bottom S4 of C with the stabilization of chi_F.
Consequently its composite with eta4 is zero stably. Since F is
three-connected, stabilization on pi5F is an isomorphism by Freudenthal.
Thus

    chi_F eta4=0,    chi_W eta5=0,    gamma_Y eta6=0.

The second and third equalities use actual adjunction and the identities
eta5=Sigma eta4, eta6=Sigma²eta4. This is distinct from an attempted
calculation of a degree-six free lift in an unrelated unstable Chang
complex inside Y. That latter route is unnecessary here.

## 2. An order-two reference Moore extension

Write alpha in pi6W=Z/4 and rho in pi7W=Z/4 for the shifted
generators of D.2.18, so alpha eta6=2rho. Let

    M2=M(Z/2,5),     M4=M(Z/4,5).

Because 2chi_W=0 there is an extension t:M2->W. The stable Moore
identity 2id_M2=i5 eta5 q6 gives

    2t=chi_W eta5 q6=0.

The identity is in the actual range here. One can recover it directly
from the cofiber exact sequence and the opened Table 1 of Pengcheng
Li, *The homotopy classification of based maps between A_n^2-complexes*,
arXiv:2008.03049v3, printed p.8: End(M2)=Z/4{id}; its restriction to
the bottom Z/2 is reduction modulo two, and its nonzero kernel is
generated by i5 eta5 q6. Thus twice the identity equals that generator.

Let d:M4->M2 have bottom degree one and top degree two, as in the
same paper's equation (3.2), printed p.7. Then

    v0=t d:M4->W

extends chi_W, has order two, and has zero image on the mod-two
top class. The last assertion follows from the even top degree of d.

All maps and homotopies in this subsection are in a stable mapping
range: W is four-connected and M2,M4 have dimension six. Hence
composition is additive and the cofiber mapping sequence is an
exact sequence of abelian groups. In particular

    [M4,W] = Z/2{v0} direct-sum Z/4{alpha q6}.

Indeed the cofiber sequence gives an extension of pi5W=Z/2 by
pi6W/4pi6W=Z/4; v0 supplies a splitting. The mod-two Hurewicz
image of alpha is the nonzero degree-six bottom class u6, since
the degree-five Hurewicz image of alpha in F is xi1(a) and this
class has nonzero homology suspension. Therefore the maps with
bottom chi_W and zero mod-two top image are exactly

    v0,  v0+2alpha q6.

## 3. Their exact eta-coextension composite

Let tilde_eta4:S7->M4 be an eta-coextension with q6 tilde_eta4=eta6.
The composite d tilde_eta4 has pinch 2eta6=0 in pi7S6. The ordinary
cofiber exact sequence in this stable range therefore places it in

    im(pi7S5 -> pi7M2)=<i5 eta5 eta6>.

The map t kills that image because chi_W eta5=0. Consequently
v0 tilde_eta4=0. Replacing v0 by v0+2alpha q6 changes this by

    2alpha eta6=4rho=0.

Thus EVERY extension of chi_W across M4 having zero mod-two top
image kills tilde_eta4. More generally, for either bottom value
and mod-two top coefficient epsilon on u6, the coextension image is

    v tilde_eta4=epsilon(2rho).

This includes the full Moore-extension indeterminacy; no choice
of the eta coextension changes the result, since two choices
differ by the bottom eta-squared map, killed by chi_W eta5=0.
It is a low-stage target fact, not an extension of a map from all of A.
