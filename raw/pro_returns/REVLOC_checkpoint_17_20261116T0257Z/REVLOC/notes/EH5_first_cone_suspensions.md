# First-cone suspension exponents three, four, and five

**Status, 15 November 2026:** the exponent-three and exponent-five
vanishing arguments below are complete and submitted for independent
audit. The exponent-four calculation is an exact restriction, not a
vanishing theorem. An earlier provisional exponent-four argument used
the wrong Moore top-cell basis and has been explicitly withdrawn.
No simultaneous-attachment or iterated-nullification conclusion is
claimed here.

Put B=K(F2,2), A=Q(Sigma B), Y=Sigma²B, T=Omega²Y and
F=fib(T->B). All homology in the coefficient arguments is mod two.
For h:Sigma^k A->Y with k>=2, take the twice-adjoint

    G:Sigma^(k-2) A -> T.

Its source is simply connected with H²=0, so it lifts to F. The
fiber inclusion is injective on homology because T≈B×F. Use

    d6=b², e6=b²+k, f6=p3,
    k={a,b}, p3=f+ac+a³, r=e6+f6.

The k coefficient on H6T is the e6 coefficient in the displayed
fiber basis. On homology suspension into Omega Y, k becomes the
old tensor commutator, d6 disappears, and p3 becomes the old linear
f class. These are the two separate functionals relevant to a
quadratic first-cone boundary.

The exact target identity, already independently audited on every
one of the eighteen degree-ten fiber classes, is

    Psi_F=Sq4(e6*)+Sq2(J1*)=0,  J1={a,d}.

## 1. Exponent three: the commutator coefficient is identically zero

Here G:Sigma A->F. The only degree-six source homology class is
the suspended unit C=Sigma²c, with c dual to u² in H4B. Restrict G
to the actual unit Sigma²B. Write f in H6B for the class dual to
u³, and i in H8B for the class dual to u⁴. Set

    C6=Sigma²c, F8=Sigma²f, I10=Sigma²i.

Serre's polynomial presentation, Cartan, and suspension give

    Sq_*4 I10=C6,  Sq_*2 I10=F8,
    beta F8=0,    Sq_*2 F8=0.

For example Sq2(u³)=u⁴+uv², so the u⁴-dual has second square f;
Sq4(u²)=u⁴ gives the fourth square. The class F8 is primitive since
its source is a suspension.

Use the complete degree-eight F basis W,C8,L,M,J1,J2,J3. Only C8
is nonprimitive, with reduced diagonal A4 tensor A4. The relevant
operations are

| class | beta | Sq_*2 |
|---|---|---|
| W | 0 | 0 |
| L | Q | d6 |
| M | Q | f6 |
| J1 | 0 | d6+e6 |
| J2 | 0 | 0 |
| J3 | R | d6+e6 |

Thus a primitive first-Bockstein cycle has form

    w W + j2 J2 + t(L+M) + j1 J1.

If its second square also vanishes, independence of d6,e6,f6 in

    t(d6+f6)+j1(d6+e6)

forces t=j1=0. Therefore G(F8) has zero J1 coefficient. Evaluating
Psi_F on G(I10) now gives

    coefficient_e6 G(C6)=coefficient_J1 G(F8)=0.

This proves zero commutator coefficient on the entire relevant
degree-six homology group of Sigma A, not just on the kernel of an
individual attaching homology map. It therefore introduces no
cross-cancellation ambiguity when considered together with other
attachments having the same zero functional.

## 2. Exponent five and all larger exponents

For k=5, G:Sigma³A->T has bottom homotopy group Z/2 in degree six.
The actual target group is pi6T=Z/4{rho}, and h_F2(rho)=r. A
homomorphism Z/2->Z/4 has image contained in {0,2rho}; both elements
have zero mod-two Hurewicz. Hence G is zero on its whole degree-six
homology group, and in particular has zero commutator functional.
For k>=6, Sigma^(k-2)A has no degree-six homology at all.

## 3. Exponent four: the corrected exact Moore interface

For k=4, G:Sigma²A->T restricts on its bottom Moore complex
M(Z/2,5). The bottom map S5->T is either zero or 2 alphaBar,
since pi5T=Z/4{alphaBar}. Let t in {0,1} record this choice.

The actual map mu:M(Z/4,5)->T constructed in D.2.17 extends
alphaBar and has top mod-two Hurewicz image exactly k. The cellular
map

    M(Z/2,5) -> M(Z/4,5)

of bottom degree two and top degree one realizes the bottom map
2 alphaBar and sends the source top class to k. The chain condition
is 2*2=4*1, so this is an actual Moore map, not just a homology
homomorphism. Use the zero map when t=0.

All other extensions of the same bottom map differ by the top-cell
action of pi6T. Since its mod-two Hurewicz image is generated by r,
the full range for the degree-six top class is

    G(Sigma² B0)=t k+epsilon r,  t,epsilon in {0,1}.   (1)

Consequently its commutator and linear-f coefficients are

    commutator = t+epsilon,   linear f = epsilon.     (2)

The case t=1,epsilon=0 is still dangerous for an individual
cofiber. The case t=0,epsilon=1 has equal nonzero linear and
commutator coefficients; it is harmless for that individual cone,
but could still participate in a simultaneous cancellation with
another source whose linear image agrees and whose commutator
coefficient differs.

**Withdrawn shortcut.** The Moore top in the four-connected cover
was denoted D=d6+e6=k. It must not be confused with the original
fiber basis vector d6=b². Making that confusion would replace
t k in (1) by t d6 and incorrectly conclude that the two
coefficients in (2) always agree. No manuscript claim or result row
uses that withdrawn calculation.

## 4. Exact scope and primary inputs

The exponent-three proof only uses the already proved full H8F and
H10F operation tables, Serre's opened polynomial-generator theorem
and Cartan identities, and the actual fiber lift. The exponent-five
proof uses the actual pi6T=Z4 calculation of D.2.18. The exponent-four
restriction uses D.2.17's actual Moore extension and D.2.18's actual

The unresolved exponent-four question is whether a map on the full
infinite-loop source can realize either coefficient choice in (1)
that contributes a commutator. Individual finite Moore and Chang
extensions do not settle that question. Exponents zero and one and
the simultaneous cobar comparison are being checked independently
by the other agents. Even a complete first-cone theorem would still
need a separate invariant to propagate through later nullification
stages.

## 5. The four-adjunction route: a precise unproved interface

The exponent-four map also has an adjunct A->Omega²F. Because A is
two-connected, it lifts to U=Omega²E, where E=F<5>. The space U is
two-connected with pi3U=Z4{alphaBar}. The bit t in (1) means that
the bottom Z2 maps to 2alphaBar. Its mod-two Hurewicz is zero, but
the next Moore-top class in degree four can still be hit. Thus the
ordinary mod-two bottom-hit contradiction of D.2.12 does not apply
without an additional calculation.

A conceivable multiplication-deviation test would use the functional
dual to the square of this degree-four Moore-top class. It would be
useful to prove that its second square is decomposable in H*U: on the
actual smash source Sigma²(B smash B), a degree-eight class with the
required b tensor b coefficient cannot have zero second square. Its
double desuspension has v tensor v coefficient one, and the terms
w tensor v+v tensor w in its second square cannot cancel against the
other degree-six basis elements u tensor u² and u² tensor u.

That target identity has NOT been proved. U is not a free double-loop
suspension, so the product-coefficient argument used in D.2.16 cannot
be copied without excluding new indecomposable Steenrod preimages.
Moreover a mismatch of source and target Bockstein lengths by itself
does not force a coefficient to vanish: a source noncycle on one page
can map to a surviving target cycle if its differential maps to zero.
These are two separate missing steps, not an asserted obstruction.

The universal Browder map Sigma(B smash B)->T explains the dangerous
Moore pattern geometrically: its bottom is the Whitehead square
[u4,u4]=-2alpha after double adjunction, and a mixed a tensor b
class maps to k. It does not supply a map from Sigma²A to that source.
The sphere-smash map eta2 eta3 eta4 smash B has bottom
u4 eta4³=0, since u4 eta4=0, and cannot realize t=1.

The subsequently proved twofold-delooping relation in
`FGH_D2_first_cone_low_suspensions.md` does not by itself remove this
case. On the exponent-four source Sigma⁴A, the relevant degree-eight
class desuspends to the degree-four B0 functional on A. The map
Sq_*4:H8A->H4A is zero: its dual is the cup square of that degree-four
cohomology class, and every positive cup square in H*A is zero.
Consequently the surjective-witness argument that settles exponent
zero cannot detect this remaining B0 coefficient at exponent four.
The exponent-three argument is consistent with that delooped method:
I9+J9 is a Sq2-cycle with fourth square C5.

## 6. A dangerous exponent-four map exists on the entire unit

There is an actual unit map realizing t=1,epsilon=0 in (1), not
merely a finite Moore extension. Let a:S²->B->T be the bottom map
and let j:B->T be the double-suspension unit. The actual generalized
Browder operation gives

    g_unit=lambda_1(a,j):Sigma(S² smash B)=Sigma³B -> T.

Its double adjunct is the generalized Whitehead product

    h_unit=[u4,id_Y]:Sigma⁵B -> Y,

where u4:S4->Y is the bottom inclusion and
Y=Sigma²B=Sigma(Sigma B). The source of the generalized Whitehead
product is Sigma(S3 smash Sigma B)=Sigma⁵B, so all suspension
indices are explicit. On the bottom sphere the image is

    [u4,u4]=-2alpha,

by the exact Hopf--Hilton formula used in D.2.17. Its double adjunct
therefore has bottom -2alphaBar. On the primitive bottom labels a,b,

    (g_unit)_*(Sigma³a)={a,a}=0,
    (g_unit)_*(Sigma³b)={a,b}=k.

In particular Sigma³b maps to k, with no p3 term. This is the
case t=1,epsilon=0 and it realizes the dangerous pattern on every
unit skeleton. Thus no further operation calculation confined to
the unit can rule out this exponent-four case.

The displayed formula is intentionally confined to the primitive
labels. The homology of the actual based Browder family includes
convolution corrections on nonprimitive labels. In particular one
must not assert g_*(Sigma³x)={a,x} for every label: for x dual to
v² the uncorrected bracket has reduced diagonal k tensor b plus
b tensor k, whereas every suspension image is primitive. This
correction does not change either of the two bottom values used here.

Its mapping cone actually has the nonzero old cup product. Under
one homology suspension, k maps to the tensor commutator
[bar a,bar b] in H7(Omega Y); the next homology suspension kills it.
The group H8(Sigma⁵B) is one-dimensional on Sigma⁵b, so the
ordinary attaching homology map in that degree is zero. The exact
per-vector comparison of D.2.26 evaluates the product of the unique
old degree-four and degree-five cohomology lifts to one on the new
connecting class. Therefore this product is nonzero in C_(h_unit).
Both old lifts are unique in these degrees by connectivity of the
source, so there is no choice-of-lift ambiguity.

The remaining test is precisely whether this actual generalized
Whitehead map extends along

    Sigma⁴(Sigma B) -> Sigma⁴Q(Sigma B).

An extension of id_Y along Sigma(Sigma B)->Sigma A would produce
such an extension by taking its bracket with u4, but D.2.12 excludes
that stronger antecedent. It does not exclude extension of the
bracket itself. No full-A extension or nonextension is asserted.

## 7. An actual degree-eleven cover class missed by a free approximation

**Audited edge calculation, 2026-11-15.** The following is an exact
calculation in the actual target. It does not assert a computation of
the homology or operations of U=Omega²E.

In the ambient double-loop algebra put

    V11={c,x}+a{a,x}+b{b,c}+abk,

where x is the degree-six label dual to v² and k={a,b}. Then

    beta V11=B5+B6,       Sq_*4 V11=R.

Indeed beta x=d+e, beta b=a, and beta a=beta c=beta k=0.
Expanding the four Bocksteins gives precisely the definitions of
B5+B6 in the complete degree-ten table. The fourth square of the
first summand is {Sq_*2 c,Sq_*2 x}={a,c}=R; the fourth squares
of the other three summands vanish by Cartan and {a,a}=0.

The class V11 is primitive. The reduced diagonal of {c,x} is the
sum of the symmetric terms {b,c} tensor b, {a,x} tensor a,
and k tensor ab. The reduced diagonals of the other three
summands cancel these and all remaining ak tensor b and bk tensor a
terms in pairs. Since H11B has no primitive, V11 belongs to H11F
under the actual product splitting T=B times F.

It also has zero image under p:F->K(F2,4). On the two degree-eleven
indecomposable base cohomology generators, this follows as follows.
The first is x11=Sq1 x10, whose evaluation is the evaluation of
x10 on B5+B6 and hence zero. The second is y11=Sq4 y7, whose
evaluation is the evaluation of y7=(P+Q)* on R and hence zero.
Every positive cohomology product vanishes on primitive V11.
Thus V11 annihilates the entire degree-eleven part of the ideal
generated by p*H>0K(F2,4).

This annihilation really gives a lift to E=F<5>; mere vanishing
of p_* alone would not suffice. The already audited Koszul
calculation in `FGH_D2_four_connected_cover.md` proves that all
positive Eilenberg--Moore Tor groups of total degree at most ten
vanish. An incoming higher differential into filtration zero and
total degree eleven would originate in positive filtration and
total degree ten, so is zero. Filtration zero has no outgoing
differential. Consequently

    (H*F / (p*H>0K4)H*F)^11 -> H11(E)

is injective in cohomology, even if positive-filtration classes
occur in total degree eleven. Dually, H11E maps onto the
annihilator just described. Choose a lift Vtilde11 of V11.
The known injectivity of H_iE->H_iF for i<=10 gives the actual
operation identities

    beta Vtilde11=B5+B6,       Sq_*4 Vtilde11=R,
    Sq_*5 Vtilde11=D,

where D=d6+e6 is the cover's Moore-top degree-six class. The last
identity is Sq_*5=Sq_*4 beta and uses Sq_*4(B5+B6)=D.
In particular Sq5(D*) is nonzero in H11E. The lift can be taken
primitive: its reduced diagonal maps to zero in the tensor
product of lower-degree homology groups, where cover inclusion
is injective.

This provides a concrete additional indecomposable target
phenomenon. Any calculation that treats U as a free E4 algebra
on its bottom Moore cells must account for it. A deduction about
the square of the degree-four Moore-top class in H*U, or about
the Bockstein of Q7 of that class, still requires the actual
loop-space comparison and is not made here.

## 8. The actual Moore-top square vanishes in U

**Independent audit passed by the axioms agent.** The relation of
Section 7 has an exact consequence for U=Omega²E. Write (D,r) for
the degree-six cover basis with r=D+E6. Let q4,r4 in H4U have
twofold suspensions D,r, respectively. Then q4²=0 in H8U.

Here are the low-degree comparison details. The lift Vtilde11 is
primitive. In a minimal Adams--Hilton model for the four-connected
space E, the generator corresponding to Vtilde11 has degree ten
and differential of degree nine. Its only possible decomposable
differential terms have lengths two and degrees 4+5. They are
dual to the reduced diagonal, so vanish. Words of length three
start in degree twelve. Hence there is a class V10 in H10(Omega E)
whose homology suspension is Vtilde11.

Let q5,r5 denote the classes suspending to D,r. Naturality of
cohomology suspension and Sq5, together with instability in degree
five, gives

    <q5* squared,V10>=1,     <r5* squared,V10>=0.

The remaining reduced diagonal terms of V10 have splittings 4+6,
6+4, or distinct 5+5. Every class in those lower degrees is
primitive. Subtracting the corresponding Pontryagin products
removes all these symmetric cross terms, leaving a representative
with reduced diagonal exactly q5 tensor q5. In a minimal
Adams--Hilton model for Omega E, its degree-nine generator
therefore has differential q4². There is no cubic correction:
the smallest loop-generator degree is three, so length three
starts in degree nine, above this differential's degree eight.
This proves the asserted actual square relation.

Consequently the tentative q4² coefficient functional in Section 5
does not exist. This closes that particular multiplication-deviation
strategy; it does not show that an ordinary full-source extension
exists or that a different deviation invariant cannot work.
