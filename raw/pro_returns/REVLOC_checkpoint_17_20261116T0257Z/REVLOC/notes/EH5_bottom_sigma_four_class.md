    a7, s9=Sq2(a), n11=Sq4(a), z13=Sq4 Sq2(a);

Dualizing the inclusion of (1) gives a LEFT comodule quotient

    H_*(P14;F2) -> N=<a7,s9,n11,z13>.

    psi(s)=xi1² tensor a,
