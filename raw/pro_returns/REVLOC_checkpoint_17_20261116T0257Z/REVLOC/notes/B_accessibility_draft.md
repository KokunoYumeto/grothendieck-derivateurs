# B: bounded-subcomplex construction (working proof)

Let N be a weak-equivalence-invariant class of pointed simplicial sets containing the point, closed under pointed homotopy colimits and all three two-out-of-three implications in pointed homotopy cofiber sequences. Assume also N is closed under desuspension (this follows from the third cofiber implication). Fix any regular uncountable kappa.

Define N_kappa by: every sub-simplicial set with fewer than kappa nondegenerate simplices is contained in such a sub-simplicial set belonging to N. This condition itself implies membership in N, because the poset of small N subobjects is kappa-filtered and its union is the whole object. Every small member of N is in N_kappa.

Key prospective proof, with each point requiring full paper treatment:

1. Weak equivalence invariance: after geometric realization, a CW equivalence has cellular homotopy inverse. Given a small subcomplex of Y, alternately close small subcomplexes in X and Y under the maps and their homotopies, enlarging the X-side to a small N object each time. At countable union the restricted maps are mutually inverse up to the restricted homotopies. N closure under sequential homotopy colimits gives X_infinity in N; hence Y_infinity in N. Regular uncountable kappa keeps all these subcomplexes small. Need compare simplicial-set and CW versions; alternatively use simplicial fibrant replacement and cylinder diagrams entirely in sSet.

2. Pointed hocolim closure: use cellular simplicial replacement of a strict diagram I→CW_*. A small subcomplex of its realization meets fewer than kappa objects/arrows/cells. Generate a small subcategory J. For each object of J choose small N subcomplexes containing the chosen cells; alternate enlargement to contain all images under all arrows of J and enlargement to N. At countable union obtain a J-subdiagram of small N objects. Its hocolim is small, belongs to N, and is a subcomplex of the original bar construction containing the given cells. This proves N_kappa closure under arbitrary pointed hocolims.

3. Cofiber two-out-of-three: model as a CW/simplicial subcomplex X⊂Y with quotient C=Y/X. Any small Y-subcomplex can be enlarged compatibly with chosen small X- and C-subcomplexes: lift the noncollapsed cells of a small C-subcomplex and include their boundaries; include any prescribed small X-subcomplex. For a chosen pair of vertices in N_kappa, alternately enlarge in those two vertices to small N subcomplexes and restore compatibility. At countable union, both chosen vertices are N and the third is N by original cofiber two-out-of-three. This works in all three cases, including Y,C⇒X. Needs explicit bookkeeping that the quotient at the limit equals the union of chosen C-subcomplexes and the intersection equals the union of chosen X-subcomplexes.

4. Desuspension has an even simpler separate proof: ΣX0 ⊂ K0 ⊂ ΣX1 ⊂ K1 ⊂ ... with every Ki small and in N. At union ΣX∞=union Ki is N; so X∞ is N. This treats the missing step in the proposal explicitly.

5. Retracts: obtained from hocolim closure via the idempotent telescope, using weak-equivalence invariance.

6. W_kappa={f:C(f) in N_kappa} is reversible fundamental by the general cofiber-null argument (arrow retracts, octahedron, pointed hocolim and relative Theorem A). It contains every small-cofiber N arrow.

7. Accessibility needs ARROWS, not merely object-to-point maps. Let S_kappa be representatives of all maps of <kappa-cell complexes with cofiber in N. Every f∈W_kappa is a filtered union of small cofibration subarrows f_i with C(f_i)∈N: choose a small N subcomplex of the quotient C(f), lift its noncollapsed cells to the target, and include the required source cells. Such arrows form a kappa-filtered cofinal system. Hence every fundamental localizer containing S_kappa contains f, by closure under homotopy colimits. Conversely W_kappa is fundamental and contains S_kappa. Therefore W_kappa is the accessible fundamental localizer generated by S_kappa.

If sound, apply with N null for all 2-local spheres and kappa=aleph_1. It contains RP∞ but excludes RP². Kahn–Priddy/Snaith gives the non-RT-fixed conclusion. This construction avoids requiring a common presentability rank for an iteration of localizations.

Important distinction: it would be incorrect to claim W_kappa is generated by the maps A→* alone. Those generate the proper nullification with the same aspherical class, not generally the reversible cofiber class. The required generator set consists of all small ARROWS whose cofibers are in N.
