# D.3 size question: a nonrepresentable-homology route does not transfer

Root opened Carles Casacuberta, Depth and simplicity of Ohkawa's
argument, the author-hosted primary PDF, Section7, Theorem7.1 and
Corollary7.2, printed preprint pages11--12:
https://www.ub.edu/topologia/casacuberta/articles/Nagoya_CC.pdf

For uncountable alpha the paper uses the exact group functor
F_alpha(A)=A^alpha/(functions with image cardinality<alpha),
composed with ordinary reduced homology. Its acyclic class contains
all spaces with fewer thanalpha cells, but not the wedge ofalpha
circles. The resulting theories have a proper class of different
acyclic classes. Their failure to preserve arbitrary coproducts is
essential and is stated explicitly in the paper.

This does not give a reversible fundamental localizer: each circle
is acyclic for this theory, but their alpha-fold wedge is not.
The aspherical objects of a fundamental localizer are closed under
that pointed homotopy colimit. Moreover reversible closure of even
one circle-to-point map is total, by desuspension to S0. Thus taking
reversible closures collapses this entire proposed cardinal family
to the same total class. No set-size result at the prescribed stable
part follows. This check is confined to this exact new route; it
makes no assertion that all possible cardinal constructions fail.
