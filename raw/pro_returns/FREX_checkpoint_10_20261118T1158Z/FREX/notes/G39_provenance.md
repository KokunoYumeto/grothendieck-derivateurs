# Superseded first amalgamation obstruction

The initial G39 draft used i=(ab,acb,acc), j=(ACB,AB,ACC). Both image triples are bifix and both group maps are isomorphisms. The source word z^-1 y x^-1 z maps to c and C^-1, and the first two length equations give length(c)+length(C)=0. This already excludes an injective locally-free amalgam, but its positive abelian cones are incompatible.

Before integration or the next checkpoint, this draft was replaced by the stronger cone-compatible example now in G.39: i=(bacb,bcacb,ccacb), j=(BABC,BCABC,CCABC). The identical determinant-one matrices identify the entire positive cones. The exact script/output and integrated theorem use only that stronger example. The original proof is retained here as provenance; it is not a separate numbered result.
