## Liping Li, orbit categories

The 2026 Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra article *On representations
of permutation groups and their orbit categories*, volume230 issue4,
article108241, DOI10.1016/j.jpaa.2026.108241, is the published version
of the already audited arXiv:2510.15348v1, 17 October2025. The public
publisher search text was retrieved from
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022404926000721
(turn671224278search0); direct opening returned403. The complete
primary arXiv PDF was reopened at https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.15348
(turn290758917view0).

The relevant full-body results remain Theorem3.6 (restriction along
the dense/orbit-equivalent subgroup) and Theorem4.4 (the opposite age
and point-stabilizer orbit category under strong amalgamation).
They provide a smooth-module/orbit-category dictionary, not a theorem
that arbitrary smooth coinvariants are exact. The Noetherian result
in arXiv Theorem1.5, renamed Corollary1.6 in the published introduction,
assumes a highly homogeneous action and polynomial coefficients k[Omega].
It is not Noetherianity for every oligomorphic group's permutation
modules and does not settle C.4's bounded-exponent saturation gate.

There is a real version difference to retain in citations. The
arXiv introduction calls the age-to-orbit functor unconditionally
fully faithful, which is too broad as recorded in the earlier source
audit. The published introduction's Remark1.5 instead calls it
faithful and essentially surjective before strong amalgamation.
The latter wording removes that particular overstatement. The public
publisher preview is not being treated as a read full proof of every
published theorem.

## M. Rovinsky, injectivity of the trivial representation

The complete primary PDF is https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.3265,
*On semilinear representations of the infinite symmetric group*,
arXiv:1405.3265v5, 16 August2015. Open references:
turn286059644view0 and turn175334002view1. The statement sometimes
indexed as Lemma2.1 in older versions is **Lemma3.1 in v5**, printed
page5. Its statement and entire proof were read.

It assumes a basis B of open subgroups such that, for every V<=U
in B, N_G(V)/V is finite and acts transitively on (G/U)^V; equivalently

    (G/U)^V = (N_G(V) U)/U.

For a Q-algebra R, essential smooth extensions of a trivial R-module
remain trivial. Consequently a trivial R-module that is injective
over R is injective among smooth R[G]-modules. The proof averages an
augmentation-zero vector over the finite normalizer quotient; the
transitivity hypothesis makes that average zero. In particular, with
R=Q the hypothesis implies rational coinvariant exactness, because
Hom_G(-,Q)=Hom_Q((-) _G,Q) and Q-duality is exact and detects exactness.

This is a valid sufficient criterion, but it does not apply to the
ordered rigid classes at the remaining gate. Under the fixed-point
identity and rigidity, N_G(G_B)=G_B. If P strongly embeds in Q in two
different ways, then (G/G_P)^{G_Q} has at least two points, while the
normalizer quotient of G_Q is trivial. Thus its required transitivity
fails already for that pair of support stabilizers. No replacement
of the hypothesis by mere oligomorphicity or amenability is stated
or justified here. The main rational and total all-prime questions
therefore remain unchanged.
