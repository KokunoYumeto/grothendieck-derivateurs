# Primary-source checks in progress

These entries record opened sources, not imported new conclusions. The available-source audit is integrated in notes/F_source_audit.tex and sections/F.tex; three original bodies remain unavailable.

- Kechris--Rosendal, arXiv math/0409567v4, Proposition6.4, pp31--32: statement and full proof read: EPPA is equivalent to an increasing compact chain with dense union. The forward proof uses comeager tuples generating relatively compact groups; the reverse takes finite compact-group orbits of a finite substructure. URL https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0409567 .
- Zucker, Amenability and unique ergodicity of automorphism groups of Fraisse structures, FundMath226(2014),41--61, DOI10.4064/fm226-1-3. Primary publisher PDF opened; Theorem3.1 gives signed-coefficient obstruction to a positive invariant measure on a companion expansion; Theorem1.2 is the GL infinite-vector-space counterexample to measure support on generic orbit. This is a2014 paper, not2016. URL https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/88659 .
- Conant, An axiomatic approach to free amalgamation, current arXiv1505.00762v5(2023corrigenda), Theorem5.6: free amalgamation theory has elimination of hyperimaginaries and weak elimination of imaginaries. Section7 corrections do not state a correction to5.6. Exact hypothesis must be retained; this is not automatically the ordered strong-class assertion. URL https://arxiv.org/pdf/1505.00762 .
- Evans--Ghadernezhad--Tent, arXiv1312.3430, Theorem4.15: for the specified nonnegative uncollapsed predimensions, Aut(M0/cl^d(empty)) is simple in ranges r=2,n>m or r>=3,n>=m. The paper uses a closure topology in part of its general argument and must not be silently transferred to the usual finite-stabilizer topology. Source opened; full relevant stabilizer/imaginary/closure-text search completed; the general St/Op statement was not identified. URL https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.3430 .

Theorem2.7 concerns complete and countably categorical geometric theories. The equivalence concerns atomic sheaves, not all covariant diagrams or their derived colimits. URL https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.2778 .
