# Independent mathematical review of B.10–B.12

The review found no defect in the stated proofs. The following are the points most likely to invalidate the conclusions if omitted; each is present in the working text checked on 2026-11-11.

## B.10: controlled acyclic simple overgroups

The displayed word w in the free product is cyclically reduced of length eight. Cyclic reduction of wh leaves length eight when hg is nonidentity and length six when hg is the identity. Thus both associated cyclic subgroups in the HNN step are infinite cyclic, including when g itself has finite order. The HNN embedding theorem applies and preserves the original group. The relation puts h in the normal closure of g, so countably repeated simultaneous steps produce a simple envelope of the claimed cardinality.

In the acyclic envelope, the two axis maps and the diagonal map are conjugate. The field Künneth argument kills degree n after a preceding map has killed all positive lower degrees; iterating this correctly kills each fixed positive degree. Vanishing over Q and every Fp forces integral homology to vanish: the integral groups are torsion by the rational test, and the next-degree universal coefficient sequence eliminates p-torsion for every p. Alternating simple and acyclic envelopes preserves embeddings and cardinality, and each required normal-closure or finite-cycle argument occurs at a later appropriate stage.

## B.11: the Kan–Thurston construction with the cardinal bound

The construction makes its simplex choices once, so T(L) really is a CW subspace of T(K) for every subcomplex L. The inductive subcomplex-injectivity proof is needed; asphericity alone would not imply it. The intermediate space V, followed by the remaining mapping-cylinder attachments, supplies exactly this missing implication. The graph-of-spaces normal form applies because both edge homomorphisms are injective. It handles disconnected lower skeleta componentwise. At an infinite stage, spheres and homotopies lie in finite CW subcomplexes, so the assertions pass to the union.

The homology argument is with every local system on the target, not only with constant coefficients. Its restriction to each simplex is constant, so the relative pair comparison is justified by acyclicity of the cone group and induction on the boundary. The normal-closure computation identifies the kernel of the specified map on pi1: dimension-two cones impose the actual boundary relators, and higher-dimensional boundaries introduce no additional quotient relations because their groups are already normally generated by lower cone groups. This also proves that the kernel is perfect.

The size bound is uniform: at most lambda simplices, at most lambda cells in each bar construction, and countably many dimensions preserve lambda. Thus the small-target homomorphism argument is valid in the stated universe. A larger target group is deliberately not covered.

## B.12: ind-representability and the one-type conclusion

The identification of the localized presheaf with the filtered colimit of homotopy-category representables is justified by left Kan extension and the Yoneda universal property. It would not be valid merely to replace each stage by its homotopy-invariant version without that argument. The universe enlargement stated in the proof makes this Kan extension legitimate.

The cardinal bound is available because the index category and all its categories belong to the same universe. The quotient groups give an actual isomorphism of ordinary functor categories, including their noninvertible natural transformations, before taking components. This avoids the invalid exchange of components with an infinite product. B.9 gives stagewise injections on homotopy classes; filtered colimits preserve these injections, so the elementary cancellation in B.12.5 is justified.

Finally, the constructed inverse to the specified ind-unit is proved on both sides using the exact ind-Hom formula and orthogonality. It is not merely an unspecified isomorphism to some groupoid-valued ind-object, and the original index category is retained.
