\begin{abstract}
Total asphericity does not imply exact abelian colimits. We specify
a category of finite linearly ordered $3$-uniform hypergraphs with
positive predimension $2|V|-3|E|$ and strict closed embeddings.
An alternating-amalgam argument classifies the open stabilizers of
its homogeneous limit. Explicit homotopy comparisons then prove
that every component of every covariant category of elements is
weakly contractible, without restricting the action. Nevertheless,
the three point arrows into one hyperedge have no affine equalizer
over any nonzero coefficient ring; a column cokernel of projective
dimension one has first derived colimit equal to that ring.
We also prove that, for every admissible repaired
Ad\'amek--Koubek--Reiterman construction, the first binary second
homology class survives every stage. All three inclusions from
filtered to exact to total to quasi-filtered categories are therefore
strict. We prove or correct all nine algebraic and homotopical leads,
state the consequent manuscript corrections, and record the exact
scope of the literature audit. No priority is claimed.
\end{abstract}
