                                              Abstract
         Total asphericity does not imply exact abelian colimits. We specify a category of finite
     linearly ordered 3-uniform hypergraphs with positive predimension 2|V | − 3|E| and strict
     closed embeddings. An alternating-amalgam argument classifies the open stabilizers of
     its homogeneous limit. Explicit homotopy comparisons then prove that every component
     of every covariant category of elements is weakly contractible, without restricting the
     action. Nevertheless, the three point arrows into one hyperedge have no affine equalizer
     over any nonzero coefficient ring; a column cokernel of projective dimension one has first
     derived colimit equal to that ring. We also prove that, for every admissible repaired
     Adámek–Koubek–Reiterman construction, the first binary second homology class survives
     every stage. All three inclusions from filtered to exact to total to quasi-filtered categories
     are therefore strict. We prove or correct all nine algebraic and homotopical leads, state
     the consequent manuscript corrections, and record the exact scope of the literature audit.
     No priority is claimed.

A.3    Proof and construction routes investigated
The accompanying report records the precise scope of each attempted route. The model-
category observation that totality makes ordinary simplicial-set colimits homotopy invariant
does not by itself identify the underlying set colimit with the abelian-group colimit. A free
abelian cotriple resolution has permutation modules as its terms, but some face maps send a
basis vector to a linear combination; it is not the linearization of a simplicial set diagram. We
therefore do not infer acyclicity of its colimit complex from totality. The row/column formulas
below identify the nonzero simultaneous-intersection obstruction in the separator, and explain
why these proof-side routes cannot imply the proposed universal vanishing.

A.4    Ramsey classes and integral affine equalizers

Corollary A.8 (Proved: ordered finite graphs are exact). Let G have one representative of
each finite linearly ordered simple graph as objects and increasing induced graph embeddings as
arrows, composed as functions. Then G has exact colimits in abelian groups. In particular it is
not a counterexample to totality implying exactness.

Proof. The category is small, has finite hom-sets, and has common successors: take the disjoint
union of two graphs and concatenate their orders. Finite simple graphs with an arbitrary
linear order have the Ramsey property by (Hubička and Nešetřil, 2019, Corollary 4.3, printed
p. 51). The source defines embeddings by preservation and reflection of relations (printed
p. 6), so these are induced embeddings in the graph case. Because a finite linear order is rigid,
an isomorphic induced copy of p is specified by a unique embedding from p; hence its Ramsey
property for copies is exactly the morphism Ramsey property used in Theorem A.6. That
theorem applies.
