\begin{corollary}[Refuted: the triple implication, Part B.1]\label{B:triple-question}
In the total category $\mathcal C_\delta$ of Theorem~\ref{A:main},
the three ordinary point arrows $v_1,v_2,v_3:V\to W$ have no
affine equalizer over any nonzero unital ring. In particular, for
integral coefficients,
\[
 T_1\!\left(\operatorname{coker}
  \left(P_W\longrightarrow P_V^2,\quad
   t\longmapsto(t(v_2-v_1),t(v_3-v_1))\right)\right)\cong\Z.
\]
Three is the smallest possible obstructing ordinary family size
in a total category.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Totality and the explicit obstruction are
Theorem~\ref{A:predimension-total} and
Proposition~\ref{A:predimension-triple}. Every ordinary pair has
an affine equalizer in a total category by
Lemma~\ref{B:row-audit} and condition I. Thus the triple is minimal.
The algebraic identities below remain valid; the example realizes
their column obstruction while totality annihilates the row
obstruction.
\end{proof}
