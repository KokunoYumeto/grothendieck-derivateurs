\begin{theorem}[Proved: universal repaired AKR survival, Part C.1]\label{C:survival-question}
For the repaired AKR construction and every admissible choice of its
parameters and targets,
\[
 H_2\!\left(\El_{K^*}
 \bigl(K^*(V,-)/(cv_1\sim cv_2)\bigr);\Z\right)\ne0.
\]
Every attachment preserves the whole old colimit of the binary cycle
functor. In particular, the first-stage class
$\kappa_+-\kappa_-$ survives the infinite union, and every such
category $K^*$ is not total.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{C:universal-components} proves that every actual arrow
is injective on binary graph components. Its simultaneous induction
uses the prescribed C.1 bounds only: component intersections of
size at least two descend to an actual commuting span with older
source, and component paths have at most one more vertex than the
point-path bound. The pointwise equalizer descends to one actual
arrow image by Proposition~\ref{C:point-equalizer-image}. Hence its
graph meets each source component in a connected set, and the
exact fixed-path kernel of Proposition~\ref{C:fixed-path-kernel}
vanishes. Corollary~\ref{C:universal-nontotal} proves the stated
first-stage calculation and passage to the union.
\end{proof}
The earlier selected large-target proof is retained as a separate
geometric argument and an explicit deterministic witness. The
universal proof does not impose its additional diameter or ordering
bounds. The all-coefficient triple obstruction in
Proposition~\ref{C:akr-all-rings} is a separate nonexactness result.
