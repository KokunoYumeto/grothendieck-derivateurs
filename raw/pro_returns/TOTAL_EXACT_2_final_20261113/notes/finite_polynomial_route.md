## Opened sources and exact scope

1. **Djament, Pirashvili, and Vespa (2016), _Cohomologie des foncteurs polynomiaux sur les groupes libres_, Documenta Mathematica 21, 205–222.** Full publisher PDF opened at https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/26312?nt=1 (web references `turn61view3`, `turn63view2`, `turn64view1`, `turn64view2`). Theorem 1 compares Ext between polynomial functors on finite-rank free groups in the bounded-degree and full functor categories. Lemma 2.3 and Proposition 2.4 give the group-ring tensor/bar description of augmentation powers for free groups. Proposition 3.5 is vanishing against polynomial target functors, under its stated resolution and torsion-freeness hypotheses. These concern a particular functor category and polynomial targets; they do not assert that restriction along an arbitrary diagram of groups makes an augmentation power colimit-acyclic. Using its bar resolution here leaves precisely the homology of the colimit complex to be proved zero. No downloaded local copy was created in this follow-up.

2. **Pirashvili, _Kan extension and stable homology of Eilenberg and Mac Lane spaces_, MPI preprint 94-143 (published in Topology 35, 1996, 883–886).** Full author preprint opened at https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/4178/1/preprint_1994_143.pdf (`turn61view0`). The unnumbered opening theorem identifies homology of the cubical construction with stable homology of Eilenberg–MacLane spaces; Lemma 4.1 and Section 5 supply the Kan-extension/projective-resolution proof. This confirms that the chain terms arise from powers of the underlying set, but supplies no contraction of their colimit over an arbitrary total category. The latter would be the unresolved derived-colimit calculation. Local copy not downloaded.

3. **Pirashvili (2002), _Polynomial functors over finite fields_, Astérisque 276, Bourbaki exposé 877, 369–388.** Full author exposition opened at https://numdam.org/item/SB_1999-2000__42__369_0.pdf (`turn61view2`). Proposition 1.1 and the following rational discussion, printed p.371, describe the homogeneous decomposition for functors of finite polynomial degree. The decomposition does not assert that the uncompleted group-algebra functor is a union of polynomial subfunctors, nor that taking its polynomial quotient preserves the relevant acyclicity. No such extension was used. Local copy not downloaded.

4. **Pirashvili (2007), _Hochschild homology, Frobenius homomorphism and Mac Lane homology_, Algebraic & Geometric Topology 7, 1071–1079.** Full publisher PDF opened at https://msp.org/agt/2007/7-2/agt-v7-n2-p18-p.pdf (`turn60view2`). Lemma 1 and Corollary 2 concern multiplication and vanishing homotopy of simplicial rings satisfying a power identity; Theorem 5 concerns Frobenius-twisted Hochschild homology. The required simplicial-ring structure is not supplied by an ordinary Set-colimit of module diagrams unless compatibility of multiplication has been established. Therefore these results were not applied to that ordinary colimit. Local copy not downloaded.

5. **Djament (2026), _Note sur la linéarisation des groupes abéliens finis_, Graduate Journal of Mathematics 11(1), 35–52.** Full publisher PDF opened at https://gradmath.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GJM2026-Djament.pdf (`turn67view0`). Theorem 2, printed p.37, concerns linearization of additive functors with finite abelian values of exponent invertible in the coefficient ring. Its invertibility hypothesis excludes using it on nonzero F_p-vector spaces with coefficients F_p; finite-value hypotheses exclude rational vector spaces. It therefore does not settle either needed same-coefficient case. Local copy not downloaded.
