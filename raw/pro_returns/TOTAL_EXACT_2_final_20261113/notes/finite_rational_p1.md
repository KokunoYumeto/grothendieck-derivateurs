# A rational obstruction to direct resolving-class membership

AI-produced by GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI, ChatGPT Pro mode), 13 November 2026. No priority claim. The complete statement and proof are in `sections/rational_p1_obstruction.tex`, labelled `B:P1-rational`.

## Proven result and precise scope

The special triple-column module Q_a need not lie in the rational analogue of P1's resolving class, even when the indexing category is filtered. This is a new obstruction independent of objectwise integral torsion. It does not refute totality ⇒ exactness or the option of replacing Q_a by another module with the same T_1; the example has T_i(Q_a)=0 for all i>0 because it has a terminal object.

## The full closure invariant

At an object v with automorphism σ, require

    ker(M(σ)^(2n) − 2^(2n) Id) = 0    for every n≥1.

Every rational permutation module satisfies this finite-support eigenvalue condition. It survives all P1 operations: extensions, kernels of epimorphisms, arbitrary sums, summands and filtered colimits. It also survives tensoring with any permutation diagram. For the latter, decompose the permuted basis into orbits of σ^(2n). An infinite orbit cannot support a nonzero finite-support eigenvector; a finite orbit of length r turns the coefficient equation into M(σ)^(2nr)m=2^(2nr)m, excluded by the old invariant.

The first informal version used only injectivity of M(σ)+2. The parent correctly identified that this weaker condition need not survive tensoring with a permutation module. The final theorem uses all even powers and includes the complete orbit argument. No claim based on the weaker invariant is retained as a proof.

## The category and column matrix

There are four objects p,q,v,t, with t terminal. End(v) is the infinite cyclic group generated by σ; other endomorphisms are identities. The nonterminal arrow families are

    p→q: a0,a1,a2;
    q→v: h_(j,n), j=1,2, n∈Z;
    p→v: b_n, n∈Z.

Composition by σ^m adds m to the subscript, and

    h_(1,n)(a0,a1,a2) = (b_n,b_(n+1),b_(n+2)),
    h_(2,n)(a0,a1,a2) = (b_n,b_(n+2),b_(n+1)).

All other compositions are identities or the unique terminal arrows. Associativity follows from integer addition and uniqueness of terminal arrows. The category is countable and filtered; it is not a separator candidate.

At v, with R=Q[z,z⁻¹] and z representing σ, the simultaneous-column presentation is

    D = [[z−1, z²−1], [z²−1, z−1]].

The sum/difference basis gives diagonal entries (z−1)(z+2) and −z(z−1). Therefore

    Q_a(v) ≅ R/((z−1)(z+2)) ⊕ R/(z−1).

The vector ξ=[(z−1,z−1)] is annihilated by z+2. It is nonzero because evaluation at z=−2 followed by coordinate sum annihilates D but takes ξ to −6. Thus ξ violates the full closure invariant at n=1. As a rational vector space Q_a(v) has dimension three. The other values are Q_a(p)=Q², Q_a(q)=Q⁵ and Q_a(t)=Q². Their finite dimensions do not make the full category finite: the prescribed cyclic endomorphisms and incoming orbits are infinite.

## Integral strengthening with torsion-free values

The same category and triple also obstruct membership in the original integral P1 class, despite every value being torsion free. The full even-power invariant works over Z, including tensor closure: on an infinite orbit one uses an extreme support coefficient and invertibility of σ, so no division by 2 is required.

Over Z[z,z⁻¹], D has determinant −z⁴+3z²−2z. Its leading coefficient −1 remains nonzero modulo every prime. Therefore both D and every reduction D mod p are injective over their Laurent-polynomial domains. From the free abelian presentation, Tor_1^Z(Z/p,Q_a(v))=ker(D mod p)=0, so Q_a(v) has no p-torsion for any prime and is torsion free. Its other values are Z²,Z⁵,Z². The same eigenvector is detected by evaluation at z=−2 into Z[1/2]. This proof is appended inside the existing labelled proposition, without a new numbered environment.

The program records the universally valid coefficient certificate −1, not merely tests at finitely many primes. The rational diagonalization is not used as an integral change of basis.

## Consequence for the polynomial-functor route

Rational coefficients do not remove the direct-membership obstruction in P1. A method using Mac Lane cubical or polynomial resolutions still needs either a different module with the same derived-colimit obstruction or additional structure that proves acyclicity without universal resolving-class membership. The source audit for that separate route is recorded in `finite_polynomial_route.md`; no source theorem is needed for the counterexample proved here.
