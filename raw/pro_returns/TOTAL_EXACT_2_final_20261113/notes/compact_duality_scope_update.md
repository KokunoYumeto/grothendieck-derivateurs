# Scope update for the compact/Ramsey exploration

The earlier proposed implication from totality to the equivalent compact inverse-limit and finite-pattern Ramsey properties is now **refuted**. The countable ordered predimension category is total and nonexact, while the compact/Ramsey properties imply exactness for a category with common successors. Consequently it has a contravariant diagram of nonempty compact Hausdorff spaces with empty inverse limit. The former numbered open item keeps the traceability label `CD:unresolved`, but is now a refuted corollary in `sections/compact_duality.tex`.

The separate question whether exactness implies the full compact/Ramsey property was an exploratory strengthening. It was not requested, is not decided by the present arguments, and is not needed for any requested item or any proof in the deliverable. It has therefore been removed from the numbered deliverable rather than retained as a purported remaining obligation.

The proved compact/Ramsey equivalence, its implication of exactness, the uniformly bounded finite-fibre result, and the coefficient-pinning counterexample remain unchanged. In particular the bounded-fibre result is consistent with the total counterexample: a finite-valued obstruction in a quasi-filtered category must have unbounded cardinalities across objects.
