# The complete stable degree-eight target and the actual Hurewicz test

Status: proved component calculation, 15 November 2026; full written
proof supplied here for independent audit. This incorporates the
essential additional second Bockstein
beta2(Pm+Az)=C. Omitting that relation would incorrectly produce a
Z4 stable summand. The correct stable group is (Z2)^3.

Keep the labels of D.2.18--D.2.19. Let E=Sigma-infinity F_(2), and
write L_i for its finite stable cell model through degree i. To
compute degree eight, take the finite approximation through degree
ten before cancelling odd-unit pairs, then retain its nine-skeleton.
All degree-nine homology and operation data are proved independently
in `notes/FGH_D2_H9_fiber.md`.

## 1. Exact degree-eight components before the seven-cells

The stable six-skeleton is

    L6=C vee M(Z4,5) vee S6_r,
    C=cofib((2,eta):S4 vee S5 -> S4).

Use gamma,delta,t,rho for its degree-six generators, as in D.2.18.
The quotient C -> S5 vee S6 injects pi8(C) into

    Z8{nu5}+Z2{eta6²},

with image cyclic of order four generated by (2nu5,eta6²).
Indeed the preceding term pi8(S4)=pi4(S)=0, and the following
map is (x,y) |-> 2x+eta³y in pi7(S4)=Z8. Choose omega with

    q omega=(2nu5,eta6²),  |omega|=4.

The fixed delta has q delta=(eta5,0), so

    delta eta6²=2omega.

The other two components are

    pi8 M(Z4,5)=Z4{n=u5 nu5}+Z2{b eta7},
    pi8 S6=Z2{c eta7}.

Here b is the order-two top eta-lift of D.2.19 and c=rho eta6.
The Moore exact sequence proves the first assertion: its kernel
is Z4{u5 nu5}; b eta lifts eta² and has order two. Also
t eta6²=i eta³=0, because the bottom nu has order four.
The opened Oka1986 (2.1),(2.2), p149, supplies the stable stems
and Moore coextension identity used in these direct calculations.

## 2. The seventh-skeleton extension and a precise kappa-eta relation

The four seven-cell attachments are

    V:gamma+t, P:gamma+2rho+delta, Q:delta, R:0.

Their eta²-images are 0,2omega,2omega,0. Their eta-images in
degree seven are a,b_C,b_C,0, where a=t eta and b_C=delta eta.
Consequently pi8(L7) fits into an exact sequence with left term

    G0=Z2{omegaBar}+Z4{n}+Z2{b eta}+Z2{c eta}

and right term (Z2)^2 generated by eta on P+Q and on R.
The R term has the actual order-two lift lambda eta7, because
the R-cell is freely wedged. A lift theta of the P+Q term can
be chosen in the subcomplex

    D=(C vee S6_r) union_(gamma+2rho) e7_(P+Q).

It exists since gamma eta=0 and 2rho eta=0. Since the quotient
class of theta has order two, 2theta comes from pi8(C vee S6),
so in L7 it belongs to the subgroup generated by omegaBar and
c eta. It has no n component. This exact choice of theta is
important; an abstract extension calculation alone would not
exclude an even nu contribution.

We next compute kappa eta, where kappa is the normalized free
degree-seven class of D.2.19 with Hurewicz image 2Q and Sq2
detector d6. Set C'=C union_delta e7_Q. The cofiber sequence,
delta eta²=2omega, and delta eta != 0 give

    pi8(C')=Z2{omegaBar}.

Choose kappa' in pi7(C') with top degree two on Q. Its cone
has beta(top8)=Q. Since Sq2 Q=z5 and beta z5=A4, the Adem
identities force Sq2(top8)=e6: this is the only six-class in C'.
After collapsing S4, one has

    C'/S4=Ceta(bottom5) vee S6_e.

The S6 component of kappa' is therefore eta6. It follows that
kappa' eta has nonzero eta6² component in this quotient, hence

    kappa' eta=omegaBar.

In L7, the difference kappa-kappa' is torsion. Its Sq2 vector
is d6+e6, so it is b plus possibly v; no c term is possible.
Since v eta=i nu eta=0, this proves the exact formula

    kappa eta=omegaBar+b eta.

This also checks compatibility with the earlier gamma normalization:
gamma was the unique class with quotient (0,2), and gamma eta=0
was proved in D.2.19 and the independent component note.

## 3. The whole degree-eight group of L8

The seven eight-cell attachments, in the order W,C,L,M,J1,J2,J3,
are

    0, v, kappa, kappa+b+c, b, 0, 2lambda+b.

Their eta-images kill exactly the subgroup generated by
kappa eta, b eta, c eta. By the formula just proved, this kills
omegaBar, b eta, and c eta. It also makes 2theta=0. No relation
on n has been introduced. Thus the torsion subgroup surviving
in pi8(L8) is exactly

    T=Z4{n=alphaBar nu5}+Z2{theta}+Z2{ell=lambda eta7}.

The quotient by T is free of rank five. Indeed the kernel of
the attaching map from the seven copies of pi7(S7)=Z_(2) to
pi7(L7) is free on

    W, J2, 2C, 2J1, 2(M-L).

The cofiber exact sequence splits as an extension by a free group.
More invariantly, the integral Hurewicz map has kernel exactly T
and image the following lattice in the free group H8(L8):

    L=Z_(2){W,J2}+2Z_(2){C,J1,D},  D=M-L.

In this line the letter L on the left denotes a lattice; the
cell L in D=M-L is the previously named degree-eight cell.
The generator projecting to 2C will later be denoted a0.

## 4. Every torsion component is detected for the required zero cycles

For a zero-integral-boundary nine-cell, its attaching map belongs
to T. The exact detector vectors are

    n:     Sq2=0,   Sq4=u5;
    theta: Sq2=P+Q, Sq4=z5;
    ell:   Sq2=R,   Sq4=0.

Only the parity of the coefficient of the order-four n is seen
by Sq4. That limitation is retained explicitly.

For n this is the ordinary Hopf-invariant-one cone on the Moore
bottom. For ell use the cone of eta on the R seven-sphere.
For theta, its quotient projection is eta on P+Q, proving the
second-square vector. Its chosen representative lies in D above,
which contains no u5 class, so its fourth-square u5 coefficient
is zero. The Adem identity
Sq2 Sq3=Sq5+Sq4 Sq1 in cohomology, dualized to homology,
forces its z5 coefficient to be one: beta(top9)=0,
Sq3(P+Q)=A4, and beta z5=A4. Thus the displayed detector is exact.

The H9 calculation provides genuine integral cycles

    N,    Ksum=K2+K3+K4.

Their vectors are (R,u5) and (R,0), respectively. Consequently
their actual zero-boundary attachments are of the form

    ell+(2r+1)n,    ell+2s n.

They have no theta component. Subtraction gives an odd multiple
of n, hence kills the entire order-four class n, not merely its
odd reduction. The remaining relation kills ell.

An additional second Bockstein is essential. The full first-
Bockstein cycle space in degree nine is generated by

    Au5, N, K1, K5, Ksum, Pn, Pm+Az.

The last two both have second Bockstein C. For Pm+Az, the exact
identity is

    Pm+Az=m+ag+ce+bf,
    beta(bg)=ag+bf,
    [Pm+Az]=[m]+[c][e] on E2.

The base relation beta2[e]=[c], multiplicativity, and the
base/fiber separation give beta2(Pm+Az)=C, just as for Pn.
The remaining five cycle generators have second Bockstein zero.
Thus

    Z0=Pm+Az+Pn

is a second-Bockstein cycle, with Sq2=P+Q and Sq4=z5.
Since H8(F) has exponent at most four, a parity-preserving
filtered Smith normalization makes Z0 an exact zero-integral-
boundary nine-cell: after first-boundary corrections, its
remaining C coefficient is divisible by eight and is removed
by twice the degree-four relation. Its attachment is theta
plus an even multiple of n, with no ell term. It therefore
kills theta after n and ell have been killed.

Hence ALL of T is killed by zero-integral-boundary nine-cells.
This is why neither an invisible even nu nor an unmeasured
coextension remains in the stable answer.

## 5. The free quotient without ambiguous lift choices

Let B9 be the image of the integral ninth cellular boundary in
H8(L8). Every boundary lies in the Hurewicz lattice L above.
Once T has been killed, the stable homotopy quotient is exactly

    pi8(E)=L/B9.

There is no remaining extension issue: every ninth attachment
has the prescribed image in the free quotient, and two choices
with the same integral boundary differ by T, already killed.

The independently proved homology is

    H8(F)=Z4{C}+Z2{W,J1,J2,D}.

The four order-two generators are precisely the first-Bockstein
images. The order-four generator C is fixed by the nonzero
second Bockstein. The lattice quotient L/B9 is therefore the
subgroup

    <2C,W,J2> <= H8(F),

which is (Z2)^3. Equivalently the five free relations are
2W,2J1,2J2,2D,4C; their lifts modulo T are
2w,e,2j,f,2a0. This yields the same group without selecting
any undetermined torsion correction on a free lift.

We have proved, with the actual integral Hurewicz map identified,

    pi8(Sigma-infinity F)=(Z2)^3,
    h_Z identifies it with <2C,W,J2> in H8(F),
    ker(h_F2)=Z2{a0},  h_Z(a0)=2C.

The group a0 is order two, not four. The previously considered
Z4 candidate was eliminated by the extra cycle Z0 above.

## 6. The ordinary zero test uses instability

Because F is a double loop space, the double suspension unit
F -> Omega² Sigma²F has a homotopy retraction by adjunction.
Since F is three-connected, Sigma²F is five-connected, and
pi10(Sigma²F) is stable. Therefore

    pi8(F) -> pi8(Sigma-infinity F)

is a split injection. In particular the already proved stable
zeros alphaBar nu5=0 and lambda eta7=0 are actual unstable zeros.

The nonzero stable class a0 is NOT in the ordinary image.
Its integral Hurewicz image is 2C, with C of order four.
In the stable cone of a0, a new mod-two top class x9 has

    beta(x9)=C modulo the old first-Bockstein images.

Every old first-Bockstein image has zero fourth square, while
Sq4 C=A4. The Adem identity Sq1 Sq4=Sq5 therefore gives

    Sq5_* x9=Sq4_* beta(x9)=A4 != 0.

If a0 came from an actual S8 -> F, its ordinary space cone
would have this operation. But Sq5 on degree-four cohomology
is zero by instability. This is a contradiction.

Thus the actual image intersects the stable mod-two Hurewicz
kernel only in zero, and

    pi8(F) -> H8(F;F2) is injective.

No identification of a quadratic complement is needed. In
particular an actual source obstruction with zero mod-two
Hurewicz image vanishes, which is the remaining condition in
the independent source nine-cell calculation.
