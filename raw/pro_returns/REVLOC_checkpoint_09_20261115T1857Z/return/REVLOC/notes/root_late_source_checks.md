## Circle Segal conjecture: all degrees matter

Opened Carlsson–Douglas–Dundas, *Higher topological cyclic homology and
the Segal conjecture for tori* (2011), Theorems 1.4, 6.1, 6.2 and 6.17,
and the rank/cotype splitting in Theorem 6.8:

<https://www.sas.rochester.edu/mth/sites/doug-ravenel/otherpapers/CDD.pdf>.

The circle function spectrum with p-complete sphere coefficients has
nontrivial positive-degree transfer contributions. Thus its reduced
degree-zero calculation cannot supply a CP-infinity-null coefficient
ring. These are sphere-coefficient statements. No tensor interchange
with an arbitrary ring R has been inferred.

Nishida, *On the S1-Segal Conjecture* (1983), introduction and theorem,
printed pp.1153–1154, was also opened:

<https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/42174>.

It explicitly distinguishes skeletal inverse-limit cohomotopy from
actual stable cohomotopy for a nonfinite compact Lie group. Neither
version gives the all-degree vanishing required by the Prüfer test.

The preceding low assignment now has an actual spherical variation.

<https://www.academia.edu/18703053/Embedding_products_of_low_dimensional_manifolds_into>.

frame the rank-two normal bundle of its central slice.

Let x,y be the degree-one generators on M=RP^3 times RP^3, and let
M->B classify xy. Pontryagin--Thom collapse of the framed embedding
gives S^8->Sigma^2 M_+->Sigma^2 B. The pullback of Sigma^2(u^3)
evaluates to one because (xy)^3=x^3y^3 is the top mod-two class.

complete primitive basis and naturality force its homology image to
be b^2+{a,b}+p3: Sq2 sets the b^2 coefficient to one and the second
Bockstein sets the bracket coefficient to one. This is an actual
unstable realization, not merely a stable or cohomological one.

The precise consequence is a choice of the top-cell correction on a
finite Moore quotient. It does not extend a map over the seven-cells
or over the full source. Those extension questions remain open.
