                                              Abstract
         This is an incomplete research record. We do not claim a proof or a counterexample
     for the implication from total asphericity to exact abelian colimits. We give complete
     proofs of the results explicitly labelled proved, distinguish the linear obstructions from
     their set-valued analogues, and record unresolved steps. A fully specified choice of repaired
     Adámek–Koubek–Reiterman targets gives a quasi-filtered category which is not total,
     deciding the strictness of that inclusion. No priority is claimed.

A.2     An arithmetic reduction
Proposition A.2 (Proved: fieldwise detection of integral exactness). For every small category
A, including the empty category and categories with several components, the following conditions
are equivalent:

    1. colimA : AbA → Ab is exact;

    2. colimA : (Q-Mod)A → Q-Mod is exact and, for every prime p, the functor colimA :
       (Fp -Mod)A → Fp -Mod is exact.

No totality hypothesis is needed. Consequently, Parts A.1 and A.2 taken together ask equivalent
universal questions. A negative answer to A.1 necessarily gives a failure over Q or over at least
one prime field, although the obstructing diagram need not remain the same after reduction.

Proof. The ordinary abelian-group colimit of a diagram of R-modules, for R = Q or Fp , carries
the unique compatible R-module structure, and its underlying abelian group is the ordinary
abelian-group colimit. This follows from the direct-sum-and-relations construction, since the
subgroup of relations is an R-submodule. Forgetting scalars is exact, so (1) implies (2).
   Assume (2). We use the normalized bar complex

A.3    Proof and construction routes investigated
The accompanying report records the precise scope of each attempted route. The model-
category observation that totality makes ordinary simplicial-set colimits homotopy invariant
does not by itself identify the underlying set colimit with the abelian-group colimit. A free
abelian cotriple resolution has permutation modules as its terms, but some face maps send a
basis vector to a linear combination; it is not the linearization of a simplicial set diagram. We
therefore do not infer acyclicity of its colimit complex from totality. The row/column formulas
below isolate the unresolved first simultaneous-intersection instance.

A.4    Ramsey classes and integral affine equalizers
The following argument is included with full proof. We do not claim priority for the implication;
the literature search did not identify an earlier statement in these terms.

Corollary A.8 (Proved: ordered finite graphs are exact). Let G have one representative of
each finite linearly ordered simple graph as objects and increasing induced graph embeddings as
arrows, composed as functions. Then G has exact colimits in abelian groups. In particular it is
not a counterexample to totality implying exactness.
Proof. The category is small, has finite hom-sets, and has common successors: take the disjoint
union of two graphs and concatenate their orders. Finite simple graphs with an arbitrary
linear order have the Ramsey property by (Hubička and Nešetřil, 2019, Corollary 4.3, printed
p. 51). The source defines embeddings by preservation and reflection of relations (printed
p. 6), so these are induced embeddings in the graph case. Because a finite linear order is rigid,
an isomorphic induced copy of p is specified by a unique embedding from p; hence its Ramsey
property for copies is exactly the morphism Ramsey property used in Theorem A.6. That
theorem applies.

and H2 (BC; Z) ∼= Z, and there is a five-arrow parallel family with no affine equalizer over any
nonzero commutative ring. No assertion that C is quasi-filtered is made.
