\begin{obstruction}[Open in this incomplete record: Part B.1]\label{B:triple-question}
We have not proved or refuted the assertion that every three parallel
arrows in every total category admit an integral affine equalizer.
The least undecided family size is three arrows. The equivalences below
are exact obstruction formulas, not vanishing theorems for those
obstructions. In particular, the vanishing of the row obstruction under
totality does not establish the vanishing of the column obstruction.
\end{obstruction}
