\begin{obstruction}[Open in this incomplete record: Part C.1]\label{C:survival-question}
For the repaired AKR construction and every admissible choice of its
parameters, the requested assertion is
\[
 H_2\!\left(\El_{K^*}
 \bigl(K^*(V,-)/(cv_1\sim cv_2)\bigr);\Z\right)\ne0.
\]
We have not established this universally quantified assertion or its
negation. Theorem~\ref{C:selected-nontotal} proves infinite-stage
survival for a fully specified choice of sufficiently large targets,
and more generally for such choices over any admissible parameter
sequence. It does not cover every admissible target choice. The
computations concern explicitly identified finite substages and do
not extend that quantifier. The all-coefficient obstruction in
Proposition~\ref{C:akr-all-rings} establishes nonexactness independently
of this unresolved totality question.
\end{obstruction}
