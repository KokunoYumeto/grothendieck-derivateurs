## Precisely what remains undecided

For a nonempty connected small category A, assume every component of every covariant category of elements El_A(H) is weakly contractible. The missing assertion is that for every finite ordinary parallel family a_0,...,a_k:p→q there exist r and t in Z[A(q,r)] with aug(t)=1 and ta_i=ta_0 for all i. The least family size not settled is three arrows. Pairwise affine equalization is already established, but iterating it produces affine pairs and does not formally handle larger ordinary families.

The column presentation Q_a=coker(P_q→P_p^k) gives exactly T_1(Q_a)=Z/ε(∩K_i). Totality supplies acyclicity of the row presentation and its row kernel. No argument here transfers that acyclicity to the column cokernel or its intersection kernel.

The coefficient questions remain undecided individually. The proved fieldwise-detection theorem shows that integral exactness, for any small category, is equivalent to rational exactness together with exactness over every prime field. This reduces the combined question; it does not decide either constituent.

For repaired AKR, the unresolved assertion is nonzero H_2 of the binary-quotient category of elements in the infinite union, uniformly for every admissible choice of construction parameters. For every admissible sequence (a_m), a choice of sufficiently large targets does give persistent nonzero H_2. The proof maintains separate linear orders on points and graph components, and strong geodesic convexity of every arrow image. A deterministic example is specified. This settles the strict inclusion total ⊊ quasi-filtered but does not replace the universal quantifier in C.1 by an existential one.

## Principal established deductions

2. **The row/column distinction.** The row cokernel is a permutation module. The column cokernel presents the simultaneous equalizer obstruction. Neither a transposition of matrices nor the separate acyclicity of K_i identifies the two.
3. **The resolving-class proposal.** Universal direct membership of Q_a in the class proposed in P1 is false. A fully specified filtered category with four objects and seventeen arrows has Q_a(x)=Z²⊕Z/3. Every member of the proposed integral class is objectwise torsion free. The invariant that σ^(2n)−2^(2n) is injective for every n≥1 is preserved by every permitted operation, including tensoring with permutation modules, but fails on this cokernel. The same second example works integrally with every value torsion free: its Laurent-polynomial determinant has unit leading coefficient and remains nonzero modulo every prime. Both examples concern direct membership only; finding a different module with the same T_1 remains open.
4. **A Ramsey criterion.** The morphism Ramsey property, with common successors, forces integral exactness. The proof extends a circle-valued character of the image of the column map, then discretizes its potentials into (3k+1)^k colors. It detects integral divisibility, not merely rational nonexistence. With arbitrary hom-sets the finite-pattern version suffices.

## Corrections and qualifications

### The Leads

- **Lead 4.** The two affine-equalization statements are true. Its final phrase cannot be read as identifying exactness with siftedness or with injectivity of the set-to-abelian colimit comparison. In the exact category Δ_inj, the vectors 2[a]−[b] and [a] have the same abelian-colimit class but distinct set-colimit classes: increasing injections preserve the nonzero coefficient pattern. The cospan category on two point objects has three components, distinguished by <, =, >.
- **Lead 5.** The phrase “carried around a 1-cycle of A” must refer to a bar chain with module coefficients. BA itself is contractible under totality. The precise assertion proved is that κ cannot die using only the binary cycle subfunctor if the triple has no affine equalizer. There is no ordinary H_1(BA) obstruction.
- **Lead 6.** “Factors through” is a strict factorization via the explicitly defined natural map H_u→H. The finite-sphere assertion uses subdivision and simplicial approximation, rather than treating a continuous sphere map as an arbitrary functor without justification.
- **Lead 7.** The cone dimension consequence retains the comma-contractibility hypothesis. Equalizing precomposition gives an embedded cycle, but without monomorphisms its component need not literally be a circle. A finite three-object, eight-arrow category yields a component with fifteen vertices, twenty edges, and H_1=Z^6. In AKR all maps are monic, so the cited exceptional component is a circle.
- **Lead 8.** “Over any ring” needs “over any nonzero unital commutative ring.” Over the zero ring the zero combination has augmentation 1=0 and is an affine equalizer. The intrinsic failure of totality and integral exactness is unchanged.
- **Leads 1–3 and 9.** No false mathematical assertion was found within the stated hypotheses. The proof of Lead 2 explicitly retains aug(z)=0 in its Tor formula. Lead 3 handles the vacuous family k=0.
