## C.1 investigations

The source invariant C.2(c_m) states: if a W-arrow h into X meets the image of any arrow g:Y→X in at least two points, then h factors through g. It is stronger than arbitrary image overlap and may control the cycle-module comparison. It implies that W-edge overlaps of quasi-filter copies can be routed across neighboring copies using actual arrows of the old category. It does not immediately imply right-division closure for arbitrary source objects.

The right-division test finds failures already at fresh identity quasi-filter targets (these are copies of V and W with bijective incoming arrows but no arrows back). These failures are harmless candidates for a contraction but invalidate an unqualified right-division argument. The selected critical third-stage test is running; its algorithm is intentionally a test of a proposed invariant, not a proof of eventual persistence.

Unsettled: a proof that every exceptional comma link has H2=0, or that its H2 maps trivially into the old category of elements. A cover by representable slices has overlaps governed by equalizer subfunctors; their components need not be treated as contractible without proof. A possible sufficient invariant is connectedness of each fixed-point intersection inside an H-component; no induction has been established.

## Exact attachment reduction

For a new target generated by α_i:T→Z, there is an exact per-attachment criterion (now proved in `C:attachment-cycle-test`):

- let K(X)=H1 of the graph of W-edges joining their first two image points;
- old Q=colim K injects into new Q iff every relation Σ α_i κ_i=0 in K(Z) has Σ[κ_i]=0 in old Q;
- this is precisely injectivity of H2 of the binary element category, by the natural dimension-shifting formula, checked directly from the two short exact sequences in TOTEX B.2.

Thus an all-stage proof can work by extending every old cycle functional through this graph-cycle map. It need not prove every link has H2=0. A computation can test the exact obstruction using cycle-space matrices, avoiding full higher nerve matrices.

## Convexity route: exact remaining issue

The independently run actual-stage tests show all finite intersections of incoming image subgraphs are connected inside each ambient H-component, and all incoming images are geodesically convex, for the selected K2 and critical K3 substages. These finite checks are not an induction.

A generic graph counterexample constructed by the independent finite-test agent shows that convex embeddings f,g and bounded point-level f/g paths do NOT guarantee preservation of H-component injections under repaired gluing: two disjoint edges, S two isolated points, f=(0,2), g=(3,1) yield a component-level 2-cycle and a connected 26-cycle after s=13 gluing. This is not known to occur among actual C.1 arrows. Thus any all-stage convexity proof needs the additional absence of component-level cycles, and must prove that property survives every allowed attachment. No proof of that survival has been obtained here.
