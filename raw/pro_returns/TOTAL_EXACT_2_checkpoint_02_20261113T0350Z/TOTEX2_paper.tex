\begin{abstract}
This is an incomplete research record. We do not claim a proof or a
counterexample for the implication from total asphericity to exact
abelian colimits. We give complete proofs of the results explicitly
labelled proved, distinguish the linear obstructions from their
set-valued analogues, and record unresolved steps. A fully specified
choice of repaired Ad\'amek--Koubek--Reiterman targets gives a
quasi-filtered category which is not total, deciding the strictness
of that inclusion. No priority is claimed.
\end{abstract}
