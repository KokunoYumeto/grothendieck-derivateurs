\section*{Conventions and scope}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Conventions and scope}

We work with nested Grothendieck universes $\mathbb U\in\mathbb V$.
Small means $\mathbb U$-small, $\Cat$ denotes small categories, and
categories of all small diagrams are formed in $\mathbb V$.
We write $BS=|NS|$, $\Spaces$ for the $\infty$-category of
small spaces, and $\W=\{u\mid Bu\text{ is a weak homotopy equivalence}\}$.
We put $\Hot=\Cat[\W^{-1}]$.
An unqualified equivalence of $\infty$-categories is a categorical equivalence,
not merely a weak equivalence of their groupoid completions. All limits and
colimits of spaces or $\infty$-categories are derived limits and colimits.
The symbol $\Ho$ is applied after forming a coherent functor category.

\begin{statement}{0.1}{Proved}{The variance convention}
Throughout this paper,
\[
 \mathcal P(S)=\Fun(NS^{\op},\Spaces),\qquad
 \HOT(S)=\Ho\mathcal P(S),\qquad
 \D_{\cC}(S)=\Ho\Fun(NS^{\op},\cC).
\]
A sieve is closed under sources and is called open. For a poset $P$, its
topology $P_{\downarrow}$ has downward closed opens. Our geometric model
for this convention is the \emph{minimum-vertex} stratification of $|NP|$,
equivalently the maximum-vertex stratification of $|N(P^{\op})|$ over
$P^{\op}$ with upward closed opens. Sheaves are covariant on exit paths;
therefore the finite-poset comparison identifies its exit category with
$P^{\op}$. The same abstract indexing convention applies for arbitrary
$P$, with geometric qualifications stated in A.4--A.6.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
The principal open $U_p=P_{\leq p}$ is contained in $U_q$ when $p\leq q$.
Restriction of sections therefore gives a map from the value at $q$ to the
value at $p$. Reversing a simplex identifies $|NP|$ homeomorphically with
$|N(P^{\op})|$ and changes minimum into maximum. This fixes all the
oppositions used below.
\end{proof}

For $u:A\to B$, the objects of $A/b$ are $(a,u(a)\to b)$ and those of
$b\backslash A$ are $(a,b\to u(a))$. An ordinary final functor means that
all $b\backslash A$ are nonempty and connected; \emph{homotopy final}
means that their nerves are weakly contractible. These conditions are not
interchanged. For a presheaf derivator a natural transformation
$\alpha:u\Rightarrow v$ induces $\alpha^*:v^*\Rightarrow u^*$.
This is the index-opposite of the usual covariant derivator convention.
An abstract derivator is assumed to satisfy Der~1--4 on all small
categories; strongness is an additional hypothesis whenever used.

We use the relative fundamental-localizer axiom: if
$A\xrightarrow{u}B\xrightarrow{v}C$ is a triangle and
$A/c\to B/c$ belongs to $W$ for every $c\in C$, then $u\in W$.
Together with this we require identities, two-out-of-three, the weak
retract axiom ($ri=1$ and $ir\in W$ imply $i,r\in W$), and
$A\to e\in W$ whenever $A$ has a terminal object. This is the relative
version printed as Loc~(3 bis) in Chapter XVI. We do not assume that it
follows from the absolute version printed there as Loc~(3).

The four status labels have literal meanings. \emph{Known} cites a theorem
whose statement and required hypotheses were inspected. \emph{Proved}
records a proof here and makes no priority claim. \emph{Refuted} includes a
counterexample to the stated formulation. \emph{Open} records what this
audit has not established; it does not assert that the question is open
in the literature. For geometric exodromy we distinguish ordinary
constructible sheaves, constructible hypersheaves, and coefficients defined
intrinsically on an abstract stratified homotopy type. An arbitrary
derivator is not silently replaced by an $\infty$-categorical enhancement.
