# STRATA: small categories as stratified spaces, and coefficients on them as constructible sheaves

Package 5 of 7 on Alexander Grothendieck, *Les Dérivateurs* (1990–91). File prefix for your deliverables: `STRATA`.

## What is to be settled

Grothendieck's vocabulary for small categories is topological throughout: a sieve is "un ouvert", a cosieve "un fermé" (the Alexandrov topology); for an open `j: U → X` and its closed complement `i: T → X` a pointed derivator has the exceptional adjoints `i^!` and `j^?` (I ms. 3–9); base change holds for "propres" and "lisses" functors (I ms. 47–59; VI ms. 162–169); over a base `S` the theory `HOT(S)` contains the full reflective subcategory `HOT^lc(S)` of locally constant objects (VI ms. 32–69); a category is glued from a sieve `A` and the complementary cosieve `B` along the bifunctor of crossing arrows (XII ms. 59–72), and the appendix to XII ends with the conjecture `BX ≃ hocolim(BA ← BE → BB)`, `E` the category of crossing arrows with commuting squares (ms. 17–18), which the companion proves for `W∞` (XIIapp block "The open–closed gluing formula").

Since then, constructible sheaves on a stratified space have been identified with functors on its exit-path ∞-category (MacPherson; Treumann; Lurie, *Higher Algebra*, Appendix A; Barwick–Glasman–Haine), stratified homotopy types with ∞-categories equipped with a conservative functor to a poset (Haine; Ayala–Francis–Rozenblyum; Douteau), and a stratified space is glued from strata and homotopy links (Quinn). The thesis to be established or corrected is that these are the same subject: that `Cat`, with its fine structure (the functor `S ↦ HOT(S)`, not the localization at `W∞`), is a category of stratified homotopy types, that a derivator evaluated on `S` is the theory of constructible coefficients on the stratified space of `S`, and that the manuscript's results translate into known theorems and, where they do not, into new ones. Nothing here is to be taken on analogy: each line of the dictionary is a theorem with hypotheses, or it is struck out.

## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

- **Lead 1.** For a poset `P` with its Alexandrov topology (decide which of the two), `Fun(P, 𝒮)` or `Fun(P^op, 𝒮)` is the ∞-category of sheaves of spaces on it, and equally the ∞-category of sheaves on `|N P|` constructible with respect to the stratification `|N P| → P`. So `HOT(P) = Ho` of constructible sheaves and `HOT^lc(P) = Ho` of locally constant sheaves, the inclusion and its two adjoints (Cisinski 2009) being the classical ones.
- **Lead 2.** For a general small category `S`, the correct statement passes through categories in which every endomorphism is an isomorphism (EI) or through a subdivision of `S`; for `S` with non-invertible endomorphisms `HOT(S)` is not constructible sheaves on a stratified space in the usual sense, and one must say what replaces it.
- **Lead 3.** The category `E` of crossing arrows of a sieve–cosieve decomposition models the homotopy link of the closed stratum, and the gluing formula is the categorical form of Quinn's. The companion's remark that replacing crossing arrows by all paths changes the answer is the difference between exit paths and paths.
- **Lead 4.** The recollement `(j_!, j^*, j_*; i^*, i_*, i^!)` of I ms. 3–9 for a pointed or stable derivator is the recollement of constructible sheaves along an open–closed decomposition, and a derivator **D** on `Cat` gives, by restriction to posets, a theory of **D**-valued constructible sheaves on finite regular cell complexes (or on finite simplicial complexes) with proper and smooth base change in Grothendieck's sense matching the topological ones in a precise class of maps.
