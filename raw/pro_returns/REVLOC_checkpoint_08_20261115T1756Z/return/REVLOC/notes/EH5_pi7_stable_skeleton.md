# Complete degree-seven stable attachment detectors

This is an independent component calculation for the target after
D.2.18. The complete degree-eight homology/operation table is owned
by the axioms/derivators agent. The calculation here fixes every
component group and detector needed to turn that table into actual
eight-cell attachments.

All groups below are two-local. Write M_m=cofib(m:S -> S), and let
C=cofib((2,eta):S4 vee S5 -> S4). In D.2.18 the stable six-skeleton is

    L6 = C vee Sigma5 M4 vee S6_r.

Its degree-six group has generators gamma,delta,t,rho0, with orders
infinity,2,2,infinity. The four seven-cell attachments V,P,Q,R are

    gamma+t, gamma+2rho0+delta, delta, 0.

The choice of gamma is fixed by its image (0,2) in
pi6(C/S4)=Z2{eta5}+Z{identity6}; delta has image (eta5,0).

## 1. Exact degree-seven groups and maps of the three summands

The opened Oka1986 Section2 (2.1),(2.2), printed p149, gives

    pi1S=Z2{eta}, pi2S=Z2{eta²}, pi3S_(2)=Z8{nu},
    eta³=4nu (after a harmless odd change of nu),
    p tilde_eta2=eta, 2 tilde_eta2=i eta².

Thus pi2(M2)=Z4{tilde_eta2}. The degree-three Moore exact sequence is

    0 -> Z2{i nu} -> pi3(M2) -> Z2{eta²} -> 0.

The element tilde_eta2 eta lifts eta² and has order two:
2 tilde_eta2 eta=i eta³=4 i nu=0, since 2 i nu=0 in M2.
Consequently

    pi3(M2)=Z2{i nu}+Z2{tilde_eta2 eta}.

Now C is obtained from Sigma4 M2 by attaching along i eta4.
In its cofiber exact sequence, pi7(S5) maps by i eta³=0,
whereas pi6(S5) maps by i eta² != 0. Hence the Moore inclusion
is an isomorphism in degree seven, and

    pi7(C)=Z2{v=i nu4}+Z2{h=delta eta6}.

The quotient to S5 vee S6 sends v to zero and h to (eta5²,0).

For the other Moore summand, the natural map M2 -> M4 with bottom
degree two and top degree one sends tilde_eta2 to an element b with
p b=eta and 2b=i(2eta²)=0. The degree-two Moore sequence therefore
splits, with specified generators:

    pi7(Sigma5 M4)=Z2{a=i eta5²}+Z2{b}.

The remaining sphere contributes c=rho0 eta6 of order two.
Thus pi7(L6) is the five-dimensional F2-vector space with basis
v,h,a,b,c.

## 2. The crucial equality gamma eta6=0

There are two compatible direct proofs.

First, gamma is the image of the unique generator gamma_eta of
pi6(Sigma4 Ceta) whose top-cell degree is two. The map Ceta -> C
includes the bottom and the six-cell; it has zero component in the
five-cell quotient. Thus its image is exactly the gamma fixed in
D.2.18, not gamma+delta.

The self-action of eta on Ceta factors as

    Sigma Ceta -> S3 --beta--> S0 -> Ceta.

Indeed eta restricted to the bottom is zero, so it factors through
the top quotient, and pi3(S) -> pi3(Ceta) is onto because
eta:pi1S -> pi2S is an isomorphism. Naturality then gives
gamma_eta eta=i beta*(2). After mapping to C this is zero: the
bottom map i:S4 -> C has a degree-two nullhomotopy. Therefore

    gamma eta6=0.

Equivalently, the quotient of gamma eta to S5 vee S6 is zero, so
gamma eta is a multiple of v. The mod-two Hopf invariant of nu
shows that Sq4 from the bottom four-class detects v. But a map
factoring as S7 --eta--> S6 --gamma--> C has zero such Sq4:
the induced mapping-cone map from Sigma6 Ceta is an isomorphism
on the new degree-eight class and has zero degree-four cohomology.
Thus the coefficient is zero. This second proof also makes clear
that no choice of an unspecified Toda-bracket lift is being used.

## 3. The whole stable seven-skeleton group

Precomposing the four seven-cell attachments with eta gives

    a, h, h, 0.

The cokernel in degree seven is therefore Z2{v,b,c}. The kernel
of the degree-six attaching homomorphism from Z_(2)^4 is freely
generated by 2Q and R. In fact the rho0 and gamma coordinates
first force the P and V coefficients to vanish, and then the
delta coordinate makes the Q coefficient even.

The cofiber exact sequence, whose right term is free, now gives

    pi7(L7)=Z_(2){kappa}+Z_(2){lambda}+Z2{v,b,c}.

Choose kappa projecting to top degree two on Q and lambda to
degree one on R. The latter can be the actual inclusion of the
freely wedged seven-sphere. Their integral Hurewicz images are
2Q and R, respectively.

The three torsion classes are not inferred from an abstract group
table: v is bottom nu, b is the specified Moore coextension, and
c is the eta action on the spherical six-class rho0.

## 4. Complete detectors for an eight-cell

Use the degree-six classes d,e,f of D.2.18, so the Moore top is
d-e and the free sphere class is f-e. The detector vectors are

    v: Sq2=0,       Sq4=A4;
    b: Sq2=d+e,     Sq4=0;
    c: Sq2=e+f,     Sq4=0.

The b vector follows from projection of its coextension onto the
Moore top. The c vector is the mapping-cone eta square on the
spherical class r=e+f. Both maps factor through complexes with
zero degree-four cohomology, which verifies their Sq4 coefficients.
The bottom Hopf map has Sq4 coefficient one, so the order-two
class v is detected completely: no invisible 2nu survives.

For kappa, the top degree two on Q gives first Bockstein Q.
The Adem relations imply

    Sq3_*(top)=Sq2_* Q=z5,
    Sq2_* Sq2_*(top)=Sq1_* z5=A4.

Since Sq2 sends each of d,e,f to A4, its coefficient vector has
odd coordinate sum. Adding b and c normalizes it to d; adding v
then normalizes its Sq4 to zero. These changes preserve its
integral top degree and first Bockstein.

The class 2lambda factors through the degree-two Moore cofiber
on its seven-sphere. It consequently has Sq2=Sq4=0 and first
Bockstein R.

Every eight-cell of a minimal modulo-two stable model attaches
by an element of the form

    n kappa+2m lambda+epsilon v+zeta b+xi c.

Its integral cellular boundary is 2n Q+2m R. Its mod-two
operation vectors are

    beta=(n mod2) Q+(m mod2) R,
    Sq2=(n mod2)d+zeta(d+e)+xi(e+f),
    Sq4=epsilon A4.

Therefore the integral boundary together with Sq2 and Sq4 fixes
the entire attaching map. This is the exact detector statement
needed for the axioms agent's degree-eight table.

## 5. Consequence of the independently supplied degree-eight table

The supplied table has first-Bockstein image spanned by Q,R.
Together with the already known H6(F)=Z2² and four degree-seven
mod-two classes, this gives H7(F)=Z2². Thus the integral eighth
boundary has image exactly 2Z_(2){Q,R}. Normalize its two nonzero
columns with the prescribed mod-two reductions as in D.2.18.
The table's cells L,J3 can have exact boundaries 2Q,2R; replace
the other Q-boundary cell M by M-L.

Using the detector formulas, the resulting relations are

    kappa, v, b+c, b, 2lambda+b, 0, 0.

Their cokernel is Z2{lambda}; in particular c=rho eta becomes
zero. This last conclusion is conditional only on the complete
H8(F) table, which is separately being written and independently
audited; the component groups and all detector formulas above
are proved here directly.
