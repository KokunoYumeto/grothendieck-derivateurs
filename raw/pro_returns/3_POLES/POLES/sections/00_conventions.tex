\subsection*{Conventions and evidentiary status}
We fix universes $\mathcal U\in\mathcal V$. Categories are $\mathcal U$-small, and classes and localizations are formed in $\mathcal V$ when needed. We write $BA=|NA|$, $e$ for the terminal category, $\varnothing$ for the empty category, and $\Winfty$ for the functors inducing weak homotopy equivalences on nerves. We put $\Hot=\Cat[\Winfty^{-1}]$. A category $A$ is $\W$-aspherical if $A\to e$ belongs to $\W$.

We use the prompt's modern relative-Theorem-A convention for a fundamental localizer: identities belong to $\W$; two out of three holds; if $i:A\to B$ and $r:B\to A$ satisfy $ri=\id_A$ and $ir\in\W$, then $i,r\in\W$; every category with a terminal object is $\W$-aspherical; and, for a commutative triangle $A\xrightarrow{u}B\to C$, if each induced $A/c\to B/c$ belongs to $\W$, then $u\in\W$. In the actual Chapter XVI this last axiom is Loc (3 bis), while Loc (3) is only the absolute Theorem A, corresponding to $C=B$ and the identity structure functor of $B$. The prompt conflates these labels. We retain the modern definition for the mathematical leads and explicitly state stronger hypotheses when auditing deductions under the weaker manuscript definition. Full arrow-retract closure and strong saturation are theorems for the modern definition, not extra assumptions silently inserted in historical arguments.

For $u:A\to B$, the objects of $A/b$ are $(a,u(a)\to b)$; the objects of $b\backslash A$ are $(a,b\to u(a))$. A final functor in the usual covariant convention has nonempty connected $b\backslash A$. A sieve is a full subcategory closed under sources of arrows; a cosieve is closed under targets. The manuscript normally uses presheaf coefficients. We state variance at each comparison where it changes a conclusion.

Every displayed claim is assigned one of the statuses Proved, Refuted, Known, or Open. Known means that the cited result has been checked in the indicated source; it does not mean that every surrounding inference in that source is accepted. Open means unresolved in this audit, unless a claim explicitly identifies an open problem in the literature. We claim no priority. The attached companion, CATHOT, and TOTASPH texts are drafts to be checked, not authorities.
