- **Finite tests do not determine a morphism of classifying topoi (Parts A and E).** PATHS E.13: let `G` be the group of finitely supported permutations of `N` and `s: G → G` the shift (`s(σ)` fixes `0` and sends `i + 1` to `σ(i) + 1`). Then `s` is not surjective, and on every finitely generated subgroup of `G` it is conjugation by an element of `G`. So the maps of classifying spaces induced by `s` and by the identity are different and become homotopic after composition with every map from a finite category. An inference of the author of this prompt, unverified: read for multi-Galois topoi, the two morphisms `BG → BG` are not isomorphic, they become isomorphic after composition with every morphism from the classifying topos of a finitely generated group, and they induce the same map on profinite completions (the only finite quotients of `G` are through the sign, which `s` preserves). In Part A, wherever a statement recovers a morphism of multi-Galois topoi, or an isomorphism between two of them, from its effect on a class of test objects (finite coverings, finitely generated groupoids, the stages of a pro-object), test it on this example and state the finiteness hypothesis it needs.
- **The shapes in Kaledin's theorem (the item "A second route" above).** PATHS D.14: in arXiv:2409.17489v3 the indexing category is that of partially ordered sets of finite chain dimension, not of finite ones; infinite discrete sets belong to it, and D.16 gives an example where they decide (a family failing the additivity axiom at a countable discrete set). D.15 (Known there, depending on the preprint): a family of homotopy categories of strict diagrams satisfies those axioms iff it is the family of the ∞-categorical localization. Read "indexed by partially ordered sets `J`" above with that class.
- **Unions of groupoids and of categories (for §3 and Part C).** PATHS A.2: for every filtered diagram of small categories the ordinary colimit has the homotopy type of the homotopy colimit, with no hypothesis on the transition functors; A.5–A.7: this is special to filtered shapes (it fails over the category of finite non-empty ordinals and injections, although colimits of abelian groups over that category are exact). So a statement about an ind-system of groupoids may be read on the union, while a van Kampen statement for a pushout must be about the 2-pushout.
