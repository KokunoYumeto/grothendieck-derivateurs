- **The category.** STRATA F.7 (Known there, from M. Ørsnes Jansen, *Moduli stack of stable curves from a stratified homotopy viewpoint*, arXiv:2308.09551v2, Construction 3.18, Proposition 3.23, Corollary 6.6; the paper is attached under `papers/2308.09551/`). Let `Γ_n` be the group of isotopy classes of orientation-preserving homeomorphisms of the sphere fixing each of `n` marked points, and `𝒞_n` the set of isotopy classes of essential multicurves on the `n`-punctured sphere, the empty one included. For a multicurve `σ` let `Δ_σ ⊂ Γ_n` be the free abelian group generated by the Dehn twists about its curves. The category `C_n` has objects `𝒞_n`, `Hom(σ, τ) = {g ∈ Γ_n : gσ ⊇ τ}/Δ_σ` (left cosets `gΔ_σ`) and composition `[h]∘[g] = [hg]`. Its nerve is the exit-path ∞-category of `M̄_{0,n}(C)` with the stratification by dual trees; every endomorphism is invertible; the automorphism group of `σ` is the product over the vertices `v` of the dual tree of the groups `Γ_{val(v)}`; the isomorphism classes of objects are the stable labelled trees. An arrow of `C_n` smooths nodes. The opposite category `T_{0,n} = C_n^op`, whose arrows are degenerations, is the Charney–Lee category, and with coefficients taken as presheaves it is `T_{0,n}` that carries the constructible sheaves: `Sh_cbl(M̄_{0,n}; 𝒞) ≃ Fun(C_n, 𝒞) = Fun(T_{0,n}^op, 𝒞)` for compactly generated `𝒞` (F.8, from Jansen, Theorem 6.7). Keep the two variances apart in everything you state. Jansen's Corollary 6.6 is stated for every stable dual graph and every genus, with the moduli stack as a stratified topological stack; STRATA used it in genus 0 only, where there is no isotropy. For the types `(1,1)` and `(1,2)` of Part E, take the category from that paper, with the isotropy, and say what changes.
- **The two smallest cases, complete.** F.1–F.2: a skeleton of `C_4` has four objects `u, p_0, p_1, p_∞` with `Aut(u) = G_4 = ⟨a, b⟩` free of rank 2, `Aut(p_i) = 1`, and `Hom(p_i, u) = G_4/⟨m_i⟩` for the peripheral elements `m_0 = a`, `m_1 = b`, `m_∞ = (ab)^{-1}`, with `G_4` acting on the left; no other arrows. F.4–F.6: `C_5` has 26 isomorphism classes of objects (the open stratum, 10 divisors `d_I`, 15 points `q_{I,J}`), `Aut(u) = G_5 = P_4/⟨(σ_1σ_2σ_3)^4⟩` (the pure braid group on four strands modulo its centre), `Aut(d_I) ≅ F_2`, `Aut(q) = 1`, `Hom(d_I, u) = G_5/⟨t_{c_I}⟩`, `Hom(q, u) = G_5/Δ_q` with `Δ_q ≅ Z²`, `Hom(q, d_I) ≅ F_2/⟨m_{q,I}⟩` with `m_{q,I}` peripheral, and the composition (F.4) through the divisors written with chosen transporters `r_{q,I}`. So the datum at each boundary stratum is a group together with the conjugacy classes of its peripheral (twist) subgroups and the bimodules between adjacent strata: a group "à lacets" in the sense of the manuscript, at every level of the stratification. F.3 (Refuted there): asphericity of the strata alone does not make the exit-path ∞-category an ordinary category; the injectivity of the peripheral subgroups is used.

- **The functors.** F.9: filling the last puncture gives a homomorphism `φ: Γ_{n+1} → Γ_n` and a map `q` on multicurves (peripheral curves are dropped, curves that become isotopic are identified), hence a strict functor `Q: C_{n+1} → C_n`, `[g] ↦ [φ(g)]`, whose opposite `F: T_{0,n+1} → T_{0,n}` represents the map forgetting the last point; repeated forgetting is strict on these models, and relabelling the points is functorial only up to specified natural isomorphisms, since no splitting of `Γ_{0,n} → 𝔖_n` is chosen. F.15: `T_{0,5} → T_{0,4}` on all 26 objects and on the coset bimodules. F.12: clutching is the inclusion of the full replete subcategory of the objects whose tree contains a given split `A|B`, equivalent to `T_{0,A∪*} × T_{0,B∪*}`; it is a cosieve in `T_{0,n}`. F.13: the strict fibre of `F` over an object is the entrance category of the corresponding stable curve stratified by its marked points and nodes. F.10: `F` is proper in the sense of *Les Dérivateurs* (right Kan extension along it commutes with every strict base change), by the contractibility of posets of multicurves lifting a given one (trees with leaves; Kent–Leininger–Schleimer, *Trees and mapping class groups*, Theorem 7.1, as cited there; not opened by the author of this prompt). F.11 (Refuted there): `F` is not smooth already for `T_{0,5} → T_{0,4}`; the lifting groupoid at the point stratum `q_{12,34}` over the arrow from the boundary point to the open stratum is `BZ`, generated by `t_1 t_2^{-1}`, the difference of the two Dehn twists that become equal after forgetting the fifth point. F.14: the resulting base-change statements for constructible coefficients.
- **What it says about the tower.** F.17: the full subcategory of `T_{0,n}` on the open stratum is the Teichmüller groupoid `BΓ_n`; the maximal subgroupoid of `T_{0,n}` is the disjoint union over the trees of the groupoids of the products `∏_v Γ_{val(v)}`; the whole category is not a groupoid, and its ∞-groupoid completion is `M̄_{0,n}` itself (`S²` for `n = 4`), which is simply connected. The Lego–Teichmüller complex of Bakalov–Kirillov presents `Γ_n` only with its mapping class group action kept. So `T_{0,n}` is neither the Teichmüller groupoid nor a completion of it: it is the open groupoid together with all the boundary groupoids and the bimodules of degenerations between them.
- **What it does not do.** No profinite completion, no action of `Gal(Q̄/Q)`, no comparison with `GT̂`, nothing in positive genus. These are Part F here.
