- **Relative posets present everything (Part D (3), and "Recent results").** B.8 (Known; Kerodon, Theorem 6.3.6.1): for every small category `S` there are a poset `Q` and a functor `Q → S` exhibiting `S` as the ∞-categorical localization of `Q` at the arrows `W_v` sent to isomorphisms, so `Fun(S^op, 𝒞)` is the full subcategory of `Fun(Q^op, 𝒞)` of the functors inverting `W_v`, for every ∞-category `𝒞`. So relative posets `(Q, W_v)` already present all small categories, with their diagram ∞-categories; B.6: the ∞-category `Fun(S^op, 𝒮)` determines the idempotent completion of `S` and nothing more; B.7: a non-invertible endomorphism cannot be removed by any unmarked replacement.
