## What is to be settled

§19 sets up the Teichmüller tower: for each type `(g, ν)` the group `A_{g,ν}` of homeomorphisms of the standard surface `U_{g,ν}`, its variants, the Teichmüller groups `T_{g,ν} = π_0`, with the maps between types given by filling holes and by finite coverings. §26 passes to profinite groups: the completion `T̂`, the full group `T̂̂` of exterior automorphisms with loops ("Outext"), "discrétifications" and "prédiscrétifications" of a profinite group with loops, the modular topos `M_{g,ν}` over `Q` with its exact sequence, and a "conjecture hâtive"; §28 finds that it "grince", §29 adjusts the notions, §§30–33 bis study the groups `N_{g,ν}` and `𝕀Γ_{g,ν}` under change of type, §34 gives a "description heuristique profinie de la catégorie des courbes algébriques" over finite extensions of `Q`, §35 proves injectivity of `𝕀Γ_Q → Autext_lac(π̂_{0,3})`, §36 treats `𝕀Γ_{1,1}`. His statements and questions:

1. The "conjecture fondamentale" (scans 341, 356, 371, 376): each `𝕀Γ_{g,ν}` is canonically `Gal(Q̄/Q)`; `𝕀Γ_Q → 𝕀Γ_{g,ν}` is bijective; consequence: the abelianization is `Ẑ*` by the cyclotomic character.
2. Is `T̂` normal in `T̂̂`; `T̂⁺` invariant; `N_Σ = T̂̂` (scans 322–326; one conjecture withdrawn as "ultra-faux"); the congruence property he assumes (scan 320); uniqueness of `Σ`.
3. `Prédiscrét⁺(π) → Prédiscrét⁺(π')` bijective, `Discret(π) → Discret(π')` injective, under filling a hole and under finite coverings (scans 347, 352, 355, 381–400).
4. Filling a hole arithmetically (§32): `𝕀Γ_{g,ν} ≅ 𝕀Γ_{g,ν−1}`, the exact sequence he marks "??", the faithfulness (f) of `Θ_{g,ν}` (scans 395–420); in passing he reconstructs "tout le topos étale d'une courbe algébrique complète, à partir du π_1 d'un ouvert anabélien".
5. §34 (scans 483–490): "Conjecture. Ce n'est pas la peine": the loop structure of `π` is the only one invariant under the outer Galois action, the `L_i` being the maximal subgroups whose normalizer has open image in `Γ`; every open homomorphism between such extensions coming from curves respects loops and comes from a morphism of curves.
6. Faithfulness (scans 496–505): `Γ → Out(π̂_1(U))` for anabelian `U`, also modulo the profinite Teichmüller group; `Γ → 𝚪_g` for `g ≥ 2` and `Γ → 𝚪_{1,1}`, "peut-être est-ce tout à fait faux ?"; centre of a free profinite group and of `Gal` and its open subgroups, "je cale sur a)", "Consulter Deligne" (scan 496).
7. Scan 479: he has "l'impression" that `Γ_Q → Γ_{0,3}` is an isomorphism, by a result of a Russian mathematician passed on by Deligne; "la question essentielle" is an algebraic description of `Γ_{0,3}` and of the admissible splittings.

## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

- **Lead 1.** The result of scan 479 is Belyi's theorem (1979), which gives injectivity. Surjectivity is the question whether `Gal(Q̄/Q)` equals the Grothendieck–Teichmüller group `GT̂` of Drinfeld and Ihara (open). Grothendieck's `𝕀Γ_{0,3}` should be compared exactly with `GT̂` and with the group of outer automorphisms of the genus-0 tower (Harbater–Schneps; Pop's theorem on the automorphisms of the tower; Hoshi–Minamide–Mochizuki): which group is his, and is his conjecture then Ihara's question, a theorem, or false at type `(0,3)` taken alone?
- **Lead 2.** Item 5 is now largely theorems: the group-theoretic characterization of inertia subgroups (Nakamura, by weights; Tamagawa; Mochizuki) and the Hom-form of the anabelian conjecture for hyperbolic curves over sub-`p`-adic fields (Mochizuki 1999).
- **Lead 3.** Item 6: faithfulness on `π̂_1` of affine hyperbolic curves is Matsumoto (1996), of proper ones Hoshi–Mochizuki (2011); the centre statements are known (free profinite groups; `Gal` of number fields is centre-free).
- **Lead 4.** Item 2 is the congruence subgroup problem for mapping class groups (known in genus 0 and 1, Asada; genus 2, Boggi; open in general), and `T̂ ≠ T̂̂` always.
- **Lead 5.** Item 4 is a profinite Birman sequence and a "cuspidalization" statement (Mochizuki; Hoshi–Mochizuki's combinatorial anabelian geometry).

## After the first Dérivateurs return (added 11 November 2026)

Package CATHOT of the companion work on *Les Dérivateurs* has been answered and checked by two readers; the named parts are attached.

- **For Part F, the profinite completion of a category.** A profinite completion of the exit-path category is a pro-object of finite categories (or of categories with finite Hom-sets). CATHOT E.4 (confirmed): an isomorphism of ind-objects of categories modulo natural transformations does not imply that the colimits are weakly equivalent, even with finite stages that are groupoids up to that equivalence; the same care is needed for pro-objects. So define the completion with its actual transition functors, state whether the Galois action is on the pro-object or on a limit, and compare automorphism groups at the level you define; for groups this is the usual distinction between a profinite group and its system of finite quotients up to inner automorphisms, and for categories with several objects it has more content.
- CATHOT C.1–C.4 (confirmed): categories and functors modulo natural transformations carry exactly 1-types, with the fundamental groupoid as reflector. The strata of `M̄_{0,n}` are 1-types, so the outer actions on the automorphism groups and on the bimodules of exit paths are the right level for Part F, and nothing is lost by working modulo natural isomorphism there.
