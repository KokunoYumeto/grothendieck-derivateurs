## 4_GALOIS: not changed (already submitted); what to check in its answer

`4_GALOIS` was submitted before POLES came back. `PROMPT.md` and the zip are untouched; one entry was appended to its `CHANGES_20261110.md`. When the GALOIS answer returns, check these parts against these POLES statements.

**Part B (the Loc axioms of `W_D^lc`, `W_D^1`, compared with `W_D`): first.**

1. *Loc (7), in any of the eight forms of XVI §4.4, including the weakest (7 D) relative to `W∞`.* POLES E.4–E.5 and A.17: a fundamental localizer has it iff it is `W∞` or the class of all functors; no accessibility. So GALOIS must say: `W_D^lc` satisfies Loc (7) exactly for the **D** with `W_D^lc = W∞` (expected: `HOT`, `D(Z)`, spectra), and fails it for sets (`W_1`), `(n−1)`-types (`W_n`), `D(Q)`, `D(F_p)`. Any other answer contradicts E.5, whose proof is half a page and which I followed. The question of Part F, "for which **D** is `W_D^lc = W∞`", is the same question.
2. *Loc (7 F) (every functor factors as a `W`-map followed by an intrinsic `W`-fibration).* POLES E.2–E.3: for accessible `W`, equivalent to: generated by maps `A → e`; right proper; the localization of spaces is a nullification. C.9: all `W_n` have it. C.5: no `W_E`, `E ≠ 0`, has it (so `W_D` for `D(k)` and spectra does not). For `W_{D(Q)}^lc` the test is whether it is the nullification at its aspherical objects (the simply connected rationally acyclic spaces): check GALOIS's verdict by that criterion.
3. *Loc (6 b), (6 c) (reversible cofibration axiom).* POLES B.5–B.6, B.11, H.8: a localizer with (6 b) contains `W_Z`, so it inverts `BG → e` for every acyclic group `G`; a non-total one lies in `W_0` and fails (7 F). Hence every class that detects `π_1` fails (6 b): `W_D^lc` and `W_D^1` for sets, `n`-types, `D(k)`, spectra. C.3: the `W_n` fail (6 b) and (6 c). F.2, F.13: `W_D` for `D(k)` and for module spectra (the classes `W_E`) satisfies (6 a, b, c). So passing from `W_D` to `W_D^lc` for a stable **D** gives up the reversible axiom; for `D(Z)` and spectra it reaches the fibration axiom instead. A GALOIS table that gives `W_D^lc` both (6 b) and (7 F), or (6 b) and `π_1`-detection, is wrong.
4. *Loc (4), (5), (8), (9).* Compare with C.2 and the table C.7 for `W_n` and `W_E` (Loc (5) holds for `W_E` iff `E ≠ 0`).
5. *Which Theorem A.* POLES A.1: literal Loc (3) of XVI is the absolute form, Loc (3 bis) the relative one. The common block of the prompt says "Loc (1)–(3) … the relative Theorem A axiom". Check which form GALOIS proves for `W_D^lc`; A.16 leaves open whether the absolute package implies the relative one.
